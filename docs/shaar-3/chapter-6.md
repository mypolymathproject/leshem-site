***Shaar* 3 Chapter 6**

ספר לשם שבו ואחלמה - הקדמות ושערים - שער ג פרק ו

אָמְנָם הֲרֵי עֲדַיִן הַדָּבָר צָרִיךְ בֵּאוּר כִּי הֲרֵי אָמַרְנוּ לְעֵיל ספ " ב ופ " ד

**However, this matter still requires some explanation, because as we said above (end of chapter 2 and chapter 4)**

שֶׁכָּל הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית עַצְמָהּ הִנֵּה הִיא לְעוֹלָם רַק אוֹר אֱלָהוּת

**– the entire macro-level neshama itself is always only Godly light,**

וְאֵין לָהּ כְּלִי כְּלָל בְּהַגּוּף לְהִתְלַבֵּשׁ בּוֹ

**and it does not have any ‘receptacle’ in the body at all in which to be clothed,**

כִּי אֵין לָהּ הִשְׁתַּוּוּת עִם שׁוּם גּוּף וּכְלִי

**since it the neshama has no equivalence or affinity to a body or vessel of any sort.**

Since the neshama is nothing less than a lofty spiritual existence, while the body is a lowly material entity, there is no affinity at all between them. How then can they interface with each other?

וְעִקַּר הַמֹּחַ אֲשֶׁר הוּא כְּלִי לְהַנְשָׁמָה הִנֵּה הוּא רַק לְהַנְשָׁמָה שֶׁבָּרוּחַ

**And that primary aspect of the brain that is a receptacle to the neshama is only as regards neshama of ruach , but not neshama itself.**

וא " כ הֲרֵי יִהְיֶה נִמְצָא לפי " ז שֶׁמִּכָּל הנר " נ ח " י הַכְּלָלִיִּים

**If this is so, it would therefore turn out that of all the aspects of macro level nr”n ch”y 1**

אֲשֶׁר יֵשׁ לָהֶם הִשְׁתַּוּוּת עִם הַגּוּף הֵם רַק שְׁנַיִם שֶׁהוּא הָרוּחַ הַכְּלָלִי וְהַנֶּפֶשׁ הַכְּלָלִי

**that could have any affinity or potential interface with a body, there are only two: macro- ruach and macro- nefesh .**

כִּי הֵם אוֹרוֹת נִבְרָאִים כְּמוֹ הַגּוּף

**This is because they are created 2 light 3 , similar to a body which is also created.**

**Even though ruach and nefesh are spiritual existences, they are nevertheless created spiritual existences. Since they are created , they have some affinity with the physical body.**

וְהֵם אֲשֶׁר מִשְׁתַּוִּים עַמּוֹ לְהִתְלַבֵּשׁ בּוֹ

**These – macro- ruach and macro- nefesh – are the ones the only levels of soul that have some affinity with the body , and therefore can interface with the physical body to become clothed in it.**

אֲבָל מֵהַנְּשָׁמָה וּלְמַעְלָה וְהִיא בִּכְלָל הוּא רַק אוֹר אֱלֹוהַּ הַמֵּאִיר בֶּהָרוּחַ וְהַנֶּפֶשׁ

**But from the macro-level of neshama and up , including the level of neshama itself , there is only the Godly light illuminating ruach and nefesh,**

לְהַשְׁפִּיעַ בָּהֶם שֶׁפַע הַחַיּוּת לְהַחֲיוֹת אֶת הַגּוּף

**that flows into them in a stream of life-force that enlivens the body.**

אֲבָל הִיא בְּעַצְמָהּ אֵין לָהּ הִשְׁתַּוּוּת עִם שׁוּם גּוּף וּכְלִי לְהִתְלַבֵּשׁ בּוֹ כְּלָל

**But the macro-level of neshama itself does not have any affinity at all with any type of body or vessel keli ( kelim in the plural) in which it could be clothed.**

וא " כ הֲרֵי צָרִיךְ בֵּאוּר לפי " ז כִּי לָמָּה נִסְדְּרוּ ג ' כֵּלִים בְּהַגּוּף שֶׁהֵם מֹחַ לֵב כָּבֵד

**Accordingly, we need an explanation as to why there are three receptacles set up in the body – the brain, the heart and the liver,**

וּפְנִימִי תִּיכוֹן חִיצוֹן שֶׁהֵם כֵּלִים להנר " נ שֶׁבְּכָל פַּרְצוּף

**arrayed as inner, intermediate and outer layers – which are the kelim for the neshama, ruach and nefesh in every partzuf .**

וְכֵן בִּכְלָלוּת הָעוֹלָמוֹת הַנִּבְרָאִים שֶׁהֵם ג " כ ג ' עוֹלָמוֹת בְּרִיאָה יְצִירָה עֲשִׁיָה

**And so too as regards the created Worlds in general – the three Worlds of Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah, but not Atzilut, which is emanated, not created,**

וְאָמַרְנוּ כִּי הֵם להנר " נ הַכְּלָלִיִּים

**and as we stated earlier, they the three Worlds of Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah are also kelim- receptacles for the macro-levels of neshama, ruach and nefesh respectively.**

הֲרֵי מְאַחַר שֶׁהָאוֹרוֹת הַנִּבְרָאִים אֲשֶׁר יֵשׁ לָהֶם הִשְׁתַּוּוּת עִם הַגּוּף

**Hence, since only created lights have some affinity with a body,**

הֵם רַק שְׁנַיִם

**of which there are only two macro-level ruach and macro-level nefesh,**

א " כ הֲרֵי צָרִיךְ שֶׁגַּם כְּלָלוּת הָעוֹלָמוֹת וְכֵן פְּרָטוּת הַכֵּלִים שֶׁבְּכָל פַּרְצוּף

**accordingly, the macro-level Worlds, and also the micro-level kelim in each partzuf ,**

לֹא יִהְיוּ ג " כ רַק שְׁנַיִם וּכְמוֹ מְצִיאוּת הָאוֹרוֹת הַנִּבְרָאִים שֶׁהֵם רַק ב '

**should also be no more than two, just like the actual created lights, which are only two,**

כִּי הַכֵּלִים צָרִיךְ שֶׁיִּהְיוּ רַק כְּמִסְפַּר הָאוֹרוֹת הַמִּשְׁתַּוִּים עִמָּהֵם וּמִתְלַבְּשִׁים בָּהֶם .

**since the number of kelim should correspond to the number of lights that interface with them and become clothed within them.**

**And yet we see that there are three immanent worlds, Beriah , Yetzirah and Asiyah, and three receptacles in the body, the brain, the heart and the liver.!**

וּמָה שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ כִּי הג ' כֵּלִים שֶׁבְּכָל גּוּף הֵם רַק לִפְרָטֵי הנר " נ אֲשֶׁר בְּהָרוּחַ וְהַנֶּפֶשׁ עַצְמָן

**Now what we said regarding the three kelim in each body – the brain, the heart and the liver – this refers only to the kelim for micro-level nr”n of ruach and of nefesh themselves.**

And therefore we can perhaps accept this as an explanation as to why there are three kelim in the body.

הֲלֹא גַּם זֶה גּוּפָהּ צָרִיךְ בֵּאוּר כִּי לָמָּה נִפְרְטוּ לְג '

**But this too requires explanation – why are there three subdivisions**

מֵאַחַר שֶׁכְּלָלֵי אוֹרוֹת הַנִּבְרָאִים הֵם רַק שְׁנַיִם

**when the macro-level created lights are only two, as mentioned above?**

הֲרֵי הַכְּלָל וְהַפְּרָט הוּא לְעוֹלָם בְּסֵדֶר אֶחֲד .

**Particularly since we have a rule that macro and micro levels always follow the same pattern.**

וְעוֹד כִּי מְאַחַר שֶׁכְּלָלוּת הָעוֹלָמוֹת הַנִּבְרָאִים הֵם ג ' שֶׁהֵם בְּרִיאָה יְצִירָה עֲשִׁיָה

**Furthermore, since the macro-level created Worlds are three – Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah –**

הֲרֵי מַשְׁמָע מִזֶּה שֶׁגַּם כְּלָלֵי הָאוֹרוֹת הַנִּבְרָאִים הֵם בֶּאֱמֶת ג " כ ג ':

**we can deduce from this that the macro-level created lights are also in fact three.**

אַךְ הָעִנְיָן בְּזֶה כִּי הָאֱמֶת הוּא כְּמוֹ שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ שֶׁעִקַּרֵי הַנִּבְרָאִים הֵם רַק שְׁנַיִם

**However the concept here is that in fact, as we said, the primary aspects of created beings are only two ruach and nefesh.**

אַךְ מָה שֶׁהֵם ג ' עוֹלָמוֹת בְּרִיאָה יְצִירָה עֲשִׁיָה

**But the fact that there are three Worlds – Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah –**

וְכֵן נִסְדָּר ג ' כֵּלִים בְּכָל פַּרְצוּף שֶׁהֵם מֹחַ לֵב כָּבֵד וּפְנִימִי תִּיכוֹן חִיצוֹן וְכַנָּ " ל

**and that there are three kelim are arrayed in each partzuf – the brain, the heart and the liver –**

וּפְנִימִי תִּיכוֹן חִיצוֹן וְכַנָּ " ל

**arranged as inner, intermediate and outer layers, as explained above,**

הִנֵּה הוּא מִשּׁוּם כִּי בַּאֲשֶׁר שֶׁרְצוֹנוֹ ית " ש בְּכָל בְּרִיאַת עוֹלָמוֹ

**this is because it is His will, may He be blessed, in creating His entire world**

הִנֵּה הָיָה שֶׁיְּהֵא כָּל הַבְּרִיאָה כֻּלּוֹ תָּמִיד קָשׁוּר עַמּוֹ וְתָלוּי בּוֹ לְעוֹלָם

**that all of Creation should always be bound to Him and be dependent upon Him forever.**

וְלָכֵן סִדֵּר וְהִמְצִיא בְּכָל הַבְּרִיאָה כֻּלָּהּ הֵן בִּכְלָל וְהֵן בִּפְרָט

**For this reason, He planned on and produced throughout the entire creation, on both macro- and micro-levels**

בְּחִי ' מְצִיאַת מָקוֹם אַחַת שֶׁהוּא רָאוּי לְקַבֵּל בְּתוֹכָהּ אוֹרוֹ תָּמִיד

**the existence of one place that would be fitting to always receive His light within it,**

אֲשֶׁר שָׁם הוּא מִשְׁכַּן כְּבוֹדוֹ שֶׁהוּא אוֹר שְׁכִינָתוֹ יִתְבָּרֵךְ שְׁמוֹ .

**a place where His glory ( Kavod ), the light of the Divine Presence, the Shechinah , is manifested, may His name be blessed.**

וְהוּא בִּבְחִי ' בֵּית הַמִּקְדָּשׁ שֶׁל מַטֶּה שֶׁשּׁוֹרֶה שְׁכִינָתוֹ בְּבֵית קֹדֶשׁ הַקָּדָשִׁים

**And that place is the Holy Temple the Beit HaMikdash below in this world, 4 in which His Shechinah resides, in the Holy of Holies.**

כֵּן הוּא בְּכָל הַבְּרִיאָה כֻּלָּהּ בִּכְלָל וּבִפְרָט ,

**And so it is throughout all of the Creation, on the macro- and micro-levels,**

הִנֵּה נִתְיַסֵּד בְּכֻלָּם בְּחִי ' מָקוֹם אֶחֲד אֲשֶׁר עַל יָדוֹ מִתְיַחֵד עִם הַמְּצִיאוּת

**in all of them is established one place through which He unites with existence as it is in that World.**

כִּי שָׁם מִתְפַּשֵּׁט וְשׁוֹרֶה אוֹרוֹ ית " ש לְמַטָּה

**Because it is there, down below, that His light or illumination spreads forth and is manifested ,**

וְשָׁם קָשׁוּר וְתָלוּי בּוֹ הַמְּצִיאוּת כֻּלּוֹ לְעוֹלָם

**and it is to that place that the entire existence is always bound and on which it depends.**

וְזֶהוּ סוֹד מָה שֶׁאָמְרוּ רז " ל בַּמִּדְבָּר רַבָּה פ ' נָשָׂא פָּרָשָׁה י " ב סִימָן ו '

**This is the secret of what our Sages declare in Bamidbar Rabba, Naso 12:6 –**

מִתְּחִלַּת בְּרִיָּתוֹ שֶׁל עוֹלָם שָׁרְתָה הַשְּׁכִינָה בַּתַּחְתּוֹנִים

**“From the beginning of the creation of the world, the Shechinah was manifested in the lowest plane.”**

וְהִנֵּה הוּא ע " פ מָה שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ לְעֵיל פ ' ג ' ד '

**Now this concurs with what we said above in chapters 3 and 4,**

שֶׁגַּם בְּאוֹר הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַכְּלָלִי שֶׁהוּא עוֹלַם הָעֲשִׂיָּה הַזֶּה

**that even the light of macro-level nefesh , which is this World of Asiyah ,**

הַיְּחִידָה שֶׁבָּהּ אוֹר אֱלָהוּת .

**the yechida therein is Divine light , not a mere radiance generated from a secondary source.**

וַהֲרֵי שֶׁיֵּשׁ מָקּוֹם גַּם בְּעוֹלַם הָעֲשִׂיָּה לִשְׁכֹּן כָּבוֹד בְּתוֹכָהּ

**Thus, even in the World of Asiyah , there is a place for the Kavod to be manifested,**

לְהִתְפַּשֵּׁט אוֹרוֹ שָׁם וּלְהִתְיַחֵד עַמָּהּ

**and in which His light can spread out and unify with.**

וְאָמַרְנוּ לְעֵיל פ " ד כִּי ע " י הַיְּחִידָה דְּאֹור הַנֶּפֶשׁ

**Now we stated above, chapter 4 , that via the yechida -light of nefesh**

הִנֵּה מִתְפַּשֵּׁט וּמִתְיַחֵד שָׁם אוֹר הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִי שֶׁהִיא כֻּלָּהּ אוֹר אֱלָהוּת

**the light of macro- neshama , which is entirely the aspect of Godliness, spreads forth and becomes one there with nefesh of Asiyah .**

הַשּׁוֹכֵן בְּהֵיכָל קֹדֶשׁ הַקָּדָשִׁים דְּהַבְּרִיאָה וּמִתְפַּשֶּׁטֶת בְּהֶאָרָתָהּ מִשָּׁם וּלְמַטָּה

**Now the light of macro- neshama is manifested in the Holy of Holies of Beriah , and spreads forth its radiance from there on down into the lower worlds,**

עֲד עוֹלַם הָעֲשִׂיָּה וּמִתְיַחֶדֶת בְּאוֹר הַיְּחִידָה שֶׁבְּהַעֲשִׂיָה

**until it reaches the World of Asiyah and becomes one with the light of yechida of Asiyah , as we just explained.**

וְהִיא הַשְּׁכִינָה הַשּׁוֹכֵן בְּבֵית קֹדֶשׁ הַקָּדָשִׁים שֶׁל מַטֶּה

**And this is what is referred to as the Shechinah , which is manifested in the Holy of Holies down below**

שֶׁהוּא מְכֻוַּן נֶגֶד הֵיכַל קֹדֶשׁ הַקָּדָשִׁים שֶׁבְּעוֹלָם הַבְּרִיאָה

**and is situated precisely in line with the Chamber of the Holy of Holies of Beriah ,**

שֶׁהוּא כִּסֵּא הַכָּבוֹד וּכְמוֹ שֶׁכָּתַב הָרַב בְּשַׁעַר כִּסֵּא הַכָּבוֹד וּבִדְרוּשֵׁי מ " ן ומ " ד דָּרוּשׁ א ' סָמוּךְ לְסוֹפוֹ \ also known as the Throne of Glory Kisei HaKavod , as the Rabbi – Rabbi Chaim Vital in the name of Rabbi Isaac Luria, the Arizal – wrote in Etz Chaim , Shaar Kisei HaKavod , and in Drushei Ma”n u’Ma”d, drush 1 near the end.

וְכֵן הוּא בַּמִּדְרָשׁ תַּנְחוּמָא פ ' וְיִקְהַל סִימַן ו '

**This is stated similarly in Midrash Tanchuma, Vayakhel section 6,**

שֶׁהָאָרוֹן הָיָה מְכֻוָּן כְּנֶגֶד כִּסֵּא הַכָּבוֹד שֶׁל מַעְלָה

**that the Ark is situated directly in line with the Throne of Glory above.**

וְשָׁם בְּפ ' פְּקוּדֵי סִימָן ב ' שֶׁהַהֵיכָל שֶׁלְּמַטָה מְכֻוָּן כְּנֶגֶד הֵיכָל שֶׁל מַעֲלָה .

**And in Midrash Tanchuma, Pekudei section 2, it states that the Heichal the section of the Temple comprising the Holy of Holies, the Holy and the Hall in front of them 5 below is situated directly in line with the Heichal above.**

וַהֲרֵי לָנוּ עכ " פ כִּי הַג ' עוֹלָמוֹת בְּרִיאָה יְצִירָה וְעֲשִׁיָה

**At the very least then, we have it that the Worlds of Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah**

הִנֵּה הֲגַם שֶׁהֵם עַצְמָן אֵינָם אֱלָהוּת כְּלָל וְהֵם עוֹלָמוֹת נִפְרָרִים

**– even though they themselves are not Godly at all, but rather are separated Worlds –**

עכ " ז הִנֵּה אוֹרוֹ ית " ש הוּא שׁוֹכֵן בָּהֶם תָּמִיד וּמִתְיַחֵד עִמָּהֶם לְעוֹלָם

**even so, His light, may His Name be blessed, always manifests within them and is one with them forever.**

וְהִנֵּה הוּא שׁוֹכֵן גַּם בְּעֲשִׁיָה ע " י הַיְּחִידָה שֶׁבָּהּ הַמְּתַפְּשֵׁט בְּהֲחַיָה שֶׁבָּהּ

**Thus the light manifests even in Asiyah , via its yechida which seeps into chaya of Asiyah ,**

וְהֲחַיָה בַּהַנְשָׁמָה שֶׁבָּהּ ( שֶׁהוּא הַנְּשָׁמָה דְּהָרוּחַ דְּהָעֲשִׁיָה וְכַנָּ " ל פ " ד ע " ש )

**and chaya in turn into neshama of Asiyah (this is the neshama of ruach of Asiyah , as mentioned above chapter 4. See there).**

וְלָכֵן נִתְיַסֵּד מָקוֹם מְיֻחָד עַל זֶה בְּכָל פַּרְצוּף בִּפְרָט

**And therefore a special place is established in each individual partzuf**

וְהוּא הַמְּקוֹם הג ' הָעֶלְיוֹן שֶׁהוּא הַמֹּחַ

**– which is the third and uppermost area of the three – brain, heart, liver – i.e. the brain.**

וְכֵן הוּא בְּהָעוֹלָמוֹת בִּכְלָל וְהוּא עוֹלָם הָג ' הָעֶלְיוֹנָה שֶׁהוּא עוֹלַם הַבְּרִיאָה .

**And so it is also in the Worlds in general i.e. in the macro-sense – the third and highest World, the uppermost of the three Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah i.e. the World of Beriah, is where a place is set aside.**

כִּי שָׁם הוּא הַמָּקוֹם אֲשֶׁר אוֹרוֹ ית " ש מִתְיַחֵד שָׁם עי " ז בְּהַמְּצִיאוּת כֻּלּוֹ בִּכְלָל וּבִּפְרָט

**Because that is the place with which His blessed light is unified, and through it, the entirety of existence becomes part of this unity, on both macro- and micro-levels.**

אֲבָל עִקַּר הַמְּצִיאוּת דְּהָאוֹרוֹת הַנִּבְרָאִים עַצְמָן הֵם בֶּאֱמֶת רַק שָׁנִים וְהֵם רוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ .

**Nevertheless, in reality, the primary created 6 lights themselves are actually only two , not three – ruach and nefesh .**

אֶלָּא מִשּׁוּם שֶׁאוֹרוֹ ית " ש מִתְיַחֵד עִמָּהֶם ג " כ לְעוֹלָם

**However, because His light is always one with them as well,**

לְכָךְ נִתְיַסֵּד עַל זֶה מָקוֹם מְיֻחָד לִשְׁכֹּן כָּבוֹד בְּתוֹכָהּ

**therefore a special place was established in which the Kavod can manifest,**

וְהוּא כְּעֵין מָקוֹם מִקְדָּשׁ וּמִשְׁכָּן אֲשֶׁר שָׁם שׁוֹרֶה הַשְּׁכִינָה לַמַּטֶּה

**which is akin to the place in which the Shechinah manifests below in the Mikdash the Temple and the Mishkan the Tabernacle.**

כֵּן הוּא בְהָעוֹלָמוֹת בִּכְלַל שֶׁהוּא בְּהֵיכָל קֹדֶשׁ הַקָּדָשִׁים דְּעוֹלַם הַבְּרִיאָה

**And so too in the macro-Worlds – in the Heichal of Beriah , in the Holy of Holies of Beriah,**

אֲשֶׁר שָׁם הוּא הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִי שֶׁהוּא כֻּלּוֹ אֱלָהוּת

**wherein is the macro- neshama, which is all Godliness,**

וְשׁוֹכֵן בהג ' רִאשׁוֹנִים דְּהַבְּרִיאָה שֶׁהוּא כִּסֵּא הַכָּבוֹד

**is manifested – in the first three sefirot of Beriah chochmah, binah and da’at [often abbreviated as chaba”d ] and is called the Throne of Glory Kisei HaKavod ,**

וְנִקְרָא כָּל עוֹלַם הַבְּרִיאָה עַל שֶׁמָּה שֶׁהוּא בְּשֵׁם נְשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִי

**so that the entire World of Beriah is named after it the World of the Throne because of the macro- neshama that is manifested there. 7**

וְכֵן הוּא בְּכָל פַּרְצוּף בִּפְרָט שֶׁהוּא הַמֹּחַ שֶׁבְּכָל פַּרְצוּף

**And so too on the micro-level of partzuf , which is the ‘brain’ of each partzuf ,**

אֲשֶׁר שָׁם הוּא הג " ר דְּהָרוּחַ שֶׁבּוֹ

**this is where the first three sefirot, chochmah, binah and da’at ( chaba”d ), of ruach are situated,**

וּמִתְפַּשֵּׁט וּמִתְיַחֵד שָׁם אוֹר הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִי ג " כ כַּנָּ " ל פ " ד

**and where the light of macro- neshama also permeates and unites with it, as mentioned previously, chapter 4.**

וְנִמְצָא כִּי הֲגַם שֶׁהָאֱמֶת הוּא שֶׁאֵין לְאוֹר הַאֲלָהוּת שׁוּם הִשְׁתַּוּוּת כְּלָל

**Thus we find that even though actually for the Divine light there is no affinity**

עִם כָּל בְּחִי ' גּוּף וּכְלִי לְהִתְלַבֵּשׁ בּוֹ ,

**to any physical entity or vessel in which to be clothed,**

עכ " ז הִנֵּה צִמְצֵם אוֹר שְׁכִינָתוֹ ית " ש וְשׁוֹכֵן בָּהֶם עכ " פ , וע " ד שֶׁאָמְרוּ בְּסִפְרָא וְיִקְרָא פָּרָשָׁה ב '

**nevertheless, He constricted the light of His Shechinah so that it can anyway manifest within them, 8 as the Sages stated in Sifra, Vayikra 2:**

דְּמִשּׁוּם חִבָּתָן שֶׁל יִשְׂרָאֵל דָּחַק לִהְיוֹת מְדַבֵּר מֵעַל הַכַּפֹּרֶת מִבֵּין שְׁנֵי הַכְּרֻבִים .

**‘Because of His love for Israel, He forced Himself to speak from on top of the lid of the Ark, between the two cherubs.’ 9**

וּבִבְרֵאשִׁית רַבָּה פ ' דּ ' סִימָן דּ ' כְּשֶׁהוּא רוֹצֶה הֲלֹא אֶת הַשָּׁמַיִם וְאֶת הָאָרֶץ אֲנִי מָלֵא

**And in Midrash Rabba, Bereishit 4:4, when this is what He wants, the verse states: “Do I not fill the heavens and the earth”? (Jeremiah 23:24)**

וּכְשֶׁהוּא רוֹצֶה הָיָה מְדַבֵּר עִם מֹשֶׁה מִבֵּין שְׁנֵי בָּדֵי הָאָרוֹן ( וע ' בִּגְמָרָא שַׁבַּת דַּף פ " ח ע " ב ובשהש " ר פֵּ " א פָּסוּק י " ד סִימָן ג ')

**And, when He wished to, He would speak with Moses from between the two poles of the Ark (see Talmud, Shabbat 88b; Shir Hashirim Rabba chapter 1, 14:3).**

כֵּן הוּא עד " ז בְּכָל הַמְּצִיאוּת בִּכְלָל וּבִפְרָט

**And so it is throughout all of existence, on macro- and micro-levels –**

הִנֵּה נִתְיַסֵּד בָּהֶם מָקוֹם אֶחָד לְאוֹר שְׁכִינָתוֹ ית " ש כְּעֵין מִקְדָּשׁ וּמִשְׁכַּן

**a place has been established for the light of His blessed Shechinah , just as in the Mikdash the Temple and the Mishkan the Tabernacle.**

וְהוּא הַמֹּחַ שֶׁל כָּל פַּרְצוּף וְהַהֵיכָל קֹדֶשׁ הַקָּדָשִׁים שֶׁל עוֹלָם הַבְּרִיאָה \ This is the ‘brain’ of every partzuf and the Chamber of the Holy of Holies of the World of Beriah,

וּלְכָךְ נִקְרָא כָּל הַבְּרִיאָה כֻּלָּהּ עַל שֵׁם הָאוֹר דְּהַנְשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִי

**and therefore the entire World of Beriah is called by the name of macro-level neshama –**

As noted in the previous chapter, macro-level nefesh corresponds to the World of Asiyah , ruach to Yetzirah , neshama to Beriah, and chaya to Atzilut.

וְכֵן נִקְרָא הַמֹּחַ שֶׁל כָּל פַּרְצוּף בְּשֵׁם הַכְּלִי לַהַנְשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִי שֶׁבּוֹ כַּנָּ " ל פֵּ " ה

**In addition, the ‘brain’ i.e. the chaba”d of each partzuf is called the receptacle or vessel for the macro-level neshama within it, as explained in chapter 5,**

מִשּׁוּם שֶׁדֶּרֶךְ שָׁם מִתְיַחֵד אוֹר הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִי ג " כ בְּהָעוֹלָמוֹת בִּכְלַל

**because by way thereof – by way of the chaba”d of each partzuf – the light of the macro-level neshama becomes one with the Worlds in general.**

וְכֵן בְּכָל פַּרְצוּף בִּפְרָט וּכְנִז ' וְדַי בַּזֶה :

**This is also the case with each individual partzuf , as mentioned. We have discussed this sufficiently.**

וְכֵן הִנֵּה יֵשׁ בַּזֶה עִנְיָן עוֹד קָרוֹב אֶל הָאָמוּר

**There is another matter here which is close in subject to what we said above:**

כִּי הָעוֹלָמוֹת בְּרִיאָה יְצִירָה עָשִׁיָה מָה שֶׁהֵם נֶחֱשָׁבִים לג '

**The fact that the Worlds of Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah are regarded as three separate worlds**

הִנֵּה הוּא רַק בְּמִקְרֶה וְלֹא בְּעֶצֶם כִּי בְּעֶצֶם הֵם רַק שָׁנִים

**is only incidentally so, not inherently so, because in essence they are only two.**

וְהוּא כִּי הֲרֵי אָמַרְנוּ לְעֵיל פֵּ " ה כִּי הָרוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ דְּעוֹלָם הַבְּרִיאָה

**The explanation of this is as we said above, in chapter 5, that ruach and nefesh of the World of Beriah**

הִנֵּה הֵם הַשֹּׁרֶשׁ דְּהָרוּחַ וְהַנֶּפֶשׁ הַכְּלָלִיִּים שֶׁהֵם עוֹלַם הַיְּצִירָה וְהָעֲשִׂיָה

**are the root or origin of macro ruach and nefesh i.e. the Worlds of Yetzirah and Asiyah.**

וְהִנֵּה אוֹתוֹ הַשֹּׁרֶשׁ אִלּוּלֵא הַחֵטְא

**Now that root – the micro-level ruach and nefesh of Beriah – had it not been for the sin of Adam and Eve partaking of the fruit of the Tree of Knowledge**

הָיָה מִתְיַחֵד עִם הַמְּצִיאוּת הָעִקָּרִי שֶׁיָּצָא מִמֶּנּוּ לְאֶחָד מַמָּשׁ

**– would have actually been unified into one with the main aspects of existence which emerged from them.**

כִּי הָרוּחַ הַפְּרָטִי שֶׁבַּהַנְשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִי שֶׁהוּא הָרוּחַ דְּעוֹלָם הַבְּרִיאָה

**Because micro-level ruach of macro- neshama , which is the ruach of the World of Beriah,**

הָיָה מִתְיַחֵד עִם עוֹלַם הַיְּצִירָה שֶׁהוּא הָרוּחַ הַכְּלָלִי לְאֶחָד מַמָּשׁ

**would actually have become united into one with the World of Yetzirah, which is the macro-level of ruach ,**

וּכְכָל שֹׁרֶשׁ שֶׁמִּתְפַּשֵּׁט בְּהַעֲנָף וּמִתְיַחֵד עַמּוֹ וְהֵם אֶחָד מַמָּשׁ

**just like any root that extends into the branches that it nourishes, and they become one entity, literally.**

וְכֵן הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַפְּרָטִי שֶׁבַּהַנְשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִי , שֶׁהוּא הַנֶּפֶשׁ שֶׁבַּבְּרִיאָה ,

**Similarly, micro-level nefesh of macro-level neshama , which is the nefesh of Beriah,**

הָיָה מִתְיַחֵד עִם עוֹלַם הָעֲשִׂיָּה , שֶׁהוּא הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַכְּלָלִי שֶׁיָּצָא מִמֶּנוֹ , לְאֶחָד מַמָּשׁ

**would have become united with the World of Asiyah, macro-level nefesh which emerged from it from nefesh of Beriah , and become one,**

וּכְכָל שֹׁרֶשׁ עִם הֶעֲנָף וְהָיָה מְקַבֵּל הָעֲשִׂיָּה מֵהַבְּרִיאָה מְכֻוָּן

**just like any root that extends into the branches that it nourishes, and Asiyah would have received from Beriah directly,**

שֶׁלֹּא דֶּרֶךְ הַיְּצִירָה כְּלָל ( ע ' זוה " ק פ ' נָח ע " ה ע " ב ובמ " מ שָׁם ).

**not via Yetzirah at all (see Zohar I, Noach 5b , and in Mikdash Melech ’s commentary there).**

וַהֲרֵי הָיָה נֶחֱשָׁב הַבְּרִיאָה עִם הַיְּצִירָה לְאֶחַד וְכֵן עִם הָעֲשִׂיָּה לְאַחַת .

**Thus Beriah would have been regarded as one with Yetzirah, and similarly Beriah would have been regarded as one with Asiyah.**

וכ " ז [ וְכָל זֶה ] הוּא כִּי הִנֵּה הָרוּחַ וְהַנֶּפֶשׁ הַפְּרָטִיִּים דְּעוֹלָם הַבְּרִיאָה

**And all of this would have been possible because micro-level ruach and nefesh of Beriah**

הִנֵּה הֵם ג " כ רַק נִבְרָאִים וְנִבְרְאוּ מֵהַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית אֲשֶׁר שָׁם

**are also created, and were created from the macro-level neshama there in Beriah**

שֶׁהִיא אֲשֶׁר הִמְצִיא אוֹתָם אַחַר אֲשֶׁר יָרְדָה בְּהַבְרִיאָהּ וְנִתְגַּלָּה בָּהֶם

**which produced them after descending into Beriah and becoming revealed in them in micro-level ruach and nefesh of Beriah.**

The World of Beriah is characterized specifically by macro-level neshama, as explained above in chapter 5. Now when macro-level neshama becomes subdivided into its micro-level components, specifically ruach and nefesh of Beriah, that macro-level neshama is regarded as producing its micro-levels, and at the same time it illuminates them – since they are, after all, micro-levels of Beriah .

וְהֵם הַשֹּׁרֶשׁ בְּהַיְּצִירָה וְהָעֲשִׂיָּה וְהֵם אֶחָד עִמָּהֶם

**And they the micro-level ruach and nefesh of Beriah become the root of Yetzirah and Asiyah, as we explained in chapter 5, and are united with them.**

כִּי שֹׁרֶשׁ הָרוּחַ עִם הָרוּחַ הֵם אֶחָד בְּעֶצֶם

**This is because the root of ruach as it is above in Beriah, and ruach below in Yetzirah and Asiyah, are one essentially. They are both of the nature of ruach.**

וְכֵן שֹׁרֶשׁ הַנֶּפֶשׁ עִם הַנֶּפֶשׁ הֵם אַחַת בְּעֶצֶם וּכְנִז '

**And so too the root of nefesh as it is above in Beriah is one in essence with nefesh below in Yetzirah and Asiyah. They are both of the nature of nefesh.**

וַהֲרֵי נִמְצָא כִּי הָג ' עוֹלָמוֹת בְּרִיאָה יְצִירָה עֲשִׂיָּה אֵינָם נֶחֱשָׁבִים בֶּאֱמֶת לג ' כְּלָל

**Thus we have it that the three Worlds of Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah are not really regarded as three at all,**

כִּי הֵם רַק שָׁנִים בְּעֶצֶם וַחֲלֻקָּתָם לג ' הוּא רַק בְּמִקְרֶה

**but rather as two in essence, while their division into three is merely incidental –**

כִּי ע " י הַחֵטְא יָרְדוּ מִמַּדְרֵגָתָם וְאֵינוֹ מִתְיַחֵד הַשֹּׁרֶשׁ עִמָּהֶם

**as a result of the sin of the Tree of Knowledge etc. they descended from their level, and their root is not unified with them , even though they are dependent upon their root and receive their life-force from it. 10**

וְנִתְחַלְּקוּ זֶה מִזֶּה וְנַעֲשׂוּ לג '

**Thus they became separated from one another and became three since Yetzirah and Asiyah became detached from Beriah through the sin.**

אֲבָל בְּעֶצֶם הֵם רַק שָׁנִים וְהַשְּׁלִישִׁי הָעִקָּרִי הוּא לְעוֹלָם רַק אֱלָהוּת

**But in essence they are really only two created worlds – Yetzirah and Asiyah – while the third and main world, Beriah, is always only Godliness.**

וְהוּא הקב " ה לְבַד שֶׁהוּא הִמְצִיאָם וּמְקַיְּמָם וּמְיַחֲדָם

**Nevertheless, only the Holy One, blessed is He, produced them, sustains them and unites them.**

וְכֵן מַנְהִיג אֹתָם תָּמִיד בְּתוֹצָאוֹת כָּל פְּעֻלּוֹתֵיהֶם שֶׁבָּעוֹלָם הַזֶּה .

**And only He directs them always, bringing forth all of their effects in this world,**

וּמִתְנַהֵג סֵדֶר כָּל הַבְּרִיאָה כֻּלָּהּ ע " ד שֶׁאָמְרוּ בְּנִדָה ל " א

**and the entire order of Creation behaves as the Talmudic Sages stated in Tractate Niddah 31 a :**

ג ' שֻׁתָּפִין בְּאָדָם הקב " ה וְאָבִיו וְאִמּוֹ

**“There are three partners in the creation of a person – the Holy One, blessed is He, his father and his mother.” \ The Talmud ( Niddah 31a) explains that certain physical aspects of a fetus come from the father, others from the mother, while the Holy One, blessed is He, gives the soul. 11**

כֵּן הוּא עד " ז סֵדֶר הַבְּרִיאָה כֻּלָּהּ שֶׁעִקָּרָן הֵם שָׁנִים

**So is it in the order of the entire creation – there are primarily two partners: the father and mother,**

וְהַשְּׁלִישִׁי הוּא הקב " ה אֲשֶׁר הִמְצִיאָם וּמַנְהִיגָם

**and the third partner is the Holy One, blessed is He, who produced them and Who directs or supervises them.**

וְהוּא ע " פ מָה שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ כִּי עִקָּרָן שֶׁל כָּל הַנִּבְרָאִים הֵם רַק שָׁנִים שֶׁהֵם רוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ

**This is according to what we stated above – that there are only two primary aspects of all created beings : ruach and nefesh.**

וְהוּא מ " ש רז " ל בִּדְבָרִים רַבָּה פ ' וָאֶתְחַנַּן סִימָן ל " א

**This was stated by our Sages in Midrash Devarim Rabba, V’etchanan 31:**

אָמַר הקב " ה לְיִשְׂרָאֵל בָּנַי כָּל מָּה שֶׁבָּרָאתִי בָּרָאתִי זוּגוֹת

**“Said the Holy Blessed One to Israel i.e. the Jewish People, ‘My children, everything I created, I created in pairs**

שָׁמִים וְאֶרֶץ זוּגוֹת , חַמָּה וּלְבָנָה זוּגוֹת , אָדָם וְחַוָּה זוּגוֹת , הָעוֹלָם הַזֶּה וְהָעוֹלָם הַבָּא זוּגוֹת

**Heavens and earth – a pair; sun and moon – a pair; Adam and Eve – a pair; this world and the World-to-Come – a pair**

אֲבָל כְּבוֹדִי אֶחֲד וּמְיֻחָד כוּ '

**– but My Glory is one and unique .**

וְכָל אֵלּוּ הַזּוּגוֹת שֶׁאָמַר הֵם כֻּלָּם בְּסוֹד רוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ כְּנוֹדָע

**Now all of the pairs mentioned are all the mystical secret of ruach and nefesh , as is known. 12**

אֶלָּא שֶׁאוֹתָן הָרוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ הִנֵּה הֵם כ " א בִּבְחִי ' שֹׁרֶשׁ וּמְצִיאוּת

**However, ruach and nefesh in those contexts are the aspect of root-source and existential being respectively.**

וְהַשֹּׁרֶשׁ הוּא תְּכוּנָה מְעֻלָּה וְזַךְ גָּבוֹהַּ מְאֹד הָרְאוּיָה לְקַבֵּל בְּתוֹכָהּ אֶת אוֹרוֹ יִתְבָּרֵךְ

**The root-source is a superior quality, pure and very lofty and is worthy of receiving His blessed light within it,**

לִהְיוֹת מִשְׁכָּן וּמָעוֹן אֵלָיו

**serving as an abode and domicile for it for His light.**

וְהִיא בֵּית קָדֶשׁ הַקָּדָשִׁים שֶׁשָּׁם שׁוֹרָה שְׁכִינָתוֹ יִתְבָּרֵךְ

**And in the Tabernacle and the Temple this is the Holy of Holies in which His blessed Shechinah dwells.**

וּמַמְשִׁיךְ שִׁפְעוֹ מִמֶנָּה בְּתוֹצָאוֹת וּפְעֻלַּת הַמְּצִיאוּת כֻּלּוֹ וְכָל הַנְהָגָתָהּ

**And from there He draws down His life-force in bringing forth and implementing all of existence and all Divine supervision of the worlds.**

וְהוּא הוּא עוֹלָם הַבְּרִיאָה שֶׁנִּקְרָא כִּסֵּא הַכָּבוֹד

**Now all of this is the World of Beriah, also called the Throne of Glory,**

כִּי עָלֶיהָ שׁוֹכֵן מֶלֶךְ מַלְכֵי הַמְּלָכִים הקב " ה

**so called because upon it the King of kings, the Holy One, blessed is He, rests**

לְהַמְצִיא וּלְהַנְהִיג אֶת כָּל הַמְּצִיאוּת כֻּלָּהּ

**in order to bring forth and direct all of existence.**

אֲבָל הַנִּבְרָאִים עַצְמָן הֵם רַק שְׁנַיִם שֶׁהֵם רוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ וְהֵם יְסוֹד כָּל הַנִּבְרָאִים כֻּלָּם

**However, created beings themselves are only two – ruach and nefesh – which are the foundation of all created beings.**

וְזֶהוּ מָה שֶׁמַּתְחִיל הַתּוֹרָה שֶׁהוּא הַתְחָלַת מַעֲשֵׂה בְּרֵאשִׁית בָּאוֹת ב '.

And this is why the Torah commences with the account of Creation, beginning with the letter bet – the בּ of בְּרֵאשִׁית .

מִשּׁוּם שֶׁהַנִּבְרָאִים הֵם שְׁנִיִּים

**This is because created beings are only second – the letter bet is the second letter of the Hebrew alphabet and also has the numerical value of 2, both alluding to the second thing, the first being Torah, as the Baal HaLeshem goes on to explain**

אֲבָל הַתּוֹרָה עַצְמָהּ פָּתַח בְּא ' שֶׁהוּא א ' דְּאֲנֹכִי

**whereas the Torah itself begins with an aleph, the aleph of Anochi .**

The first word of the Ten Commandments, which encapsulates the entire Torah, 13 is אֲנָכִי . Thus the Torah itself, in the form of the two tablets of stone, begins with an aleph , which has the numerical value of 1, alluding to the fact that the worlds were created for the sake of Torah. 14

כִּי הוּא עַל הקב " ה הַמַּמְצִיא אֶת הַכֹּל וְהוּא אֶחָד

**He, the Holy One, blessed be He, brought forth everything, and yet He is One.**

וְכֵן הוּא מְיַחֵד אֶת הַב ' יְסוֹדוֹת הַנִּבְרָאִים שֶׁהֵם רוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ

**And He unites these two foundational aspects of created beings – ruach and nefesh –**

ע " י שֶׁמַּשְׁפִּיעַ אוֹרוֹ בָּהֶם לְצֹרֶךְ תּוֹלְדוֹת כָּל הַמְּצִיאוּת כֻּלָּם

**by the outflow of His light into them for the purpose of generating all of existence,**

הַנַּעֲשֶׂה מֵהָרוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ הַכְּלָלִים

**which is produced from macro-level ruach and nefesh.**

וְאוֹרוֹ הַמִּתְפַּשֵּׁט בָּהֶם הוּא הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית וְהוּא שֻׁתַּף הַשְּׁלִישִׁי

**And the light that spreads into them into macro ruach and nefesh is the light of macro-level neshama , which is the ‘third partner,’**

**The third partner as mentioned above, where the two primary partners in producing the body of the child are the mother and father, and the third partner is the Holy One, blessed is He, who gives the soul. All of this is an analogy for the Creation as well, where ruach maps to the father, nefesh to the mother, and neshama to the Holy One, blessed is He.**

שֶׁהוּא מֵאוֹרוֹ עַצְמוֹ הַמִּשְׁתַּתֵּף בְּהַנִּבְרָאִים בְּקִיּוּמָם וְהַנְהָגָתָם

**the light in and of itself i.e. the light of Beriah itself, since neshama is the essential level of Beriah which ‘partners’ with created beings in maintaining and directing them.**

וְהִנֵּה עִקַּר גִּלּוּיוֹ שֶׁל אוֹתוֹ הָאוֹר שֶׁהוּא הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית

**Now the primary revelation of that very macro-level neshama light**

הוּא בְּעוֹלָם הַבְּרִיאָה שֶׁהִיא הַשֹּׁרֶשׁ דְּהַיְּצִירָה וְהָעֲשִׂיָה

**is in the World of Beriah, which is the root of Yetzirah and Asiyah ,**

רוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ הַכְּלָלִיִּים וְעוֹלָם הַבְּרִיאָה הוּא שָׁרְשָׁם

**and they (Yetzirah and Asiyah) are macro-level ruach and nefesh , and the World of Beriah is their root , as explained previously.**

וּמִשּׁוּם שֶׁשָּׁם הוּא הַגִּלּוּי דְּאֹורוֹ עַצְמוֹ הַיּוֹרֵד שָׁם שֶׁהִיא הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית כַּנּזְכָּר

**And because there in Beriah is where the revelation of its own light the light of macro-level neshama descends, as mentioned,**

לָכֵן נִקְרָא כָּל הַבְּרִיאָה כֻּלָּהּ עַל שֶׁמָּה

**therefore the entire Beriah is also called by that name – the ‘World of Creation.’**

וּבְשֵּׁם נְשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית כִּי הִיא מֵאִיר בְּאוֹרוֹ וּמִתְבַּטֶּלֶת אֵלָיו

**The World of Beriah is also called the world of macro-level neshama , since it the World of Beriah is illuminated with that light the light of macro-level neshama .**

אֲבָל עִקַּר הַבְּרִיאָה עֲצְמָה הֲרֵי הִיא מֵהַנִּבְרָאִים

**However, Beriah itself is a created entity**

וְעַצְמוּתָהּ הִיא ג " כ רַק רוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ לְבַד אֶלָּא שֶׁהִיא בִּבְחִי ' שֹׁרֶשׁ אֲלֵיהֶם וְכַנִּז '

**whose essence is also only ruach and nefesh , albeit that it Beriah is their root,**

כִּי הָרוּחַ דְּהַבְּרִיאָה הוּא הַשֹּׁרֶשׁ דְּהַיְּצִירָה וְהַנֶּפֶשׁ דְּהַבְּרִיאָה הוּא הַשֹּׁרֶשׁ דְּהָעֲשִׂיָה

**being that ruach of Beriah is the root of Yetzirah, and nefesh of Beriah is the root of Asiyah.**

וְאִלּוּלֵא הַחֵטְא הָיָה מִתְיַחֵד הַבְּרִיאָה עִמָּהֶם

**And were it not for the sin of Adam and Eve partaking of the fruit of the Tree of Knowledge Beriah would have united with them with Yetzirah and Asiyah,**

כְּכָל שֹׁרֶשׁ שֶׁהוּא מְיֻחָד עִם הֶעָנָף הַיּוֹצֵא וְנִמְשָׁךְ מִמֶּנּוּ

**just like any root that becomes one with the branch that emerges out of it and is drawn from it.**

וְלֹא הָיָה נִבְדָּל מֵהֶם הַבְּרִיאָה לְעוֹלָם בפנ " ע [ בִּפְנֵי עַצְמָה ] כְּלָל כִּי הֵם רַק גִּלּוּיָהּ .

**Nor would it Beriah have become a world disconnected from them Yetzirah and Asiyah at all, since they are only the revelation of Beriah.**

אַךְ ע " י הַחֵטְא הִנֵּה נִבְדָּל הַבְּרִיאָה לְעַצְמָהּ

**However, because of the sin, Beriah did become detached from them and remains a World on its own.**

[ הגהה וְהוּא בְּסוֹד קַטְנוּת וְגַדְלוּת

**[Author’s note: This is the secret of spiritual immaturity or undeveloped consciousness – katnut, and spiritual maturity or developed consciousness – gadlut :**

כִּי עוֹלָמוֹת בי " ע הֵם בִּבְחִי ' בִּינָּה וְזו " ן [ זְעֵיר אַנְפִּין וְנֻקְבָּא ] כְּנוֹדָע .

**since the Worlds of Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah are under the ‘patronage’ of binah, zeir’ anpin, and nukvah ( malchut ) respectively, as is known.**

Binah is in the state of gadlut since it represents expanded and developed consciousness. Accordingly Beriah is qualitatively different from Yetzirah and Asiyah, where ze’ir anpin and malchut respectively are the primary sefirot in those Worlds. The latter (and particularly malchut ) are in an immature and undeveloped state compared with binah .

וּבְסוֹד מ " ש הספ " צ [ הֲסִפְרָא דֶּצְנִעוּתָא ] ספ " א אָתָא מַפְתְּחָא כו ' וַוי לְמָאן דַּגָּלֵי פִּתְחָאָה ... עַיֵּן שָׁם ]

**And this is the secret of that which is stated in the Sifra Ditzniuta 15 a subsection of the Zohar on parshat Terumah chapter 1 : “Along comes the key that comprises six attributes… and covers the opening… Woe to one who reveals that opening…” See there. End of note ].**

The basic idea here is a warning to one who engages in illicit sexual behavior, as Adam did when he coupled with Eve prior to the appropriate time. This was the sin of the Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil as explained in the Zohar and the writings of the Arizal. This sin caused the World of Beriah to become detached from the Worlds of Yetzirah and Asiyah.

וְהֵם עַתָּה ג ' עוֹלָמוֹת , וְנִקְרָא בְּשֵׁם נְשָׁמָה ,

**And this is why there are now three Worlds – Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah – but only Beriah is named neshama**

מִשּׁוּם שֶׁבָּהּ מִתְלַבֵּשׁ אוֹר הָאֲצִילוּת שֶׁהוּא אוֹר אֱלֹהוּתוֹ ית " ש הַמִּתְפַּשֵּׁט לַמַּטֶּה

**due to the fact that the light of Atzilut, which is the light i.e. revelation of His blessed Godliness, is clothed in it and spreads out into Beriah below.**

וְהוּא הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית שֶׁהִיא רַק אוֹר אֱלָהוּת כַּנָּ " ל

**And this light that illuminates Beriah is the light of macro-level neshama , which is exclusively Godliness, as explained above.**

וּמִתְבַּטֶּלֶת הַבְּרִיאָה אֶל הָאוֹר הַזֶּה הַשּׁוֹכֵן עָלֶיהָ וְנִקְרֵאת עַל שְׁמָּהּ

**And the World of Beriah is existentially nullified to this light that rests upon it and by whose name it is called.**

אֲבָל הַבְּרִיאָה עַצְמָהּ הִיא בֶּאֱמֶת ג " כ רַק רוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ שֹׁרֶשׁ דִּיצִירָה וַעֲשִׂיָּה כַּנָּ " ל

**But in and of itself Beriah too is actually only ruach and nefesh , which are the roots of Yetzirah and Asiyah respectively, as explained above.**

וּכְנֶגְדָּהּ הוּא הַמֹּחַ שֶׁבְּכָל פַּרְצוּף שֶׁהוּא הַכְּלִי לַהַנְשָׁמָה הַפְּרָטִית שֶׁהִיא הַשֹּׁרֶשׁ דְּהַרוּחַ

**And corresponding to it to Beriah in every partzuf is its ‘brain,’ which is a receptacle for the micro-level neshama that is the root of ruach ,**

וְכֵן מִתְפַּשֶּׁטֶת וּמִתְלַבֵּשׁ בָּהּ הֶאָרַת הַנְשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית ג " כ וְכַנָּ " ל .

**and a radiance of macro-level neshama also spreads forth into it and becomes clothed within it, as explained above.**
