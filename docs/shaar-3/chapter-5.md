***Shaar* 3 Chapter 5**

אָמְנָם מָה שֶׁיֵּשׁ לִכְאוֹרָה לִתְמֹהַּ בְּזֶה כִּי הוּא נֶגֶד הַנּוֹדָע

**However, at first glance, what we just said[^1] – that *neshama* of *ruach* and *nefesh* is in the brain, rather than *neshama* of *neshama*[^2] – seems surprising, because it contradicts what we know,**

וְנֶגֶד מָה שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ לְעֵיל בְּכָל דְּבָרֵינוּ

**and it goes against everything we said above –**

שֶׁיֶּשְׁנוֹ לכ"א מהנר"נ מָקוֹם מְיֻחָד בְּהַגּוּף וְהֵם הָג' כֵּלִים מֹחַ לֵב כָּבֵד

**that *neshama*, *ruach* and *nefesh* \[from here on abbreviated as NR"N for the macro-level and nr"n for the micro-level\] each have a designated location in the body, which are the three 'vessels' for the three parts of the soul – the brain, the heart and the liver –**

שֶׁהַנְּשָׁמָה הִיא לְמַעְלָה בְּהַמּוֹח וְהָרוּחַ הוּא בְּאֶמְצָע שֶׁהוּא בַּלֵּב

**such that the *neshama* is located above in the brain, *ruach* in the middle, in the heart,**

וְהַנֶּפֶשׁ הוּא לְמַּטָּה שֶׁהוּא בַּכָּבֵד

**and *nefesh* below, in the liver.**

כִּי כְּמוֹ שֶׁהוּא בִּכְלָלוּת הָעוֹלָמוֹת כֵּן הוּא בִּפְרָטוּת כָּל פַּרְצוּף

**The answer is that just as it is in the general or universal i.e. macro-level Worlds, so too is it in the individual or local i.e. micro-level of every *partzuf*:**

In order to maintain the proper perspective regarding these two aspects – the general/universal as opposed to the individual/local – and thus avoid misunderstanding, we will refer to them as macro-level and micro-level respectively.

וּבִכְלָלוּת הָעוֹלָמוֹת שֶׁהֵם בְּרִיאָה יְצִירָה עָשֶׁיהָ

**in the macro-level Worlds – *Beriah*, *Yetzirah* and *Asiyah* –**

וּבְרִיאָה הִיא נְשָׁמָה וִיצִירָה רוּחַ וְהָעֲשָׂיָה נֶפֶשׁ

***Beriah* is *neshama*, *Yetzirah* is *ruach* and *Asiyah* is *nefesh*.**

הֲרֵי הֵם בְּכָל עִקָּרָם זֶה לְמַעְלָה מִזֶּה לְגַמְרֵי

**Thus inherently they are one above the other completely, as discrete levels with no overlap.**

וְהֵם הנר"נ הַכְּלָלִיִּים

**And they are *neshama*, *ruach* and *nefesh* \[NR"N\] on the macro-level.**

וְכֵן הוּא בִּפְרָטוֹת ג"כ שֶׁבְּמָקוֹם שֶׁמִּסְתַּיֵּם הָעֶלְיוֹן שָׁם מַתְחִיל הַתַּחְתּוֹן

**And similarly on the micro-level – where the higher level ends, only there does the lower level begin, with no overlap.**

אֲבָל לְפִי מָה שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ עַתָּה הֲרֵי יִהְיֶה נִמְצָא שֶׁאֵין חִלּוּק מְקוֹמוֹת בֵּינֵיהֶם כְּלָל

**But according to what we just said – that nr"n are arranged in an integrated, overlapping manner, not one below the other – it would turn out that there is no separation of place i.e. (spiritual) boundary separating them at all,**

כִּי הֲרֵי הֵם שְׁלָּשְׁתָם בַּמֹּחַ שֶׁהוּא הַנְּשָׁמָה שֶׁבָּהֶם

**since all three of them are manifested in the brain which is the *neshama*-level among them,**

וְשְׁלָּשְׁתָם בַּלֵּב שֶׁהוּא הָרוּחַ שֶׁבָּהֶם

**and all three of them are manifested in the heart which is the *ruach*-level among them,**

וְשְׁלָּשְׁתָם בַּכָּבֵד שֶׁהוּא הַנֶּפֶשׁ שֶׁבָּהֶם

**and all three of them are manifested in the liver which is the *nefesh*-level among them.**

וְהוּא ע"פ מָה שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ שֶׁגַּם הנר"נ דְּהַנְשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית

**This is according to our statement that NR"N of *neshama* at the macro-level**

הֵם מִתְפַּשְּׁטִים ג"כ וּמִתְלַבְּשִׁים עִם הַקָּצֶה הַיּוֹתֵר תַּחְתּוֹן שֶׁבָּהֶם בְּהַָרוּחַ

**also spread forth and become clothed – via their lowest edge – in *ruach*.**

וּפָשׁוּט הוּא שֶׁאוֹתָן הַבְחִי' דהנר"נ הֵם מִתְפַּשְּׁטִים וּמִתְחַלְּקִים ג"כ

**And it is obvious that those aspects of NR"N also spread out and subdivide**

ע"ד הַסֵּדֶר דהנר"נ שֶׁבְּהַָרוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ הַכְּלָלִיִּים

**in the same manner and order as NR"N of macro-level *ruach* and *nefesh*,**

וַהֲרֵי הֱווּ שְׁלָשָׁתָם בְּהַמּוֹחַ וּשְׁלָשָׁתָם בְּהַלֵּב וּשְׁלָשָׁתָם בְּהַכָּבֵד

**so that all three of them are vested in the brain, all three of them in the heart, and all three of them in the liver.**

וא"כ הֲרֵי אֵין חִלּוּק מְקוֹמוֹת בֵּין מַעֲלֶה וּמַטָּה בהנר"נ הַכְּלָלִיִּים

**Accordingly, there is no separation i.e. no boundary separating between upper and lower of the macro-level NR"N**

אֲשֶׁר בְּכָל פַּרְצוּף וּפָרְצוּף גּוּפָהּ כְּלַל.

**that is in each *partzuf* itself; none at all.**

משא"כ בהנר"נ הַכְּלָלִיִּים דְּהָעוֹלָמוֹת הֲרֵי הֵם חֲלוּקִים בִּמְקוֹמָם לְגַמְרֵי

**But this is not so as regards the macro-level NR"N of the Worlds.**

שֶׁהֲרֵי עוֹלַם הַבְּרִיאָה שֶׁהִיא הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית דְּהָעוֹלָמוֹת

**Thus the World of *Beriah*, which is the macro-level Neshama of the Worlds,**

הִיא עוֹמֶדֶת בְּכָל עִקָּרָהּ לְמַעְלָה

**is located entirely there in the upper segment of the map of the Worlds.**

וְעוֹלָם הַיְּצִירָה שֶׁהוּא הָרוּחַ הַכְּלָלִי הוּא עוֹמֵד בְּכָל עִקָּרוֹ בְּאֶמְצַע

**And the World of *Yetzirah*, which is the macro-level Ruach of the Worlds, is located entirely in the middle segment of the map of the Worlds.**

וְעוֹלָם הָעֲשִׂיָּה שֶׁהִיא הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַכְּלָלִי הִיא בְּכָל עִקָּרָהּ לְמַטָּה

**And the World of *Asiyah*, which is the macro-level Nefesh of the Worlds, is located entirely in the lower segment of the map of the Worlds.**

וַהֲרֵי לֹא הֱווּ הנר"נ הַכְּלָלִיִּים שֶׁבְּהָעוֹלְמוֹת

**Hence the NR"N of the macro-level Worlds**

עִם הנר"נ הַכְּלָלִיִּים שֶׁבְּכָל פַּרְצוּף וּפָרְצוּף בְּסֵדֶר אֶחָד כְּלָל

**and the NR"N of each macro-level *partzuf* are not of one configuration i.e. they do not parallel each other.**

וְהוּא לִכְאוֹרָה קוּשְׁיָא גְּדוֹלָה כִּי הֲרֵי לְעֵיל פ' א' וב'

**Now this would seem to present a tremendous difficulty, for above in chapters 1 and 2**

אָמַרְנוּ שֶׁסֵּדֶר מְצִיאַת הָעוֹלָמוֹת עִם סֵדֶר בִּנְיַן כָּל פַּרְצוּף שָׁוִים הֵם בְּכָל וְכָל:

**we stated that the manner in which the existence of the Worlds is arranged, and the manner in which each *partzuf* is configured, is identical in every respect!**

אַךְ הָעִנְיָן בְּזֶה הוּא כִּי הֲרֵי כְּבָר מִתְבָּאֵר מֵדִבְרֵינוּ לְעֵיל פֵּ"א

**However, the solution to this conundrum was already comprehensible from what we said in chapter 1 –**

כִּי כָּל נר"נ הִנֵּה כְּשֵׁם שֶׁהֵם חֲלוּקִים בֵּין מַעֲלֶה וּמַטָּה

**that in all instances of NR"N, just as there is a distinction between top and bottom i.e. on the vertical plane,**

שֶׁהַנְּשָׁמָה הוּא לְמַעְלָה וְהָרוּחַ בָּאֶמְצַע וְהַנֶּפֶשׁ לַמַּטֶּה

**so that *neshama* is in the top segment, *ruach* is in the middle segment, and *nefesh* is in the bottom segment,**

כֵּן הֵם חֲלוּקִים ג"כ בֵּין פְּנִימִי לְחִיצוֹן

**so too are they distinct in terms of inner and outer i.e. on the horizontal plane.**

שֶׁהַנְּשָׁמָה הִיא בַּחֵלֶק הַפְּנִימִי שֶׁבַּגּוּף

**Thus the *neshama* is in the inner layer of the body,**

וְהָרוּחַ הוּא בְּחֵלֶק הַתִּיכוֹן וְהַנֶּפֶשׁ בַּחֵלֶק הַחִיצוֹן

***ruach* is the central layer, and *nefesh* is in the outer layer.**

וַהֲרֵי הֵם ג' מַדְרֵגוֹת בְּגֹבַהּ שֶׁהֵם מַעֲלֶה וְאֶמְצַע וּמַטֶּה

**Thus there are three levels vertically – top, mid-level, bottom –**

וְכֵן ג' מַדְרֵגוֹת בְּעָבְיוֹ שֶׁהֵם פְּנִימִי תִּיכוֹן חִיצוֹן

**and so too there are three layers horizontally – inner, central and outer.**

וְהִנֵּה הֵם תֵּשַׁע בְּחִי' שֶׁהֵם רֹאשׁ תּוֹךְ סוֹף

**Thus in all there are nine aspects: top, middle, bottom – transliterated: *rosh*, *toch*, *sof* –**

שכ"א הוּא כָּלוּל מִג' שֶׁהֵם פְּנִימִי תִּיכוֹן חִיצוֹן

**such that each of them comprises three subdivisions – inner, central, outer –**

וְהִנֵּה הֵם הנר"נ שֶׁבַּנְּשָׁמָה וְהוּא בַּמֹּחַ

**and these are nr"n of *neshama* in the brain;**

והנר"נ שֶׁבָּרוּחַ וְהוּא בְּלֵב

**nr"n of *ruach* in the heart;**

והנר"נ שֶׁבַּנֶּפֶשׁ בְּנֶפֶשׁ וְהוּא בַּכָּבֵד וְהֵם ט'

**and nr"n of *nefesh* in the liver – making 9 altogether, since each of the three macro-levels is subdivided into three.**

\[וְעִם שֹׁרֶשׁ הַנֶּעֱלָם דכ"א אֲשֶׁר מִמֶּנּוּ הוּא יוֹצֵא וְנִמְשָׁךְ הוּא עֶשֶׂר.

**\[Author's note: Together with the hidden root of each of them – from which they emerge and are drawn down – there are 10.**

וְהֵם בְּחִי' הָעֶשֶׂר סְפִי' שֶׁיֶּשְׁנָם בכ"א

**These are the 10 *sefirot* that are in each level:**

כִּי הנר"נ שֶׁבְּהַנְשָׁמָה בְּהַפְנִימִי תִּיכוֹן חִיצוֹן שֶׁבָּהּ הֵם חב"ד \[חָכְמָה, בֵּינָהּ, דַּעַת\].

**The nr"n of *neshama* in its inner, central and outer layers are *chochmah*, *binah* and *da'at*.**

והנר"נ דְּהָרוּחַ בְּהַפת"ח \[פְּנִימִי, תִּיכוֹן, חִיצוֹן\] שֶׁבּוֹ. הֵם חג"ת \[חֶסֶד, גְּבוּרָה, תִּפְאֶרֶת\].

**The nr"n of *ruach* in its inner, central and outer layers are *chesed*, *gevurah* and *tiferet*.**

והנר"נ דְּהַנֶּפֶשׁ בְּהַפת"ח שֶׁבָּהּ הֵם נה"י \[נֶצַח, הוֹד, יְסוֹד\]

**And the nr"n of *nefesh* in its inner, central and outer layers are *netzach*, *hod*, *yesod*.**

(וְהַמַלְכוּת הִנֵּה הוּא פַּרְצוּף בפנ"ע פַּרְצוּף הַנּוּקְבָא

**(And *malchut* is an independent *partzuf*, called the *partzuf* of *nukvah*, the female *partzuf*,**

שֶׁיֶּשְׁנָם בָּהּ ג"כ כָּל אוֹתָן הָעֶשֶׂר)\]

**which also has all those ten *sefirot* in it). End of author's note.\]**

וכ"ז הוּא הֵן בִּכְלָלוּת הָעוֹלָמוֹת וְהֵן בִּפְרָטוֹת כָּל עוֹלָם וְכָל פַּרְצוּף

**And all this is so in the Worlds on the macro-level, and also on the micro-level in each world and each *partzuf*,**

כִּי כ"א הוּא כָּלוּל מנר"נ כַּנָּ"ל

**since each of them the macro-levels of NR"N is comprised of micro-level nr"n, as explained above,**

וְיֶשְׁנָם בכ"א כָּל הַתֵּשַׁע בְּחִי' שֶׁהֵם ג' חֶלְקֵי נר"נ וְהֵם הַי' סְפִי' שבכ"א.

**such that there is in each of them all nine aspects – the three layers of the inner, central and outer layers of each of NR"N – and these are the ten *sefirot* in each of them.**

As explained above: the nr"n of the inner layer (*neshama*) are *chochmah*, *binah* and *da'at*; the nr"n of the central layer (*ruach*) are *chesed*, *gevurah* and *tiferet*; and the nr"n of the outer layer (*nefesh*) are *netzach*, *hod*, *yesod* – plus *malchut*.

וְהִנֵּה הג' עוֹלָמוֹת בְּרִיאָה יְצִירָה עָשֶׁיהָ שֶׁהֵם הנר"נ הַכְּלָלִיִּים

**Now in the three worlds, *Beriah*, *Yetzirah* and *Asiyah*, which are the macro-level NR"N,**

וְיֶשְׁנָם בכ"א מֵהֶם הַתְּשַׁע בְּחַי' הַנִּזְ'

**the nine aspects mentioned are present in each of them.**

הֲרֵי דַּעַת לְנָבוֹן נָקָל[^3]

**Behold, (Proverbs 14:6) "…wisdom will come easily to one who understands"[^4]**

כִּי הג' חֶלְקֵי רוּחַ הַפְּרָטִיִּים אֲשֶׁר בָּרֹאשׁ תּוֹךְ סוֹף דְּהַנְשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית, שֶׁהִיא עוֹלָם הַבְּרִיאָה,

**that the three micro-level aspects of *ruach* – the aspect of *ruach* of *rosh*, *toch*, *sof* of the macro-level *neshama*, which corresponds to the World of *Beriah*,**

וְהֵם בְּחֶלְקֵי הַתִּיכוֹן שֶׁבָּהֶם,

**are the subsections of their central layer horizontally, since we are referring to *ruach*, the central layer –**

הִנֵּה הֵם הַשֹּׁרֶשׁ לְכָל עוֹלַם הַיְּצִירָה כֻּלּוֹ

**– they the three micro-level components of the macro-level i.e. macro-level Neshama are the root of the entire World of *Yetzirah*,**

שֶׁהוּא הָרוּחַ הַכְּלָלִי בְּכָל פְּרָטָיו שֶׁבּוֹ

**which is the macro-level Ruach with all of its subsections.**

וְכֵן הג' חֶלְקֵי נֶפֶשׁ הַפְּרָטִיִּים אֲשֶׁר בָּרֹאשׁ תּוֹךְ סוֹף דְּהַנְשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית

**Similarly, the three micro-level components of the *rosh*, *toch*, *sof* of the macro-level *neshama*,**

שֶׁהוּא בְּחֶלְקֵי הַחִיצוֹן שֶׁבָּהֶם

**which is their outer layer horizontally,**

הִנֵּה הֵם הַשֹּׁרֶשׁ לְכָל עוֹלַם הָעֲשִׂיָּה כֻּלָּהּ

**are the root of the entire World of *Asiyah*,**

שֶׁהִיא הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַכְּלָלִי בְּכָל פְּרָטֶיהָ שֶׁבָּהּ:

**which is the macro-level Nefesh with all of its details.**

וְהֲעִנְיָן בְּבֵאוּר הוּא כִּי הִנֵּה מִחֵלֶּק הָרוּחַ הַפְּרָטִי שבהנר"נ דְּהַנְשָׁמָה דְּבְרִיאָה

שֶׁהֵם הַבְּרִיאָה וְהַיְּצִירָה

**which are i.e. correspond to the Worlds of *Beriah* and *Yetzirah* respectively,**

וְהוּא מֵחֵלֶּק הַחִיצוֹן שֶׁבָּהֶם.

**and are of their outer layer.[^6]**

וְכֵן הֲרֵי נִמְצָא ג"כ כִּי עוֹלַם הַבְּרִיאָה הִיא הַשֹּׁרֶשׁ וְהַנְּשָׁמָה לְהַיְּצִירָה וְהָעֲשָׂיָה.

**We also find that the World of *Beriah* is the root and the *neshama* of *Yetzirah* and *Asiyah*,**

וְעוֹלָם הַיְּצִירָה הוּא הָרוּחַ לְהַבְרִיאָהּ וְהָעֲשָׂיָה,

**and that *Yetzirah* is the *ruach* of *Beriah* and *Asiyah*,**

וְעוֹלָם הָעֲשִׂיָּה הִיא הַנֶּפֶשׁ לְהַבְרִיאָהּ וְהַיְּצִירָה.

**and that *Asiyah* is the *nefesh* of *Beriah* and *Yetzirah*.**

כִּי עִקַּר הַגִּלּוּי דְּרוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ אֲשֶׁר נַעֲשָׂה בְּהַבְּרִיאָה

**This is because the primary emergence of *ruach* and *nefesh* generated in *Beriah***

הוּא רַק אַחַר שֶׁיָּצָא וְנַעֲשָׂה עוֹלַם הַיְּצִירָה וְהָעֲשִׂיָּה

**is only after the Worlds of *Yetzirah* and *Asiyah* emerge and are produced.**

The primary aspect of *Beriah* is *neshama* (since it characterizes *Beriah* on the macro-level) and therefore it has no 'need' for *ruach* and *nefesh*. However, once *Yetzirah* and *Asiyah* are created, the *ruach* of *Beriah* becomes the source of the *neshama* of *Yetzirah* (\#a in Diagram 6), and *nefesh* of *Beriah* becomes the source of the *neshama* of *Asiyah* (\#b in Diagram 6).

אֲבָל מִקֹּדֶם הָיָה כָּל בְּחִי' רוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ בָּעוֹלָם הַבְּרִיאָה רַק בְּכֹחַ וְלֹא בְּפֹעַל.

**But prior to this, any aspect of *ruach* and *nefesh* in the World of *Beriah* was only in potential, but was not actualized.**

וְיָצָא הָרוּחַ בַּבְּרִיאָה מִן הַכֹּחַ אֶל הַפּוֹעֵל רַק לְצֹרֶךְ הַמְּצִיאוּת דְּהַיְּצִירָה וְעַל יָדוֹ

**Thus *ruach* emerged from a state of potential to actual in *Beriah* only for the requirements of the existence of *Yetzirah*, and by way thereof.**

וְכֵן הַגִּלּוּי דִּמְצִיאַת הַנֶּפֶשׁ בְּעוֹלָם הַבְּרִיאָה וְהַיְּצִירָה

**And likewise the emergence of the actualization of *nefesh* in the Worlds of *Beriah* and *Yetzirah***

הוּא רַק לַצֹּרֶךְ הַמְּצִיאֻת דְּעוֹלָם הָעֲשִׂיָּה וְעַל יָדָהּ דַּוְקָא

**is only for the requirements of the existence of *Asiyah*, and precisely by way thereof.**

*nefesh* of *Beriah* is required for *neshama* of *Asiyah* (\#b in Diagram 6), and *nefesh* of *Yetzirah* is required for *ruach* of *Asiyah* (\#c in Diagram 6).

וַהֲרֵי הֲוָה הָעֲשִׂיָּה הַנֶּפֶשׁ לִשְׁנֵיהֶם וְהַיְּצִירָה הָרוּחַ לִשְׁנֵיהֶם וְהַבְּרִיאָה נְשָׁמָה לִשְׁנֵיהֶם

**Thus *Asiyah* constitutes the *nefesh* of both of them – of *Beriah* and *Yetzirah*; and *Yetzirah* constitutes the *ruach* of both of them – of *Beriah* and *Asiyah*; and *Beriah* constitutes the *neshama* of both of them – of *Yetzirah* and *Asiyah*.**

כִּי לֹא נִתְגַּלּוּ כ"א בְּכָל הג' בְּחִי' נר"נ שֶׁבּוֹ אֶלָּא ע"י שְׁלָּשְׁתָם דַּוְקָא

**This is because none of them became revealed in all three aspects of its nr"n, other than precisely by way of all three of them.**

אֲבָל מִקֹּדֶם שֶׁיָּצְאוּ שְׁלָּשְׁתָם כ"א עַל שְׁלֵמוּתוֹ הָיָה כ"א רַק בִּבְחִינָתוֹ לְבַד

**But prior to all three emerging in a state of completion with all three aspects of nr"n, each of them possessed only its own aspect –**

הַבְּרִיאָה בִּבְחִי' נְשָׁמָה וְהַיְּצִירָה רוּחַ וְהָעֲשָׂיָה נֶפֶשׁ

***Beriah* had only *neshama*, *Yetzirah* had only *ruach*, and *Asiyah* had only *nefesh* –**

וּשְׁאָר הַב' בְּחִי' הָיָה בכ"א רַק בְּכֹחַ וְלֹא בְּפֹעַל

**while the other two aspects were only in potential in each of them, but were not actualized.**

וְנִגְמַר וְנִשְׁלַם כ"א רַק ע"י שְׁלָּשְׁתָם דַּוְקָא וַהֲרֵי הֵם כְּלוּלִים זֶה בְּזֶה

**Hence each one of them becomes complete and fulfilled only by way of all three of them, and thus they are all inter-included, one with the other.**

וְזֶהוּ שֶׁאָמַר הַסֵּפֶר יְצִירָה פ"א מִשָּׁנָה ז'

**And this is what it states in *Sefer Yetzirah*, chapter 1 mishnah 7:**

עֶשֶׂר סְפִי' בְּלִימָה נָעוּץ סוֹפָן בִּתְחִלָּתָן וּתְחִלָּתָן בְּסוֹפָן

**"The ten *sefirot* without substance (*bli mah*, meaning 'of incomprehensible nature') – their end is wedged in their beginning, and their beginning in their end."**

וְהוּא ע"פ מָה שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ כִּי הֵם מֻשְׁרָשִׁים וּכְלוּלִים זב"ז

**This is according to what we said above that they are rooted in each other, and are inter-included with one another,**

וּמִתְגַּלִּים זֶה ע"י זֶה וְנִמְשָׁכִים וְנַעֲשִׂים זֶה מִזֶּה:

**and become revealed one via the other, and they are drawn from and produced from each other.**

וְהִנֵּה הג' חֶלְקֵי נר"נ אֲשֶׁר אָמַרְנוּ שֶׁהֵם בְּכָל עוֹלָם וְכֵן בְּכָל פַּרְצוּף

**Now regarding the three sections called nr"n which we stated are in every World, and in every *partzuf* –**

הִנֵּה הנר"נ שֶׁבַּהַנְשָׁמָה הוּא בְּחִי' הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִי שֶׁבְּכָל עוֹלָם וְשֶׁבְּכָל פַּרְצוּף

**The nr"n of *neshama* is the aspect of macro-level Neshama in each World and in each *partzuf*.**

The *neshama* level of each World and the *neshama* level of each *partzuf* of that World ultimately derive from the macro-macro-level Neshama of *Beriah*.

והנר"נ שֶׁבְְּהַָרוּחַ הוּא בְּחִי' רוּחַ הַכְּלָלִי שֶׁבְּכָל עוֹלָם וְשֶׁבְּכָל פַּרְצוּף

**And the nr"n of *ruach* is the aspect of macro-level Ruach in each World and in each *partzuf*.**

The *ruach* level of each World ultimately derives from the macro-macro-level *ruach* of *Yetzirah*.

והנר"נ שֶׁבַּנֶּפֶשׁ הוּא בְּחִי' נֶפֶשׁ הַכְּלָלִי שֶׁבְּכָל עוֹלָם וְשֶׁבְּכָל פַּרְצוּף

**And the nr"n of *nefesh* is the aspect of macro-level Nefesh in each World and in each *partzuf*.**

The *nefesh* level of each World ultimately derives from the macro-macro-level *nefesh* of *Asiyah*.

וַהֲרֵי נִמְצָא כִּי הנר"נ הַכְּלָלִיִּים שֶׁבְּכָל עוֹלָם וְשֶׁבְּכָל פַּרְצוּף

**Thus we find that the macro-level nr"n in each of the Worlds and in each *partzuf***

הִנֵּה הֵם ג"כ בֶּאֱמֶת רַק זֶה לְמַעְלָה מִזֶּה לְגַמְרֵי

**are also in fact only arrayed one completely above the other without overlap,**

כִּי הֲרֵי הֵם מְסֻדָּרִים בָּרֹאשׁ תּוֹךְ סוֹף

**so that they are arranged in the manner of *rosh*, *toch*, *sof* – or top, middle, bottom – with no overlap.**

וַהֲרֵי הֵם זֶה לְמַעְלָה מִזֶּה לְגַמְרֵי כְּמוֹ הנר"נ דִּכְלָלֵי כְּלָלוּת[^7]

**Hence they are arrayed one completely above the other, just like the NR"N of the macro-macro-level,**

שֶׁהֵם הָעוֹלָמוֹת בְּרִיאָה יְצִירָה עָשֶׁיהָ שֶׁהֵם זֶה לְמַעְלָה מִזֶּה לְגַמְרֵי

**– i.e. the general or macro-macro-level Worlds of *Beriah*, *Yetzirah* and *Asiyah*, which are arrayed one completely above the other with no overlap,**

שֶׁהַקַּרְקַע דְּהָעֶלְיוֹן הוּא הַגַּג דְּהַתַּחְתּוֹן

**so that the floor of the upper World is the roof of the lower World,**

כִּי הֵם בִּבְחִי' עֲלִיּוֹת זוֹ עַל גַּבֵּי זוֹ וכמ"ש הָרַב בַּשַּׁעַר דְּרוּשֵׁי אבי"ע פ' י"ד

**and they are like the ascending floors of a building, one on top of the other, as Rabbi Chaim Vital writes in the name of the Arizal in *Eitz Chaim*, *Shaar Drushei Abiy"a* chapter 14.**

כֵּן הוּא ג"כ הַבְּחִי' נר"נ הַכְּלָלִיִּים שֶׁבְּכָל עוֹלָם וְשֶׁבְּכָל פַּרְצוּף

**And so too regarding the aspect of macro-level nr"n in each World and in each *partzuf* –**

הֵם זֶה לְמַעְלָה מִזֶּה עַד שֶׁבְּמָקוֹם שֶׁמִּסְתַּיֵּם הָעֶלְיוֹן שָׁם מַתְחִיל הַתַּחְתּוֹן

**they too are arrayed one above the other to the extent that where the upper one ends, only then does the lower one begin, and again there is no overlap.**

אֶלָּא שעכ"ז הֵמָּה הֵם כְּלוּלִים זב"ז ג"כ

**Nevertheless, they are all inter-included one with the other as well.**

Even though each level is distinct and there is no overlap between the Worlds or between the *partzufim*, the inner structure of all the Worlds is similar, as will be explained.

וְהוּא ע"י כִּי מָה שֶׁהֵם יוֹרְדִים זֶה מִזֶּה ע"י שֶׁהֵם מְסֻדָּרִים בָּרֹאשׁ תּוֹךְ סוֹף

**This is due to the fact that although they descend from one another since they are arrayed in the manner of *rosh*, *toch*, *sof* (top, middle, bottom – vertically),**

הִנֵּה הֵם חוֹזְרִים וְעוֹלִים זב"ז ע"י הַסֵּדֶר דִּפְנִימִי תִּיכוֹן חִיצוֹן

**they also return and ascend via one another due to their inner configuration of inner, central and outer layers horizontally.**

כִּי הַתֹּךְ וְהַתִּיכוֹן הֵם מִשֹּׁרֶשׁ אֶחָד וְנַעֲשִׂים לִבְחִי' אַחַת

**The inner layer horizontally and the middle section vertically stem from the same root and become one composite aspect.**

וְכֵן הַסּוֹף וְהַחִיצוֹן שֶׁהֵם מִשֹּׁרֶשׁ אֶחָד נַעֲשִׂים לִבְחִי' אַחַת.

**Also the bottom section (*sof*) and the outer layer (*chitzon*) stem from the same root and become one composite aspect.**

וַהֲרֵי נִמְצָא כִּי הֲגַם שֶׁהֵם יוֹרְדִים זֶה מִזֶּה עכ"ז הִנֵּה הֵם חוֹזְרִים וְעוֹלִים זֶה בְּזֶה

**Thus we find that they descend from one another, nevertheless, they also return and ascend into one another,**

וְהֵם עוֹמְדִים כְּלוּלִים זב"ז לְעוֹלָם.

**and they always maintain their inter-included state.**

וכ"ז הִנֵּה הוּא הֵן בְּכָל עוֹלָם וּבְכָל פָּרְצוּפִים הַפְּרָטִיִּים

**Now all of this is in each of the worlds and in all of the micro-level *partzufim***

וְהֵן בְּהָעוֹלָמוֹת בְּרִיאָה יְצִירָה עָשֶׁיהָ הַכְּלָלִיִּים

**as well as in the macro-macro-worlds of *Beriah*, *Yetzirah* and *Asiyah*.**

וַהֲרֵי לָנוּ כִּי הָאֱמֶת הוּא שֶׁהַכְּלָל וְהַפְּרָט הֵם שְׁנֵיהֶם מְסֻדָּרִים בְּסֵדֶר אֶחָד

**Thus we come to the conclusion that in truth the macro-macro-level i.e. NR"N and their sub-levels i.e. nr"n are arranged in the identical manner –**

כִּי הֲרֵי בִּשְׁנֵיהֶם הֲוָה עִקַּר הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית לְמַעְלָה

**since in both of them the primary aspect of macro-level *neshama* is above at the top,**

וְעִקַּר רוּחַ הַכְּלָלִי בְּאֶמְצַע וְעִקַּר הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַכְּלָלִי לְמַטָּה

**and the primary aspect of macro-level *ruach* is in the middle, and the primary aspect of macro-level *nefesh* is below at the bottom.**

אֶלָּא שע"י הַסֵּדֶר דְּהַפְּנִימִי תִּיכוֹן חִיצוֹן שֶׁבָּהֶם

**However, via the arrangement of inner, central and outer,**

הִנֵּה כָּלוּל הַנְּשָׁמָה דכ"א שֶׁבִּשְׁנֵיהֶם לְמַטָּה ג"כ[^8]

**the *neshama* of each level in both of them is integrated below into the Worlds of *Yetzirah* and *Asiyah* as well,**

וְהָרוּחַ וְהַנֶּפֶשׁ דכ"א שֶׁבִּשְׁנֵיהֶם לְמַעְלָה ג"כ וְהֵם כְּלוּלִים זב"ז לְעוֹלָם:

**and the *nefesh* and *ruach* of each level in both of them is above in *Beriah*[^9] as well, and they are forever inter-included with one another.**

וְהִנֵּה כַּאֲשֶׁר תִּתְבּוֹנֵן בְּכָל דְּבָרֵינוּ הַלָּלוּ

**Now when you contemplate all of these words of ours,**

תָּבִין כִּי עֲצְמוּת הָעִקָּרִי דְּהַנְּשָׁמָה[^10]

**you will understand that what we refer to primarily as 'the *neshama* itself'**

הִנֵּה הוּא רַק חֵלֶק הַנְּשָׁמָה הַפְּרָטִי מהנר"נ שֶׁבַּנְּשָׁמָּה

**is actually only the micro-level aspect of *neshama* of nr"n of macro-level Neshama**

וְהוּא חֵלֶק הַפְּנִימִי דְּהַנְּשָׁמָּה הָעוֹמֵד לְמַעְלָה שֶׁהוּא הַנְּשָׁמָה דְּנְשָׁמָה.

**i.e. the inner aspect of the Neshama as it is above, that is to say, the *neshama* of macro-level Neshama.**

וְעֲצְמוּת הָעִקָּרִי דְְּהָרוּחַ הִנֵּה הוּא רַק חֵלֶק הָרוּחַ הַפְּרָטִי מהנר"נ דְּהָרוּחַ

**And what we refer to primarily as 'the *ruach* itself' is actually only the micro-level aspect of *ruach* of nr"n of macro-level Ruach**

וְהוּא חֵלֶק הַתִּיכוֹן דְּהָרוּחַ הָעֹמֵד בְּאֶמְצַע שֶׁהוּא הָרוּחַ דְּרוּחַ.

**which is the central layer horizontally of the *ruach* that is situated in the middle vertically, that is to say the *ruach* of macro-level Ruach.**

וְעֲצְמוּת הָעִקָּרִי דְּהַנֶּפֶשׁ הִנֵּה הוּא רַק חֵלֶק הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַפְּרָטִי מהנר"נ שֶׁבַּנֶּפֶשׁ

**And what we refer to primarily as 'the *nefesh* itself' is actually only the micro-level aspect of *nefesh* of nr"n of macro-level Nefesh**

וְהִיא הַחֵלֶק הַתַּחְתּוֹנָה וְהַחִיצוֹנָה שֶׁהוּא הַנֶּפֶשׁ דְּנֶפֶשׁ.

**which is the lowest and outermost layer, that is to say the *nefesh* of macro-level Nefesh.**

**And this is the hidden root of the macro-level *neshama*. Understand this well. End of author's note.\]**

ועד"ז הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַכְּלָלִי שֶׁהִיא בַּכָּבֵד כִּי הִנֵּה הַנֶּפֶשׁ שֶׁבָּהּ הוּא עִקַּר מְצִיאוּתָהּ גּוּפָהּ

**Similarly regarding macro-level *nefesh* in the liver –**

כִּי הִנֵּה הַנֶּפֶשׁ שֶׁבָּהּ הוּא עִקַּר מְצִיאוּתָהּ גּוּפָהּ

**the micro-level of *nefesh* therein i.e. *nefesh* of *nefesh* is its primary existence,**

שֶׁהוּא עַצְמוּתָהּ הָעִקָּרִי וּכְמוֹ שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ לְעֵיל בְּסָמוּךְ

**since this is its primary essence, as we just explained above.**

שֶׁעֲצָמוֹת הָעִקָּרִי דְּהַנֶּפֶשׁ הַכְּלָלִי הוּא הַנֶּפֶשׁ שֶׁבַּנֶּפֶשׁ

**Since the primary essence of the macro-level *nefesh* is the *nefesh* of *nefesh*.**

וְהָרוּחַ שֶׁבָּהּ הוּא תְּלִיָּתָהּ לְמַעְלָה

**And the aspect of *ruach* therein in the macro-level *nefesh* links the *nefesh* in to a higher level –**

ור"ל כִּי הוּא בְּחִי' הַמְּצִיאוּת אֲשֶׁר בּוֹ וְעַל יָדוֹ הִיא קְשׁוּרָה לְמַעְלָה בֶּהָרוּחַ הַכְּלָלִי

**meaning to say that it is that 'entity' in which and through which it the *nefesh* is bound above into macro-level *ruach* – \#9 in the diagram above.**

וְהַנְּשָׁמָה שֶׁבָּהּ הוּא תְּלִיָּתָהּ לְמַעְלָה בַּהַנְשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִי

**And the aspect of *neshama* therein of *nefesh* links the *nefesh* in to macro-level *neshama* – \#6 in the Diagram 6.**

כִּי מֵהַנְּשָׁמָה וְהָרוּחַ הַכְּלָלִיִּים נִמְשָׁךְ וְנַעֲשֶׂה הַנְּשָׁמָה וְהָרוּחַ שֶׁבָּהּ

**Because from macro-level *neshama* and *ruach*, is drawn down and produced therein in macro-level *neshama* \#2 in the diagram and *ruach* \#4 in the diagram respectively.**

וּבָהֶם הִיא קְשׁוּרָה לְמַעְלָה וְהוּא עַצְמוּתָהּ הָעִקָּרִי שֶׁבָּהּ, שֶׁהִיא הַנֶּפֶשׁ שֶׁבַּנֶּפֶשׁ

**And via them it the macro-level *nefesh* is bound up above, and this – *nefesh* of *nefesh* – is the primary essence thereof of macro-level *nefesh*.**

וַהֲרֵי לָנוּ אֵיךְ שֶׁהֵם כְּלוּלִים זֶה בְּזֶה וּכְנִזְ'.

**Thus we have the manner in which one is inter-included in the other, as mentioned,**

וַהֲרֵי נִתְבָּאֵר לָנוּ בכ"ז בעזה"י כָּל הָעִנְיָן עַל נָכוֹן:

**and thus the entire matter – each subject in the proper way – has been explained, with God's help.**
