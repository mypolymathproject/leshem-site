***Shaar* 3 Chapter 4**

וְדַע כִּי הַנְּשָׁמָה הַפְּרָטִית אֲשֶׁר גַּם בְּהַרוּחַ וְהַנֶּפֶשׁ הַכְּלָלִיִּים

**Know that micro-level *neshama*, even of *ruach* and *nefesh* on the macro-level,**

הִנֵּה מְקוֹמָם הוּא ג"כ רַק בְּהַמּוֹחַ

**is also located only in the brain** – so that not only the level of *neshama* of *neshama* resides in the brain, but even *neshama* of *ruach* and of *nefesh* resides there, as will be explained below.[^1]

וְהוּא מָה שֶׁאָנוּ רוֹאִין שֶׁיֵּשׁ בְּחִי' מֹחַ גַּם לַבְּהֵמָה

**This is evident in that we see that even animals have a brain.**

וְכֵן יֵשׁ בְּחַי' מֹחַ גַּם לַנֶּפֶשׁ הַצּוֹמַחַת שֶׁבְּכָל מִין צוֹמֵחַ

**Furthermore, there is some semblance of a brain[^2] in the vegetal life-force[^3] of every type of plant.**

וְכֵן הוּא גַּם לְנֶפֶשׁ הַמְּרַכֶּבֶת שֶׁבְּכָל חֶלְקִי הַדּוֹמֵם

**And the same is so even regarding the integrating existential force[^4] that permeates all aspects of the inanimate world.**

וְהוּא הַמָּקוֹם דְּהַנְּקֻדָּה הַפְּנִימִית דְּכָל גֶּשֶׁם שֶׁהִיא הַנּוֹשֵׂא אוֹתוֹ

**This is the central inner point of all matter that sustains its existence** (as stated in *Sefer Yetzirah* 4:4, see GR"A[^5]),

כִּי שָׁם שׁוֹכֵן הַצּוּרָה הָעִקָּרִית דְּכָל מְצִיאוּת

**for it is there that the primary *tzurah* of every entity resides,**

שכ"א הוּא כָּלוּל מֵחֹמֶר וְצוּרָה כַּנָּ"ל שַׁעַר ב'

**since every entity is comprised of matter and form, as explained above in *Shaar* 2.**

וְאוֹתוֹ הַמָּקוֹם דְּהַנְּקֻדָּה הַפְּנִימִית אֲשֶׁר בּוֹ שׁוֹכֵן הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַמְּרַכֶּבֶת

**Now within that inner point resides the integrating existential force**

שֶׁהִיא הַצּוּרָה אֲשֶׁר יֶשְׁנוֹ גַּם בַּהֲדוֹמָם הוּא בְּחַי' הַמֹּחַ שֶׁבּוֹ

**which is the *tzurah* that is present even in the inanimate world – and that is its brain.**

וְשָׁם הוּא שָׁרְשׁוֹ וְהַתְחָלָתוֹ שֶׁהוּא בְּחִי' הָרֹאשׁ שֶׁבּוֹ.

**And there is its root and its beginning, which can therefore be referred to as its 'head.'**

וע' שַׁעַר פִּרְקֵי הַצֶּלֶם רפ"ב שֶׁאָמַר שָׁם הָרַב כִּי אֵין דָּבָר שֶׁאֵין בּוֹ נרנח"י

**See the section of *Eitz Chaim* titled *Pirkei HaTzelem* beginning of chapter 2, where Rabbi Chaim Vital states that there is nothing that does not have *nrnch"y*,**

וְהוּא כְּלָלוּת הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַמְּרַכֶּבֶת שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ שֶׁיֶּשְׁנוֹ גַּם בַּהֲדוֹמָם

**which is the general integrating existential force that is also present in inanimate entities**

וְהוּא הַכֹּחַ הַמְּקַיֵּם אֶת הַרְכָּבָתוֹ שֶׁהוּא בְּחִי' נֶפֶשׁ דְּהַדּוֹמֵם

**as the sustaining force that integrates their various elements. This is the *nefesh* of the inanimate world.**

וע' בְּדִבְרֵי הָרַב בְּשַׁעַר קִצּוּר אבי"ע פ"ב וְשָׁם פ"י בדה"מ הַדּוֹמֵם ע"ש.

**See Rabbi Chaim Vital's words in the section of *Eitz Chaim* titled *Kitzur Abiy"a* chapter 2, and chapter 10 beginning with the words, 'The inanimate.'**

וְנַחֲזֹר לְהַעֲנִין כִּי הִנֵּה עכ"פ הוּא כִּי מְקוֹמָהּ שֶׁל הַנְּשָׁמָה הַפְּרָטִית

**Let's return now to our subject, which is that in any event, the proper place of the micro-level *neshama*,**

אֲשֶׁר גַּם בְּהַרוּחַ וְהַנֶּפֶשׁ הַכְּלָלִיִּים הוּא ג"כ רַק בְּהַמּוֹחַ

**even of the macro-level *ruach* and *nefesh*, is also only in the brain.**

וְעַל זֶה נִתְיַסֵּד ג' מְקוֹמוֹת בְּהַגּוּף דְּכָל פָּרְצוּף שֶׁהֵם מֹחַ לֵב כָּבֵד

**It was due to this that three places were established in the body of each *partzuf* – the brain, the heart and the liver –**

כִּי הֵם הַמִּשְׁכָּן וְהַמָּעוֹן להנר"נ הַפְּרָטִיִּים אֲשֶׁר בְּהַרוּחַ וְהַנֶּפֶשׁ הַכְּלָלִיִּים

**because they are the abode and home for micro-level *neshama*, *ruach* and *nefesh*[^6] of macro-level *ruach* and *nefesh*,**

שֶׁהֵם עִקַּר מַדְרֵגַת שֶׁל כָּל פָּרְצוּף וְכַנָּ"ל

**which are the primary levels of each *partzuf*, as mentioned above.**

וּלְכָךְ נִתְיַסֵּד להנר"נ שֶׁבָּהֶם ג' כֵּלִים מל"כ

**And therefore, for the *neshama*, *ruach* and *nefesh* three vessels were designated – the brain, the heart and the liver –**

וְהֵם מָעוֹן וּמָדוֹר לָהֶם שֶׁבָּהֶם הֵם שׁוֹכְנִים

**and they serve as their housing and a lodging in which they reside.**

אֲבָל הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית שֶׁהִיא כֻּלָּהּ אֱלָהוּת אֵין לָהּ הִשְׁתַּווּת כְּלָל עִם שׁוּם גּוּף וּכְלִי

**But the macro-level *neshama*, which is entirely Godliness, has nothing in common with any bodily entity or receptacle.**

וְלָכֵן לֹא נִתְיַסֵּד לָהּ כְּלִי כְּלָל בְּהַגּוּף לְקַבֵּל אוֹתָהּ בְּתוֹכָהּ

**And for this reason no receptacle in the body was assigned to the *neshama* in which to receive her.**

אֶלָּא שֶׁהִיא שׁוֹכֶנֶת בְּתוֹךְ הַנְּשָׁמָה דְּהָרוּחַ אֲשֶׁר בַּמֹּחַ

**Instead she resides in the *neshama* of *ruach* in the brain.**

שֶׁהַנְּשָׁמָה דְּהָרוּחַ הוּא הַכְּלִי שֶׁלָּהּ

**Thus the *neshama* of *ruach* is her – the macro-level *neshama*'s – receptacle**

וְשָׁם הִיא מִתְפַּשֵּׁט בַּהֲקָצָה הַיּוֹתֵר תַּחְתּוֹן דהנר"נ שֶׁלָּהּ

**and there she spreads forth via the lowest extremity of her micro-level *neshama*, *ruach* and *nefesh*,**

וּבְזֶה לְבַד הוּא מִתְלַבֵּשׁ בְּכָל פָּרְצוּף.

**so that only this aspect is clothed in the entire *partzuf*.**

וְהִנֵּה בְּעוֹלָם הָעֲשִׂיָּה אֲשֶׁר הַאֲלָהוֹת שֶׁבָּהּ הִיא רַק הַיְּחִידָה דְּהָרוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ וְכַנָּ"ל פ"ג

**Now in the World of *Asiyah*, where the only Godliness is in *yechida* of *ruach* and *nefesh*, as explained in chapter 3,**

הִנֵּה מִתְיַחֶדֶת הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית בְּהַאָדָם ע"י הַיְּחִידָה שֶׁבּוֹ

**the macro-level *neshama* unites with a person via their micro-level *yechida*.**

וְהַיְּחִידָה הֲרֵי הִיא מִתְפַּשֶּׁטֶת וּמִתְלַבֵּשׁ בְּהֶאָרָתָהּ בְּהֲחַיָה

**And this *yechida* spreads forth and its radiance becomes clothed in *chaya*,**

וְהֶחָיָה בְּהַנְשָׁמָה שֶׁבַּמֹּחַ (שֶׁהוּא הַנְּשָׁמָה דְּרוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ כְּנִז') וְכַנָּ"ל פ"ב.

**and *chaya* in the *neshama* in the brain (which is the *neshama* of *ruach* and *nefesh*, as mentioned) – as was explained above in chapter 2.**

וּבָעוֹלָם הַיְּצִירָה מִתְיַחֶדֶת הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית בְּכָל פָּרְצוּף

**And in the World of *Yetzirah* the macro-level *neshama* unites with the entire *partzuf***

ע"י הַיְּחִידָה וְהֶחָיָה שֶׁבְּהַרוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ

**via the *yechida* and *chaya* of *ruach* and *nefesh*.**

וּבָעוֹלָם הַבְּרִיאָה מִתְיַחֶדֶת הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית שֶׁבָּהּ

**And in the World of *Beriah* the macro-level *neshama* in it is united with it – with the World of *Beriah* –**

ע"י הַיְּחִידָה וְהֲחַיָה וְהַנְּשָׁמָה אֲשֶׁר בְּהַרוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ

**via the *yechida*, *chaya* and *neshama* of *ruach* and *nefesh*.**

וְאוֹתָן הַנְּשָׁמָה וְהַחַיָה וְהַיְּחִידָה דְּרוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ שֶׁבָּהּ

**And that *neshama*, *chaya* and *yechida* of its *ruach* and *nefesh***

הֵם הג"ר \[ג' רָאשִׁין\] דְּעוֹלַם הַבְּרִיאָה הַנִּקְרָא כִּסֵּא הַכָּבוֹד

**are the three 'heads' i.e. *binah*, *chochmah* and *keter* (from lowest to highest)[^7] of the World of *Beriah*, which is called *Kisei Hakavod* – the Throne of Glory.**

וְהִנֵּה הֵם בְּהֵיכַל הז' דְּבְרִיאָה שֶׁהוּא בְּחִי' הַמֹּחַ שֶׁבָּהּ

**In fact they are vested in the seventh Chamber of *Beriah* which is the aspect of 'brain' within it** – i.e. *keter*, *chochmah* and *binah* of *Beriah* are its 'intellectual' aspect, referred to here as *mo'ach* or brain.

וּבָהֶם מִתְפַּשֶּׁטֶת וּמִתְיַחֶדֶת הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית דְּהַבְּרִיאָה וכמ"ש בְּשַׁעַר כִּסֵּא הַכָּבוֹד פ"א.

**And through them the macro-level *neshama* spreads into and unites with *Beriah*, as it states in *Eitz Chaim*, *Shaar Kisei HaKavod* chapter 1.**

אָמְנָם מָה שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ שֶׁהַנְּשָׁמָה דְּרוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ הֵם בְּהַמּוֹחַ

**However, our statement at the beginning of this chapter that micro-level *neshama* of the macro-level *ruach* and *nefesh* are vested in the brain needs elucidation –**

הִנֵּה עִקַּר הַנְּשָׁמָה שֶׁבְּהַמּוֹחַ עַצְמוֹ הוּא רַק הַנְּשָׁמָה דְּהָרוּחַ

**in fact the primary aspect of the *neshama* vested in the brain itself is only the *neshama* of *ruach*, but not the *neshama* of *nefesh* –**

אָבֵל הַנְּשָׁמָה שֶׁבַּנֶּפֶשׁ הִנֵּה הִיא אֵינָה בְּהַמּוֹחַ עַצְמוֹ

**since the *neshama* of *nefesh* is not vested in the brain itself**

אֶלָּא רַק בְּהַקְּרוּמִין דְּהַמֹּחַ וְהַקְּרוּמִין

**but rather in the membranes, called the meninges[^8] (or *krumin* in Aramaic) surrounding the brain.**

הֵם בִּבְחִי' בָּשָׂר שֶׁהוּא מִשֹּׁרֶשׁ הַדָּם וְהַכָּבֵד מָקוֹם מִשְׁכָּן דְּהַנֶּפֶשׁ

**For our purposes these are in the same category as flesh – from the same root as the blood and the liver in which the *nefesh* physical life-force resides.**

וְאוֹתָן הַקְּרוּמִין הִנֵּה בֶּהֱיוֹת הַוָּלָד בִּבְחִי' עִבּוּר שֶׁאָז אֵין בּוֹ רַק נֶפֶשׁ לְבַד

**Regarding these *krumin*-membranes – when the fetus is in the womb, when only *nefesh* is manifested in them (this state is called *ibbur*) –**

הֲוָה אוֹתָן הַקְּרוּמִין אָז בּוֹ בִּמְצִיאוּת מֹחִין מַמָּשׁ וְהֵם הַמֹּחִין דְּעִבּוּר

**then these *krumin*-membranes have the de facto status of brain in the fetus, and they are called *mochin d'ibbur* (prenatal intellect).**[^9]

*Mochin d'ibbur* is a state of developing (but still immature) intellect.

אַךְ אַחַ"כּ בְּגַדְלוּת כְּשֶׁנִּגְמַר בּוֹ הַמֹּחִין הָאֲמִתִּי

**But later, in the state of maturity when the real brain has developed** – in our analogy, when the child has developed into an adult and has gained intellectual maturity –

הִנֵּה נַעֲשֶׂה מֵהַמֹּחִין דְּעִבּוּר לַקְּרוּמִין עַל הַמֹּחִין

**then the *mochin d'ibbur* (prenatal intellect) become the membranes surrounding the brain rather than the mere de facto status of brain in the fetus.**

וְשָׁם שׁוֹרָה בּוֹ הַנְּשָׁמָה דְּהַנֶּפֶשׁ

**There, as they surround the fully developed brain, the micro-level *neshama* of macro-level *nefesh* resides therein in the *krumin*-membranes,**

וְהוּא לָבוּשׁ עַל הַנְּשָׁמָה דְּהָרוּחַ שֶׁהוּא בְּהַמּוֹחַ מַמָּשׁ

**which function as the 'garment' or surrounding membrane enveloping the micro-level *neshama* of macro-level *ruach* which is vested in the actual brain,**

כְּמוֹ הַקְּרוּמִין שֶׁהֵם לָבוּשׁ עַל הַמֹּחִין

**just like the *krumin*-membranes that function as a 'garment' surrounding the brain.**

וְנִמְצָא כִּי עכ"פ הוּא שֶׁהֵם שְׁנֵיהֶם בְּהַמּוֹחַ שֶׁבְּרֹאשׁ זֶה בְּהַמּוֹחַ מַמָּשׁ וְזֶה בְּהַקָּרוּם.

**So it turns out in any event that both of them (*neshama* of *ruach* and *neshama* of *nefesh*) are vested in the brain in the head – this *neshama* of *ruach* in the actual brain, and that *neshama* of *nefesh* in the membrane surrounding the brain.**

וְכֵן עד"ז בְְּהָרוּחַ שֶׁבִּשְׁנֵיהֶם כִּי הֵן הָרוּחַ שֶׁבָּרוּחַ וְהֵן הָרוּחַ שֶׁבַּנֶּפֶשׁ שְׁנֵיהֶם הֵם בַּלֵּב

**And similarly regarding *ruach* in both of them – both micro-level *ruach* of macro-level *ruach* and micro-level *ruach* of macro-level *nefesh* are both vested in the heart.**

וְזֶהוּ מְצִיאַת הַשְּׁנֵי חֲלָלִים שֶׁבַּלֵּב בְּאֶחָד רוּחַ וְאֶחָד דָּם

**And this is the parallel to the existence of two chambers in the heart – in one *ruach* and in the other blood i.e. the *nefesh*, "for the blood is the *nefesh*" (Deut. 12:23) – as the Baal haLeshem will explain shortly –**

הַיָּמִין רוּחַ וְהַשְּׂמֹאל דָּם

**in the right, *ruach*; in the left, blood.**[^10]

וְהֵם הָרוּחַ שֶׁבְּרוּחַ וְהָרוּחַ שֶׁבַּנֶּפֶשׁ

**When we said that *ruach* and *nefesh* are vested in the heart, these are *ruach* of *ruach*, and *ruach* of *nefesh*.**

וּמָה שֶׁהוּא דָּם הוּא מִשֹּׁרֶשׁ הַכָּבֵד אֲשֶׁר בּוֹ שֶׁשָּׁם הוּא עִקַּר מְצִיאוּתוֹ וְגִלּוּיוֹ

**The reason that *nefesh* is referred to as 'blood' is because it is rooted in the liver, for there is its primary existence and where it is primarily revealed,**

כִּי עִקַּר מְצִיאוּתוֹ וְגִלּוּיוֹ דְּהַנֶּפֶשׁ הוּא רַק בַּכָּבֵד.

**since the primary aspect of the existence and revelation of the *nefesh* is only in the liver.**[^11]

וְכֵן הוּא עד"ז בְּהַנֶפֶשׁ שֶׁבִּשְׁנֵיהֶם כִּי הֵן הַנֶּפֶשׁ שֶׁבְּרוּחַ וְהֵן הַנֶּפֶשׁ דְּהַנֶּפֶשׁ

**The same concept applies to the *nefesh* in both of them, since *nefesh* of *ruach* and *nefesh* of *nefesh***

שְׁנֵיהֶם הֵם בְּהַכָבֵד הַנֶּפֶשׁ דְּהָרוּחַ מִבִּפְנִים וְהַנֶּפֶשׁ דְּהַנֶּפֶשׁ מִבַּחוּץ

**are both vested in the liver – *nefesh* of *ruach* inwardly, and *nefesh* of *nefesh* outwardly.**

'Inwardly' and 'outwardly' here possibly refer to two of the general functions of the liver – metabolism and synthesis of nutrients (inward), and purifying the blood of toxins (outward).

וְהוּא אֲשֶׁר מִשְׁתַּתֵּף וּמִתְעָרֵב עִם הַדָּם וְהַבָּשָׂר

**It is the liver that interfaces and intermingles with the blood and flesh,**

וכמ"ש (דברים יב, כג) כִּי הַדָּם הוּא הַנָּפֶשׁ וְלֹא תֹאכַל הַנֶּפֶשׁ עִם הַבָּשָׂר

**as it is written (Deut. 12:23) "For the blood is the life *nefesh* and you shall not eat the life *nefesh* with the meat."**

וְהוּא הַנֶּפֶשׁ דְּנֶפֶשׁ אֲשֶׁר הוּא מִשְׁתַּתֵּף וּמִתְעָרֵב עִם הַדָּם וְהַבָּשָׂר

**This is the level of *nefesh* of *nefesh* which interfaces and intermingles with the blood and flesh.**

אֲבָל הַנֶּפֶשׁ דְּהָרוּחַ הוּא מִפְנִים אֵלָיו וּשְׁנֵיהֶם הֵם בַּכָּבֵד

**But *nefesh* of *ruach* is internal to that, although both of them are in the liver.**

וְזֶהוּ הָעִנְיָן מָה שֶׁתָּלוּי טְרֵיפַת הַכָּבֵד בְּשִׁעוּר ב' זֵיתִים

**This is the concept that the kosher or non-kosher status of a liver is determined by the measure of two olives'-worth:**

כַּזַּיִת בִּמְקוֹם מָרָה וְשָׁם הוּא הַנֶּפֶשׁ שֶׁבַּנֶּפֶשׁ

**if there is an olive's-worth in the area of the gall-bladder where the *nefesh* of *nefesh* is situated,**

וּכְזֵית בִּמְקוֹם חַיּוּתוֹ שֶׁשָּׁם הוּא הַנֶּפֶשׁ דְּהָרוּחַ שֶׁבַּנֶּפֶשׁ

**and an olive's-worth in the place of its life i.e. in the place from which it receives its nourishment, so that the animal can survive, which is the *nefesh* of *ruach* of *nefesh*, then it is kosher.**[^12]

וְנִקְרָא הוּא מָקוֹם חַיּוּתוֹ כִּי הָרוּחַ הוּא עִקַּר הַחַיִּים.

**And it is called 'the place of its life' since *ruach* is the primary aspect of life.**

וַהֲרֵי נִתְבָּאֵר לָנוּ מכ"ז כִּי הֵן הנר"נ דְּהָרוּחַ וְהֵן הנר"נ דְּהַנֶּפֶשׁ

**All the above clarifies for us that the *neshama*, *ruach* and *nefesh* of *ruach* as well as the *neshama*, *ruach* and *nefesh* of *nefesh* –**

שְׁנֵיהֶם הֵם בְּמֹחַ לֵב כָּבֵד שֶׁהֵם רֹאשׁ גְּוִיָהּ בֶּטֶן

**both of them are vested in the brain, heart and liver, which are housed in the head, torso and the abdominal section.**

וְכֵן הֵם שְׁנֵיהֶם בְּכָל חֶלְקִי פְּנִימִי תִּיכוֹן חִיצוֹן דְּהַגּוּף שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ לְעֵיל פ"א

**Both of them are also vested in all parts of the body – inner, middle and outer, as described in chapter 1 above.**

וַהֲרֵי הֵם מַתְחִילִים שְׁנֵיהֶם מִן הַמֹּחַ וְכֵן הֵם מִתְפַּשְּׁטִים שְׁנֵיהֶם עַד סוֹף הַגּוּף:

**And both of them begin in the brain, and both of them spread into the body all the way down to the final i.e. the lowest and least important aspects of the body.**
