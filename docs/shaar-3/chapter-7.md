***Shaar* 3 Chapter 7**

וְהִנֵּה הֲגַם שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ לְעֵיל פ " ג שֶׁהַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית הִיא לְעוֹלָם אֱלָהוּת גָּמוּר

**Now even though we stated above chapter 3 that the macro-level neshama is always completely Godly,**

גַּם בְּעוֹלַם הָעֲשִׂיָּה עכ " ז הִנֵּה פָּשׁוּט הַדָּבָר שֶׁאֵין כָּל הָעוֹלָמוֹת שָׁוִים בְּקַבָּלָתָהּ

**even in the World of Asiyah, nevertheless, it is a simple fact that not all the worlds are equal in terms of how they receive it :**

וְאֵינוֹ דּוֹמֶה אֵיכוּת אוֹר הַנְּשָׁמָה שֶׁבְּהַבְרִיאָהּ לְאֵיכוּת אוֹר הַנְּשָׁמָה שֶׁבַּהִיצִירָה

**the quality of the light of the neshama in Beriah is not the same as the quality of the light of the neshama in Yetzirah**

The light of the neshama in Beriah is obviously much greater than the light of the neshama in Yetzirah.

וְכֵן אֵינוֹ דּוֹמֶה אֵיכוּת אוֹר הַנְּשָׁמָה דְּהַיְּצִירָה לְאֵיכוּת אוֹר הַנְּשָׁמָה דְּהָעֲשָׂיָה

**Similarly, the quality of the light of the neshama in Yetzirah is not the same as the quality of the light of the neshama in Asiyah.**

The light of the neshama in Yetzirah is obviously much greater than the light of the neshama in Asiyah.

וְהֶעָנִין בַּבֵּאוּר הוּא כִּי הִנֵּה כָּל הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית שֶׁבְּכָל הָעוֹלָמוֹת בי " ע

**To explain this matter further: the macro-level neshama in each of the Worlds of BiY”A**

הִנֵּה הִיא נִמְשֶׁכֶת מֵהַמַּלְכוּת דְּהָאֲצִילוּת וּמִבְחִי ' הַגּוּף וְכֵלִים שֶׁבָּהּ

**is drawn from malchut of Atzilut and from the aspect of ‘body’ and ‘vessels’ therein , as the Baal HaLeshem goes on to explain.**

כִּי בְּכָל עוֹלָם יֵשׁ אוֹר וְכֵלִים שֶׁהוּא צוּרָה וָחֹמֶר

**For in every World there are lights and ‘vessels,’ which are also known in Jewish thought 2 as tzurah and chomer form and substance.**

וּמֵהַכֵּלִים דְּעוֹלָם הָעֶלְיוֹן שֶׁהֵם הַחֹמֶר שֶׁבּוֹ

**From the ‘vessels’ of the higher world, which is the chomer therein,**

נַעֲשָׂה הַצּוּרָה וְהָאוֹר שֶׁהוּא הַנְּשָׁמָה דְּעוֹלָם הַתַּחְתּוֹן כַּנָּ " ל שע " ב פֵּ " א

**comes the tzurah , which is the soul of the lower world, as explained above, 2:1.**

וּכְבָר אָמַרְנוּ לְעֵיל פֵּ " א כִּי כָּל גּוּף הִנֵּה הוּא נִבְחָן בַּג ' חֲלֻקּוֹת שֶׁסִּימָנָם פַתַ " ח

**We already explained above, in chapter 1, that every ‘body’ can be subdivided into three aspects, abbreviated as PTa”CH the initials of pnimi, tichon, chiztoni – the inner, in-between and outer layers.**

וְהִנֵּה הֲוָה הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית שֶׁבְּעוֹלַם הַבְּרִיאָה מִבְּחִי ' כְּלִי הַפְּנִימִי שֶׁל הַמַּלְכוּת דַּאֲצִילוּת

**Now the macro-level neshama of Beriah is from the aspect of the inner layer of the vessel of malchut of Atzilut;**

וְהַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית שֶׁבְּעוֹלַם הַיְּצִירָה הִיא מִבְּחִי ' כְּלִי הָאֶמְצָעִי דְּהַמַּלְכוּת דַּאֲצִילוּת

**and the macro-level neshama of Yetzirah is from the aspect of the in-between layer of the vessel of malchut of Atzilut;**

וְהַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית דְּעוֹלָם הָעֲשִׂיָּה הִיא מִבְּחִי ' כְּלִי הַחִיצוֹן דְּהַמַּלְכוּת דַּאֲצִילוּת .

**and the macro-level neshama of Asiyah is from the aspect of the outer layer of the vessel of malchut of Atzilut .**

אָמְנָם הֲרֵי אָמַרְנוּ כִּי הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית הִנֵּה הִיא כְּלוּלָה ג " כ מִכָּל הָהּ ' בְּחִי ' דנרנח " י

**However, we also said that the macro-level neshama is comprised of all five aspects of nrnch”y, 3**

וְהִנֵּה אוֹתָן הנרנח " י שֶׁבָּהּ הִנֵּה הֵם נִמְשְׁכוּ ג " כ מִכָּל הָהּ ' פַּרְצוּפִי הָאֲצִילוּת כֻּלָּם

**and those nrnch”y in it in the macro-level neshama are also drawn from all the five partzufim of Atzilut,**

מִבְּחַי ' הג ' חֶלְקֵי הַכֵּלִים שבכ " א מֵהֶם

**from the three layers of the vessels – the inner layer, the in-between layer, and the outer layer of each of them,**

כִּי יֶשְׁנָם בְּחִי ' כָּל הג ' חֶלְקֵי כֵּלִים שֶׁסִּימָנָם פַתַ " ח בְּכָל פַּרְצוּף וּפָרְצוּף

**for there are aspects of all three aspects of the vessels, the initials of which are pt”ch – pnimi (inner), tichon (in-between), chitzon (outer) – in each and every partzuf .**

וַהֲוָה הנרנח " י שֶׁבְּהַנְשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית דְּהַבְּרִיאָה

**So that the nrnch”y of the macro-level neshama of Beriah**

מֵהָהּ ' כֵּלִים הַפְּנִימִים דְּהָה ' פַּרְצוּפִי הָאֲצִילוּת

**derive from the inner ( pnimi ) layer of the vessels of the five partzufim of Atzilut i.e. Arich Anpin, Abba, Imma, Ze’ir Anpin, Malchut.**

Therefore, each of the five levels of nrnch”y of the macro-level neshama of Beriah has a different origin: its yechida derives from the inner layer of the vessels of Arich Anpin ; its chaya derives from the inner layer of the vessels of Abba ; its neshama derives from the inner layer of the vessels of Imma; its ruach derives from the inner layer of the vessels of Ze’ir Anpin ; its nefesh from the inner layer of the vessels of Malchut.

והנרנח " י שֶׁבְּהַנְשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית דְּהַיְּצִירָה מֵהָהּ ' כֵּלִים הָאֶמְצָעִיִּים דְּהָה ' פַּרְצוּפִי הָאָצִי '

**Similarly, the nrnch”y of the macro-level neshama of Yetzirah derive from the in-between ( tichon ) layer of the vessels of the five partzufim of Atzilut i.e. Arich Anpin, Abba, Imma, Ze’ir Anpin, Malchut.**

**Therefore, each of the five levels of nrnch”y of the macro-level neshama of Yetzira has a different origin: its yechida derives from the in-between layer of the vessels of Arich Anpin ; its chaya derives from the in-between layer of the vessels of Abba ; its neshama derives from the in-between layer of the vessels of Imma; its ruach derives from the in-between layer of the vessels of Ze’ir Anpin ; its nefesh from the in-between layer of the vessels of Malchut.**

והנרנח " י שֶׁבְּהַנְשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית דְּהָעֲשָׂיָה מֵהָהּ ' כֵּלִים הַחִיצוֹנִיִּים דְּהָה ' פַּרְצוּפִי הָאֲצִילוּת

**And so too as regards the nrnch”y of the macro-level neshama of Asiyah derive from the outer ( chitzon ) layer of the vessels of the five partzufim of Atzilut i.e. Arich Anpin, Abba, Imma, Ze’ir Anpin, Malchut.**

Therefore, each of the five levels of nrnch”y of the macro-level neshama of Asiyah has a different origin: its yechida derives from the outer layer of the vessels of Arich Anpin ; its chaya derives from the outer layer of the vessels of Abba ; its neshama derives from the outer layer of the vessels of Imma; its ruach derives from the outer layer of the vessels of Ze’ir Anpin ; its nefesh from the outer layer of the vessels of Malchut.

כִּי הִנֵּה כָּל הָה ' פַּרְצוּפִי הָאֲצִילוּת הִנֵּה הֵם מִתְלַבְּשִׁים זב " ז

**Now all five of the partzufim of Atzilut are clothed one within the other,**

וְהַמֲלְכוּת הִיא מִבַּחוּץ לְכֻלָּם וְכוֹלֵל אֶת כֻּלָּם

**while malchut is external to all of them and thus includes all of them.**

וְכֵן הוּא עד " ז בְּעִנְיַן הַכֵּלִים שֶׁלָּהֶם הַמִּתְפַּשְּׁטִים בְּהֶאָרָתָם

**And similarly as regards their vessels, the radiance of which spreads forth**

לְהֵעָשׂוֹת לַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית בְּהָעוֹלָמוֹת בי " ע הִנֵּה הוּא ג " כ עד " ז

**to become the macro- neshama of the Worlds of BiY”A, also in a similar fashion.**

כִּי מִתְלַבְּשִׁים כָּל בְּחִי ' הֶאָרַת כְּלִי הַפְּנִימִים

**So that what becomes clothed in the lower worlds of BiY”A is all aspects of the radiance of the inner layer of the vessels**

דְּכָל הָהּ ' פַּרְצוּפִי הָאֲצִילוּת זֶה בְּזֶה

**of all five partzufim of Atzilut, one within the other,**

וּמִתְפַּשֵּׁט וְנִמְשָׁךְ בְּעוֹלָם הַבְּרִיאָה

**and they spread forth and are drawn down into the World of Beriah**

וְנַעֲשָׂה מֵהֶם כֻּלָּם הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית דְּהַבְּרִיאָה כְּלוּלָה מנרנח " י

**and from all of them is fashioned the macro- neshama of Beriah, comprising nrnch”y .**

וְכֵן מִתְלַבְּשִׁים כָּל בְּחִי ' הֶאָרַת כְּלִי הָאֶמְצָעִיִּים דְּכָל הָהּ ' פַּרְצוּפִי הָאָצִי ' זב " ז

**Similarly, the radiances of the in-between layer of the vessels of all five partzufim of Atzilut are clothed one within the other,**

וּמִתְפַּשֵּׁט וְנִמְשַׁךְ בְּעוֹלַם הַיְּצִירָה

**and they spread forth and are drawn down into the World of Yetzirah**

וְנַעֲשָׂה מֵהֶם כֻּלָּם הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית דְּהַיְּצִירָה כְּלוּלָה מנרנח " י

**and from all of them is fashioned the macro- neshama of Yetzirah, comprising nrnch”y .**

וְכֵן מִתְלַבְּשִׁים כָּל בְּחִי ' הֶאָרַת כְּלִי הַחִיצוֹנִים דְּכָל הָהּ ' פַּרְצוּפִי הָאֲצִילוּת זב " ז

**Similarly, the radiances of the outer layer of the vessels of all five partzufim of Atzilut are clothed one within the other,**

וּמִתְפַּשְּׁטִים וְנִמְשָׁכִים בְּעוֹלַם הָעֲשִׂיָּה

**and they spread forth and are drawn down into the World of Asiyah**

וְנַעֲשָׂה מֵהֶם כֻּלָּם הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית דְּהָעֲשָׂיָה כְּלוּלָה מנרנח " י :

**and from all of them is fashioned the macro- neshama of Asiyah, comprising nrnch”y .**

אָמְנָם הוּא כִּי הֲגַם שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ שֶׁהַצּוּרָה דְּכַל עוֹלָם תַּחְתּוֹן

**Nevertheless, although we said that the tzurah 4 of every lower world**

הִנֵּה הִיא נַעֲשֶׂה מֵהַחֹמֶר דְּעוֹלָם הָעֶלְיוֹן שֶׁהוּא הַגּוּף וְכֵלִים שֶׁלּוֹ

**is fashioned from the material aspect of the upper world – it’s ‘body’ and ‘vessels’ –**

ועי " ז אָמַרְנוּ כִּי נַעֲשֶׂה מֵהַג ' חֶלְקֵי כֵּלִים דְּכָל פַּרְצוּפִי הָאֲצִילוּת

**and consequently we deduced that from the three aspects of the vessels of all of the partzufim visages of Atzilut i.e. the inner, in-between and outer aspects is fashioned**

הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית דְּכַל הָעוֹלָמוֹת בי " ע וּכְנָז '

**the macro-level neshama of each of the worlds of BiY”A, as mentioned.**

הִנֵּה הָאֱמֶת הוּא כִּי נִמְשָׁךְ עִם הַכֵּלִים דְּכָל עֶלְיוֹן מֵהָאוֹר וְהַפְּנִימִיּוֹת שֶׁבּוֹ ג " כ

**Now the truth is that with the vessels of all higher levels is drawn down the light and the inner aspects thereof as well.**

ועי " ז הִנֵּה נַעֲשֶׂה כָּל תַּחְתּוֹן כָּלוּל מִכָּל הַבְּחִי ' כֻּלָּם שֶׁבְּכָל הָעֶלְיוֹנִים כֻּלָּם

**And thereby the lower level comprises all of the aspects of all of the upper levels,**

וכמ " ש בְּסֵדֶר אבי " ע פ ' א ' ב ' ובכ " מ וְכֵן הוּא גַּם בְּעִנְיָנֵינוּ

**as is written in Eitz Chaim in the section titled “The Order of ABiY”A” chaps 1 & 2 and in several places. And so to as regards this matter:**

כִּי הִנֵּה נִמְשָׁךְ עִם הג ' חֶלְקֵי כֵּלִים דכ " א מֵהָהּ ' פַּרְצוּפִי הָאֲצִי '

**For with the three aspects of the vessels of each of them, is drawn forth from the 5 partzufim of Atzilut – Atik, Arich, Abba, Imma, Zeir Anpin and Malchut or Nukbah –**

הַמִּתְפַּשְּׁטִים לִהְיוֹת לִנְשָׁמָה כְּלָלִית בְּהָעוֹלָמוֹת בי " ע

**that spread forth to become the macro -neshama of the Worlds of BiY”A, ( briah, yetsira, asiya)**

הִנֵּה נִמְשָׁךְ עִמָּהֶם מֵהָאוֹרוֹת דְּכָל פָּרְצוּף וּפָרְצוּף ג " כ

**together with them are also drawn down from the lights from each of the partzufim .**

וְנִמְשַׁךְ מִכָּל חֶלְקֵי הנרנח " י דְּכָל פָּרְצוּף וּפָרְצוּף

**so that there is drawn down from all the parts of nrnch”y of each partzuf**

אֵיזֶה הֶאָרָה מֵהֶם עִם בְּחַי ' הַכְּלִי שֶׁמִּתְפַּשֵּׁט מִמֶּנּוּ

**some aspect of radiance from them, together with the aspect of the vessel that spreads out from it.**

וְנַעֲשֶׂה מִזֶּה הַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית דְּהָעוֹלָמוֹת בי " ע

**And this becomes the level of macro- neshama of the Worlds of BiY”A**

כָּלוּל וּמְלֻבָּשׁ מִכָּל הָעֶלְיוֹנִים כֻּלָּם אֲשֶׁר לְעֵלָּא לְעֵלָּא

**which comprises and is clothed within it all aspects of the higher worlds to the very highest levels**

[ וְזֶהוּ עֹמֶק כַּוָּנַת דִּבְרִי הָרַב בְּכָל עִנְיַן הַהִתְלַבְּשׁוּת אֲשֶׁר בְּשַׁעַר עֲגֻלִּים וְיֹשֶׁר עֲנַף דּ ' ע " ש בִּדְבָרֵינוּ בַּאֲרוּכָה ]

**[And this is indeed the intention of the Rabbi Chaim Vital regarding the entire matter of hitlavshut – ‘enclothing’ or ‘insertion’ of one partzuf in another – as discussed in Eitz Chaim, Sha’ar Iggulim v’Yosher , anaf 4. See our commentary there at length].**

וְאוֹתוֹ הָאוֹר הוּא אוֹרוֹ ית " ש מַמָּשׁ הַשּׁוֹכֵן בְּתוֹךְ הַפְּנִימִית דְּעוֹלָמוֹת בי " ע

**This light is in fact His light, may He be blessed, that rests within the inner dimension of the Worlds of BiY”A**

בִּבְחִי ' נְשָׁמָה לַנְּשָׁמָה ( וכמ " ש בָּרַעַ " מ פ ' בְּהַר ק " ט ב ')

**– as the neshama of the neshama (as it states in Zohar vol . III in Raya Mehemna, Behar p. 109b). 5**

וּמִזֶּה הָאוֹר מִתְפַּשֵּׁט וְנִמְשָׁךְ כָּל הַתּוֹרָה כֻּלָּהּ

**And from this light the entire Torah emerges and is drawn down,**

אֲשֶׁר בָּהּ נִבְרָא וְנוֹצַר וְנַעֲשֶׂה כָּל הָעוֹלָמוֹת בי " ע

**and with it with the Torah all the worlds of Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah were created (Beriah) , formed (Yetzirah) and made (Asiyah), 6**

וְכֵן מִתְקַיְּמִים וּמִתְנַהֲגִים רַק עַל יָדָהּ

**and they endure and are directed exclusively by her by the Torah.**

וְהוּא הַנְּשָׁמָה לִנְשָׁמָה דְּאוֹרָיְתָא שֶׁאָמְרוּ בזוה " ק בְּהַעֲלֹתְךָ קנ " ב א '

**The aspect of Torah that achieves all of this is the soul of the soul of Torah i.e. the inner dimension of Torah, Kabbalah, as they state in Zohar Beha’alotcha 152a.**

וְכֵן מִזֶּה הָאוֹר הוּא שֹׁרֶשׁ נִשְׁמַת כְּלַל יִשְׂרָאֵל וכמ " ש באד " ר קמ " א ע " ב

**Furthermore, from very light is the root of the souls of all Israel, as the Zohar III states in the sub-section called Idra Rabba, p. 141b:**

וְיִפַח בְּאַפָּיו נִשְׁמַת חַיִּים כּוּ ' בְּגִין לְאִשְׁתַּלְּפָא וּלְעָיְילָא בֵּיהּ

**“And He blew into his nostrils the breath of life...” (Genesis 2:7) in order to draw down from above, from Arich Anpin 7 and implant in him in Adam (and in man in general, by extension,)**

סָתִים דִּסְתִּימָא עִלָּאָה עַד סוֹפָא דְּכָל סְתִימִין ,

**the most hidden of hidden levels above, to the very limit of all hidden things all the way up until Adam Kadmon , the highest ‘world’ or plane of reality, if a person is worthy of this by virtue of his deeds 8**

נִשְׁמְתָא דְּכָל חַיֵּי דְּעֵילָּא וְתַתָּא תַּלְיָין מֵהַהִיא נִשְׁמְתָא , וּמִתְקַיְימֵי בָּהּ כּוּ ' ע " ש

**And Adam Kadmon is the ultimate soul of all supernal life above, and it is also the life-force below of the lower worlds BiY”A all of which depend on that soul, and they endure thereby. 9**

וּלְכֵן נֶאֱמַר בָּנִים אַתֶּם לי " א [ לַה ' אֱלֹקֵיכֶם ] וכמ " ש בפ ' וַיְחִי רמ " ה ופ ' שָׁלַח קנ " ט ב '

**And therefore it states in the verse (Deuteronomy 14:1) “You are children of the Lord your God,” as stated in Zohar I, Vayechi p. 245, and in Zohar III, Shelach p. 159b.**

**The highest, or most exalted, of the five worlds is called Adam Kadmon . Adam means “in the likeness of” or “in the image of”, from the Hebrew word domeh . Kadmon means “primordial”, or “primary”, from the Hebrew root kadam . So, Adam Kadmon is the primordial world which is "in the likeness of" the Infinite Light which preceded it and which was concealed in the process of creation. This means that even though Adam Kadmon is a world, meaning that it comes into being through the concealment of the Infinite Light, it is such an elevated plane of reality that it is “in the likeness of” the Infinite Light (which, by nature, “precedes” the world of Adam Kadmon ). In this sense, souls that emerge from Adam Kadmon can be described as God’s children.**

אֲבָל שְׁאָר כָּל הַמְּצִיאוּת כֻּלָּם נִמְשַׁךְ הִתְהַוּוּתָם רַק מִכֹּחַ הַכֵּלִים

**But all the rest of existence comes into being and descends from the power of the vessels rather than the light.**

שֶׁהֵם הַחִיצוֹנִית דְּהָאֲצִילוֹת וְנֶעֱלַם בָּהֶם הָאוֹר הַפְּנִימִי הַנִּזְכָּר מְאֹד מְאֹד

**They the vessels are the outer layer of Atzilut and the inner light mentioned above is very, very concealed within them –**

כִּי הוּא לָהֶם רַק לִבְחִי ' שֹׁרֶשׁ הַשָּׁרָשִׁים

**it is merely the source of their source.**

וְאַפִילוּ הַמַּלְאָכִים שֶׁבִּיצִירָה וְהַשְּׂרָפִים שֶׁבַּבְּרִיאָה

**Even the angels in Yetzirah and the seraphs in Beriah**

Different classes of angels are found in each of the worlds – ofanim in Asiyah, melachim in Yetzirah, seraphim in Beriah.

הֵם ג " כ רַק מֵהַחִיצוֹנִיוּת לְבַד וּמִזֶּה נִמְשַׁךְ מְצִיאוּתָם וְכָל שִׁפְעֲם תָּמִיד

**are also only from the outer layer exclusively. It is from there that their existence and constant life-flow is drawn.**

וְלָכֵן אָמְרוּ רז " ל בִּדְבָרִים רַבָּה פֵּ " א סִימַן י " ב וְכֵן הוּא בַּיְּרוּשַׁלְמִי שַׁבָּת ספ " ו

**And therefore our Sages state in Devarim Rabba 1:12 and in Talmud Yerushalmi Shabbat end of chapter 6 that**

עֲתִיד הקב " ה לַעֲשׂוֹת מְחִיצָתָן שֶׁל צַדִּיקִים לִפְנִים מְחִיצָתָן שֶׁל מַלְאָכִי הַשָּׁרָת

**in the future the Holy One, blessed be He, will construct a section literally a ‘partition’ for the righteous closer in to the Divine presence 10 than the area designated for the ministering angels.**

וּמַלְאֲכֵי הַשָּׁרָת שׁוֹאֲלִין אוֹתָן וְאָמְרַן לָהֶן מָה פָּעַל אֵל

**And the ministering angels ask them the righteous saying to them, (Numbers 23:23) “What has God done?” Why have the righteous been given a place even closer to the divine Presence than the ministering angels?**

The context of this verse: God redeemed Israel from Egypt, the epicenter of witchcraft and sorcery, and gave them the Torah, without them using magical means to foretell the future or affect events (see Nedarim 32a; Rashi on the verse). The question is thus in what merit were the righteous are given such a lofty position, even higher than the ministering angels?

וְהוּא מִשּׁוּם מָה שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ כִּי נִשְׁמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל הוּא מְאוֹר הַפְּנִימִי

**The answer is as we said above – that the souls of Israel are from the inner light,**

וּמַלְאֲכֵי הַשָּׁרָת הֵם רַק מֵהַחִיצוֹנִית

**while the ministering angels are only from the outer aspects.**

וַהֲרֵי נִמְצָא כִּי הֲגַם שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ שֶׁהַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית דְּעוֹלָם הָעֲשִׂיָּה

**So it turns out that even though we stated that the macro-level neshama of the World of Asiyah**

הִיא לַמַּטֶּה בְּמַדְרֵגָה הַרְבֵּה מֵהַנְּשָׁמָה הַכְּלָלִית דְּהַיְּצִירָה וְהַבְּרִיאָה

**is on a much lower level than the macro-level neshama of Yetzirah and Beriah,**

אַךְ שֹׁרֶשׁ נִשְׁמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל הִיא עוֹלָה עַל כֻּלָּם כִּי הוּא מְאוֹר הַפְּנִימִי

**nevertheless, the root of the Israelite soul transcends them all, because it is from the inner light,**

קַו הָאֶמְצָעִי הַמַּבְרִחַ מִן הַקָּצֶה אֶל הַקָּצֶה

**the middle column that extends from end to end,**

וּכְנוֹדָע דִּנְשַׁמַּת יִשְׂרָאֵל הוּא חֵלֶק אֱלֹהַּ מִמַּעַל וכמ " ש כִּי חֵלֶק ה ' עַמּוֹ

**and as is known the Israelite soul is part of God above, as the verse states, “For God’s portion is His people” (Deuteronomy 32:9).**

וְאָמְרוּ בזוה " ק אַחֲרֵי מוֹת ס " ב ב ' דְּהָא אִנּוּן אִתְאַחְדָן לְעֵלָּא לְעֵלָּא

**Regarding this, verse the holy Zohar ( Acharei Mot 62b) states: They are one with the very highest of levels**

וּבְפ ' בְּרֵאשִׁית כ " ה א ' אִלֵין יִשְׂרָאֵל דְּנִשְׁמְתַיְהוּ תַּלְיָן מֵהַהוּא אֲתָר

**and in parshat Bereishit 25a: “… those Israelites whose souls derive from that place**

דְּאַתָּמָר בֵּיהּ בָּרוּךְ כְּבוֹד ה ' מְקוֹמוֹ

**regarding which it states (Ezekiel 3:12) ‘Blessed is the glory of God from His place’.”**

The Zohar there discusses several general types and levels of soul from the highest levels to the lowest.

אָמְנָם בִּפְרָטוֹת הַנְּשָׁמָה דכ " א יֵשׁ מַדְרֵגוֹת רַבּוֹת אֵין חֵקֶר

**All of the above is in a general sense; however, in terms of the individual soul of each person there are innumerable levels,**

וכמ " ש בְּפ ' מִשְׁפָּטִים צ " ד ב ' וְכֵן בָּרַעַ " מ פִּנְחָס רכ " ד סע " ב

**as the Zohar states in parshat Mishpatim 94b, and in Raya Mehemna, Pinchas 224b.**

אַךְ עכ " פ כִּי כֻּלָּם מֻשְׁרָשִׁים וַאֲחִדֵּן לְעֵלָּא לְעֵלָּא וּכְנִז '

**Nevertheless, all of them are rooted in and united with the loftiest of levels, as mentioned.**

ועי " ז אֶפְשָׁר לכ " א מִיִּשְׂרָאֵל לְהִתְקַדֵּשׁ בִּקְדֻשָּׁה שֶׁל מַעֲלָה

**Consequently, it is possible for each Jewish person to sanctify themselves with supernal sanctity,**

וכמ " ש וְאַתֶּם הַדְּבָקִים בי " א [ בַּה ' אֱלֹקֵיכֶם ] כוּ ':

**as is written (Deuternonomy 4:4) , “And you who cleave the Lord your God are all alive today!”**
