**שער ד. *פרק א׳***

**Shaar 4 Chapter 1**

הִנֵּה אָמַרְנוּ לְעֵיל שַׁעַר ב' שֶׁהַצּוּרָה שֶׁהִיא הַנְּשָׁמָה 

**We stated above, Shaar 2** chapter 1 **that the *tzurah*, which is the *neshama*,**

הִנֵּה הִיא כֹּחַ הַחֹמֶר וְהַגּוּף וְהִיא סִבָתוֹ בְּכָל עִנְיָנָיו

**is the power** invested in **the material** world **and the body, and is the cause of all aspects** of each of them**.**

וא"כ הֲרֵי פָּשׁוּט הוּא כִּי יֶשְׁנוֹ בְּהַצּוּרָה כָּל תְּכוּנַת וְהוֹרָאַת הַגּוּף כֻּלּוֹ.

**This being so, it is obvious that all of the qualities and modes of operation relating to the body are in the *tzurah*.**

Since the *tzurah* is the source of the qualities and modes of operation of the body, it follows that these qualities and modes of operation must in some way be in the *tzurah*, albeit in a much more elevated manner. An analogy can perhaps be drawn from the way in which an idea is present in thought and how it emerges in speech. The idea expressed in speech is merely an extension of the idea as it is in thought, albeit a mere shadow of the thought, as the Baal HaLeshem will explain.  

וְאַדְּרַבָּה הוּא שֶׁעִקַּר תְּכוּנוֹתָיו דְּהַגּוּף הִנֵּה הוּא רַק מֵהַצּוּרָה שֶׁבּוֹ, שֶׁהַצּוּרָה הִיא הָאָדָם הָעִקָּרִי.

**And to the contrary, the primary qualities of the body are only due to the *tzurah*** imbedded **within it, since the *tzurah* is the primary aspect of a person,**

וְהַגּוּף הוּא רַק הַכְּלִי שֶׁלָּהּ שֶׁעַל יָדוֹ מוֹצִיא הַצּוּרָה אֶת כָּל תְּכוּנוֹתֶיהָ וְכָחוֹתֶיהָ מִן הַכֹּחַ אֶל הַפּוֹעֵל

**whereas the body is merely its vessel by which the *tzurah* manifests all of its qualities and powers from** a state of **potential to actuality. ** 

אֲבָל עִקַּר פַּרְצוּף הָאָדָם הוּא רַק הַצּוּרָה.

**So the primary aspect of man is only the *tzurah*,** not the body. 

אָמְנָם עכ"ז הִנֵּה בְּהַצּוּרָה לְבַד עוֹמֵד שָׁם כָּל עִנְּיָנֵי הַגּוּף רַק בְּכֹחַ לְבַד

**Nevertheless, in the *tzurah* itself, all aspects** relating to the physical **body are only in potential,**    

וְכָל הַפְּעֻלָּה הֲרֵי הוּא רַק ע"י הַגּוּף 

**and any action is exclusively via the body.**  

כִּי כַּאֲשֶׁר חָסֵר אֵיזֶה אֵבֶר מֵהַגּוּף הֲרֵי אֵינוֹ פּוֹעֵל בָּהּ הַצּוּרָה מְאוּמָה.

And the proof of this is **that when a limb is missing from the body, the** aspect of the ***tzurah*** that corresponds to that limb **cannot be expressed at all.**  

וְנִמְצָא כִּי הַכֹּחַ וְהַפּוֹעֵל הִנֵּה הוּא הַצּוּרָה.

**It turns out that** both **potential and actual are** embedded in **the *tzurah,*** 

אֲבָל הַפְּעֻלָּה וְהַמַּעֲשֶׂה הִנֵּה הוּא בְּהַגּוּף.

**whereas the action and the deed are via the body** exclusively, 

וְלֹא נַעֲשָׂה שׁוּם דָּבָר אֶלָּא ע"י שְׁנֵיהֶם דַּוְקָא.

so that **nothing is accompllished without both of them.**  

וְהִנֵּה אָמַרְנוּ כִּי חֶלְקֵי הַצּוּרָה הֵם ה' שֶׁהֵם נרנח"י. 

**Now we said** above[^1] **that there are five parts to the *tzurah*; they are *nrnch"y*** – *nefesh, ruach, neshama, chaya* and *yechida*.

וְאֵלוּ הָה' הֵם נַעֲשֶׂה כ"א לְבוּשׁ לַחֲבֵרוֹ 

**And each one of these five becomes a 'garment' for its companion**, i.e. for the level that transcends it.

Whereas the concept of *hishtalshelut* – the chaining down of reality from the very highest levels to the most mundane – is a fundamental concept in the writings and systems of both the Ramak and the Arizal, the concept of 'enclothing' is far more prominent in the writings and system of the Arizal. It explains how higher spiritual levels impact lower ones by becomes 'clothed' in them, and how lower ones in turn become the means by which higher levels are manifested, albeit in a concealed manner. In other words, the higher levels become 'clothed' in a 'body' or 'garment' that acts as a receptacle and thus the means of expression of the higher level, while at the same time obscuring the higher level to a greater or lesser degree, depending on the level of refinement of the 'body' or 'garment.' 

שֶׁכָּל תַּחְתּוֹן לְגַבֵּי עֶלְיוֹן הוּא בִּבְחִי' גּוּף אֵלָיו 

**This is because every lower** level **is** regarded as **the aspect of 'body'** (synonymous with 'garment' here) **compared with the higher** level

וְכָל עֶלְיוֹן הוּא הַצּוּרָה לְהַתַּחְתּוֹן. 

**and the higher** level **is** regarded as **the *tzurah*** or soul **of the lower,**

וְעִקַּר הַכֹּחַ וְהַפּוֹעֵל הוּא בְּהָעֶלְיוֹן וְעִקַּר הַפְּעֻלָּה וְהַמַּעֲשֶׂה הוּא בְּהַתַּחְתּוֹן. 

**so that the primary potential and its actualization** (actualization in the sense that is appropriate for the *tzurah*) **is in the upper level, whereas the primary activity and deed is in the lower level.**

אָמְנָם הִנֵּה לֹא נִשְׁלָם שׁוּם דָּבָר אֶלָּא רַק ע"י כֻּלָּם יַחַד דַּוְקָא

**Nevertheless, nothing can be achieved without both of them together.** 

הֲיְינוּ ע"י כָּל הֲשׁשָׁה בְּיַחַד שֶׁהֵם הָה' חֶלְקֵי צוּרָה דנרנח"י. 

**That is to say, all six** aspects **together – the five parts of the *tzurah, nrnch"y*** – *nefesh, ruach, neshama, chaya* and *yechida **–***

עִם הַגּוּף כְּשֶׁהֵם כֻּלָּם בְּיַחַד 

**with the body** in which they become clothed**, all of them together form a single unit.**

כִּי עִנְיַן הָה' חֶלְקֵי צוּרָה דנרנח"י 

**The nature of the five aspects of the *tzurah, nrnch"y,*** 

הוּא כִּי נִתְפַּשֵּׁט בָּהֶם הַגִּלּוּי דְּכָל תְּכוּנַת פַּרְצוּף אָדָם

**is that in them is manifested in a revelation of all the qualities of the human *partzuf*** *–* literally 'countenance,' but here implying the entire concept of what it means to be human, as a reflection of the Divine Image (alternatively, we could simply transliterate this as *partzuf Adam*)–   

מִגִּלּוּי לְגִלּוּי עַד הַצּוּרָה הָאַחֲרוֹנָה שֶׁהִיא הַנֶּפֶשׁ

**from illumination to illumination** or revelation to revelation **until the final *tzurah* which is the** level of ***nefesh.*** 

וְשָׁם נִגְמַר כָּל תְּכוּנוֹת וַעֲנִינִי הָאָדָם בְּכֹחַ כְּכָל הָרָאוּי אֵלָיו

**It is there that all the appropriate qualities and characteristics of man are completed in potential.**

This is because the level of *nefesh* corresponds to *malchut*, which completes the revelation of the levels of soul,[^2] just like *malchut* completes the revelation of the array of *sefirot*.    

אֲבָל עֲדַיִן הוּא רַק בְּכֹחַ לְבַד אֶלָּא שֶׁהַנֶּפֶשׁ שֶׁהִיא הַצּוּרָה אַחֲרוֹנָה

**Nevertheless, it is still only in a potential state, just that the *nefesh* is the final aspect of *tzurah*,** 

הִנֵּה בָּהּ נִגְמַר הַגִּלּוּי דְּכַל מְצִיאַת הַכֹּחַ הָרָאוּי לִתְכוּנַת עִנְיָנֵי פַּרְצוּף אָדָם מַמָּשׁ

**and with it** (the *nefesh*) becoming manifested, **the revelation of all the latent powers that are appropriate for the characteristics of the *partzuf* of man are in fact complete.** 

וּמִמֶּנָּה הִנֵּה נִמְשָׁךְ וְיוֹצֵא מִן הַכֹּחַ אֶל הַפֹּעַל ע"י הַגּוּף

**And from it** from the *nefesh* **it** the latent powers **are drawn down and emerge from potential to actual via the body**[^3] 

אֲשֶׁר הַנֶּפֶשׁ מִתְעָרֵב בּוֹ וּמִתְעַלֵּם עַמּוֹ לְהֵעָשׂוֹת לְאֶחָד.

**which is infused with the *nefesh* concealed within it, to form a single entity,** 

וכמ"ש 'כִּי הַדָּם הוּא הַנָּפֶשׁ' 'וְלֹא תֹאכַל הַנֶּפֶשׁ עִם הַבָּשָׂר'.

**as it is written, "for the blood is the *nefesh*"** (Genesis 17:11), **and** "be strong and do not eat the blood, for the blood is the *nefesh,* **and when you eat meat do not consume the *nefesh*** **with it"** (Deuteronomy 12:23).

It is the nefesh 'clothed' in the blood that engenders life, and thus the two together, the *tzurah* and the vessel (the blood, the body etc.) form a single unit.

וְנִמְצָא כִּי גְּמַר הַשְׁלָמַת פַּרְצוּף אָדָם הוּא רַק ע"י כָּל הֲשֵׁשָׁהּ יַחַד דַּוְקָא.

**So it turns out that the completion of the human *partzuf* is only when all six aspects are together as one.**  

כִּי בְּהָנֶפֶשׁ עוֹמֵד בָּהּ הַמַּהוּת דְּפַרְצוּף אָדָם 

**Because in the *nefesh* is situated the essence of the human *partzuf*** 

בְּכָל עִנְיָנָיו שֶׁבּוֹ רַק בְּכֹחַ אֲבָל לֹא בְּפֹעַל 

**in all of its aspects, albeit that this is only in a potential state and not** yet **actual.** 

וּבְהָרוּחַ הִנֵּה הוּא רַק בִּבְחִי' כֹּחַ לְכֹּחַ 

**In *ruach* this is only in a state of latent potential** (literally, 'capacity of the capacity') i.e. twice removed from actualization  

וּבְהַנְשָׁמָה הִנֵּה בָּהּ הוּא רַק הַשֹּׁרֶשׁ לְהַכֹּחַ דְּכֹּחַ,

**while in *neshama* only the root of latent potential is there** i.e. three times removed from actualization. 

וְכֵן הוּא עוֹלֶה לְמַעְלָה לְמַעְלָה וְנִתְמַעֵט גִּלּוּיוֹ וּמַהוּתוֹ 

**And so too** as we progress through the levels **higher and higher, the** degree of **revelation and essential nature** of the *tzurah* **becomes more and more ethereal**[^4] 

בְּכָל עֶלְיוֹן עַד הַצּוּרָה הָעֶלְיוֹנָה שֶׁהוּא הַיְּחִידָה 

**at each** progressively **higher level, until the final** aspect of ***tzurah*****, which is *yechida.***

אֲשֶׁר שָׁם עוֹמֵד כָּל הַתְּכוּנָה דְּפַרְצוּף אָדָם בְּלִי מַהוּת כְּלָל

**It is there that the entire human *partzuf* is situated without any defining characteristics at all,** since, at this level, the *tzurah* completely transcends the limitations of defining characteristics.  

אֶלָּא רַק בִּבְחִי' שֹׁרֶשׁ לְשֹׁרֶשׁ שֹׁרֶשׁ הַשָּׁרָשִׁים.

**Instead it is merely the aspect of the root of the root of the root of all roots.**  

כִּי בְּכָל צוּרָה שֶׁהִיא יוֹתֵר עֶלְיוֹנָה נִתְמַעֵט וְנִתְעַלֵּם בָּהּ הַגִּלּוּי דְּמַהוּת וְתֹאַר פַּרְצוּף אָדָם.

**This is because the loftier the *tzurah*, the more ethereal** or transcendent **and concealed the display of the human** **characteristics and definitions of a *partzuf* become.**

וְלָכֵן נִקְרָא הַיְּחִידָה בְּפִי הַמְּקֻבָּלִים בְּשֵׁם אֶפֶס וְאַיִן 

**Therefore *yechida* is referred to by the Kabbalists as "*efes*"** nothingness **and "*ayin*"** indiscernible or inscrutable,

מִשּׁוּם שֶׁבָּהּ מִסְתַּיֵּם הַהַשָּׂגָה לְגַמְרֵי, וְהִיא בְּלִי מַהוּת מֻשָּׂג.

**since this is where understanding terminates completely, and it is of an unfathomable nature.**

כִּי אֵלוּ הָה' חֶלְקֵי נְשָׁמָה שֶׁבְּכָל פַּרְצוּף שֶׁהֵם נרנח"י הֵם נִמְשָׁכִים מֵהַעֱשֶׂר סְפִירוֹת:

**Now these five aspects of the soul in every *partzuf*, i.e. *nrnch"y*, are drawn from the ten *sefirot*:**  

יְחִידָה – כֶּתֶר. וְהוּא נִקְרָא אַיִן, כמ"ש בזוה"ק בְּשֶׁלַח ס"ד ב' ובאדר"ז רפ"ז ב'.

***yechida*** **from *keter*, and this is called *ayin*, as the *Zohar*, *Beshalach* 64a and *Idra Zuta* p. 287b state;** 

חַיָּה הוּא חֲכָמָה ע"ש הַכָּתוּב (קֹהֶלֶת ז') 'הַחֲכָמָה תִּחְיֶה בְּעָלֶיהָ' וְכֵן חָכָם גִּימַטְרִיָא חַיִּים

 ***chaya*** which is related to the word *chayim –* life **is** from ***chochma*****, as in (*Kohelet* 7**:12**) "*chochma*** wisdom **preserves the life** *chayim* **of those who possess it,"**

וְהִיא נִמְשַׁךְ וְיוֹצֵא מִן הַכֶּתֶר וכמ"ש (אִיּוֹב כ"ח) 'וְהַחָכְמָה מֵאַיִן חַמְּצָא'.

**and it** (*chochma*) **is drawn down from and emerges from *keter*, as the verse (Job** 28:12**)** **states, "and wisdom emerges from *ayin*"** (i.e. *keter*). We have translated the verse the way it is interpreted here. However, the plain meaning of the text presents this as a question – "where is *chochma* to be found?      

שֶׁהוּא מֵהַכֶּתֶר שֶׁנִּקְרָא אַיִן לְגֹדֶל הֶעְלֵמוֹ. כִּי הוֹרָאַת מִלַּת אַיִן הוּא בר"ת אַיֶּה נֶעֱלַם. 

**For it comes from *keter,* which is called *ayin*** *–* inscrutable – **due to its profound concealment*,*** 

כִּי הוֹרָאַת מִלַּת אַיִן הוּא בר"ת \[בְּרָאשֵׁי תֵּבוֹת\] 'אַיֵּה נֶעֱלַם'

**which is why the word *ayin*** can be interpreted as **an acronym "where is it concealed?"**

The letters *aleph* and *yud* of **אַיֵּ**ה and the letter *nun* of **נֶ**עֱלַם together form the word אַיִן. 

וְהַחָכְמָה הוּא נִקְרָא יֵשׁ, כמ"ש ברע"מ פִּנְחָס רל"ט א'.

By way of contrast with the 'nothingness' of *keter*, ***chochma* itself is called *'yesh'*** – something –

וְהִיא יֵשׁ מֵאַיִן וְנִתְגַּלָּה מְצִיאוּתָהּ וְלֹא מַהוּתָהּ.

**for it** emerges ***ex nihilo*** **in a manner that its existence, but not its essence, is revealed.**

נְשָׁמָה בֵּינָהּ וְנִקְרָא 'הוּא' וכמ"ש בפ' וְיָצָא קנ"ד ב' כִּי הִיא עֲדַיִן נִסְתָּר. 

***Neshama*** **is** associated with the *sefirah* of ***binah*****, which is referred to** in the third person, **'he,'** indicating that it is as yet remote from us.

*Binah* is a 'feminine' *sefirah*, often referred to in *Zohar* as *imma*, the 'mother' *sefirah,* while *chochma* is a 'male' *sefirah* referred to as the 'father' *sefirah*[^5]

וְהָרוּחַ שֶׁהוּא ת"ת הַכּוֹלֵל כָּל הו"ק נִקְרָא אַתָּה, כמ"ש בְּתִקּוּנִים תִּקּוּן י"ט דַּף ט"ל. 

***Ruach*** **is** associated with the *sefirah* of ***tiferet*****, which includes all of the six extremities** i.e. the six *sefirot* from *chesed* to *yesod* **and is called 'you'** in the second person. This indicates that it is a level that is close to us, with which we can communicate directly, face-to-face so to speak, **as is explained in the *Tikunim*** the *Tikunei Zohar, **Tikun*** **19, p. 39\.**  

הַנֶּפֶשׁ מַלְכוּת נִקְרָא אֲנִי. וכמ"ש בְּפ' מִקֵּץ ר"ד ע"ב וּפ' תְּרוּמָה קנ"ו א' וְכֵן בהק"ז ו' ע"א:

***Nefesh*** **is** associated with the *sefirah* of ***malchut,*** **and is called 'I'** in the first person, **as the *Zohar,* *parshat Miketz* p. 204b, and *parshat Terumah* 156a, and in the Introduction to the *Zohar* p. 6a state.**

Not only is it close to us, it actually is our life force and thus who we are (in this world). 

וְנַחֲזֹר עַל הַדְּבָרִים כְּדֵי לְבָא לְכַוָּנָתֵנוּ 

**Let's review what we've said in order to proceed to our intention:**    

כִּי כָּל הָה' חֶלְקֵי צוּרָה שֶׁהֵם נרנח"י הִנֵּה הֵם מָה שֶׁמִּתְפַּשְּׁטִים מִגִּלּוּי לְגִלּוּי לִתְכוּנַת כָּל עֲנִינִי דְּתֹאַר פַּרְצוּף אָדָם

**All five parts of the *tzurah*, the *nrnch"y*, radiate from illumination to illumination** or revelation to revelation, thus infusing **all of the properties of the human *partzuf* with all its characteristics.**

אֶלָּא שֶׁכָּל הִתְפַּשְּׁטוּתָם וְגִלּוּיָים הִנֵּה הוּא רַק לִמְצִיאוּת הַכֹּחַ לְבַד אֲשֶׁר לְזֶה

**However, all their radiating forth and illumination is only insofar as their existential** aspect of the **power**, but not its essential inner dimension. 

וְנִמְשַׁךְ הַגִּלּוּי דְּהַכֹּחַ אֲשֶׁר לְזֶה מִדַּרְגָּא לַדַּרְגָּא עַד הַנֶּפֶשׁ,

**And the revelation of that power is drawn down from level to level until** the level of ***nefesh,***

וְשָׁם נִשְׁלָם אוֹתוֹ הַכֹּחַ אֲשֶׁר לִתְכוּנַת מַהוּת וְתֹאַר פַּרְצוּף אָדָם בְּכָל הָרָאוּי.

**where that power becomes complete in the essential qualities and description of** what it means to be **human in the proper sense.**  

אַךְ יְצִיאָתָם מִן הַכֹּחַ אֶל הַפּוֹעֵל מַמָּשׁ הוּא רַק ע"י הַגּוּף

**But** again, **their emergence from potential to actual** corporeal **reality is only via the body,**

כִּי רַק בְּהַגּוּף נַעֲשֶׂה הַפְּעֻלָּה אֲבָל כָּל מָה שֶׁלְּמַעְלָה מִן הַגּוּף הוּא רַק בְּכֹחַ לְבַד

**because it is only an action done with the body, whereas when it is above**, transcending **the body**, **it is only** in a state of **potential**.   

שֶׁהַנֶּפֶשׁ הוּא הַכֹּחַ הַיּוֹתֵר קָרוֹב אֲשֶׁר לְזֶה וְהָרוּחַ הוּא הַכֹּחַ לְכֹחַ

**Thus the *nefesh*** level of the *tzurah* **is the closest capacity** to the human being, **whereas *ruach* is** only **the latent potential** (literally, 'capacity of the capacity') twice removed.   

וְהַנְּשָׁמָה הוּא הַשֹּׁרֶשׁ לכ"ז וְהַחַיָּה הוּא שֹׁרֶשׁ הַשָּׁרָשִׁים

**And** the level of ***neshama*** **is the root of all of them** of the levels below, **and *chaya* is the root of the root,** 

וְנִתְמַעֵט גִּלּוּיָים בכ"א שֶׁהוּא לְמַעְלָה

**so that the loftier** the level **the more ethereal** or transcendent **the revelation becomes,** 

עַד שֶׁבַּהִיחִידָה שֶׁהוּא הַשֹּׁרֶשׁ לְכֻלָּם אֵין בּוֹ לְכָל זֶה שֵׁם וּמִלָּה כְּלָל,

**until in *yechida*, which is the root of all of them, there is no name or term** **at all** that can describe or define it, 

וְנֶעֱלַם בּוֹ לְגַמְרֵי הֲגַם שֶׁכֻּלָּם הֵם מִמֶּנּוֹ וּלְכָךְ נִקְרָא אַיִן כַּנִז'.

**and in it** (in *yechida*) these qualities **are completely concealed, even though they all derive therefrom. And this is why** *yechida* **is called *ayin –* inscrutable.** 

אָמְנָם עכ"ז הִנֵּה 'דַּעַת לְנָבוֹן נָקָל' (משלי יד, ו) שֶׁכָּל מָה שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ מִגֹּדֶל הַעֲלָמָתָם 

**Nevertheless, 'wisdom will come easily to the understanding one'** (Proverbs 14:6) **regarding everything we stated about these very abstruse matters,** 

אֲשֶׁר בְּכָל יוֹתֵר עֶלְיוֹן, הִנֵּה הוּא רַק בְּעֵרֶךְ הַפְּעֻלָּה הָאַחֲרוֹנָה,

**where the loftiest of levels is only** very vaguely **related to the final act,  **  

הַפְּעֻלָּה הַמַּמָּשִׁית הַנַּעֲשֶׂה בְּהַגּוּף הַמֻּשָּׂג וְהַנִּתְפָּס לְכָל עַיִן חָמְרֵי וְגוּפָנִי. 

**i.e. the physical act done with a body that can be comprehended and grasped by an eye that is material and corporeal.**

Accordingly, there is no real paradigm by which to evaluate the lower levels by comparing them with the higher levels, since they are basically (almost) incomparable.

אֲבָל בַּמְּצִיאוּת הנרנח"י עַצְמָן, בְּהַעֲרֵךְ זֶה כְּנֶגֶד זֶה, 

**But as regards the *nrnch"y* themselves, when evaluating one against the other,**

הִנֵּה נִבְחָן כָּל תַּחְתּוֹן בְּהַעֲרֵךְ הָעֶלְיוֹן מִמֶּנּוּ 

**each lower level can be assessed in comparison with** the level **above it.** 

וּבִפְרַט בְּעֵרֶךְ הַשְּׁלִישִׁי שֶׁעָלָיו שֶׁהוּא הָעֶלְיוֹן דְּעֶלְיוֹן

**And this is particularly** apropos **when evaluating** a lower level by comparing it with **the third level above – that which is above the level above it** i.e. twice removed where the distinctions between them are even more blatant,  

הִנֵּה נִבְחַן הַתַּחְתּוֹן נֶגְדּוֹ הֵיטֵיב, בְּכָל תְּכוּנַת מַהוּת וְתֹאַר אָדָם מַמָּשׁ.

so that **the lower level can indeed be appraised well in comparison with it, in every aspect of the** inner **essence and** outer **description of the human** *partzuf*, since they both have the same *tzurah*, albeit one much loftier than the other,     

כִּי הֲרֵי כָּל תַּחְתּוֹן הוּא בִּבְחִי' גּוּף וּלְבוּשׁ לְהָעֶלְיוֹן שֶׁהוּא נְשָׁמָה אֵלָיו 

**since every lower level** of *nrnch"y* **serves as a 'body' and 'garment' for the higher level which is its soul** ( neshama)**.**

וְנֶחֱשָׁב הָעֶלְיוֹן אֵלָיו בִּבְחִי' אֵיכוּת וְכֹּחַ.

Accordingly, **the higher level is regarded** by the lower level **as its superior quality and potential,** 

וְהַתַּחְתּוֹן הוּא נֶחְשָׁב לְהָעֶלְיוֹן, שֶׁבּוֹ מוֹצִיא אֶת תְּכוּנָתוֹ וְכֹּחוֹ מִן הַכֹּחַ אֶל הַפּוֹעֵל וּמִן הָאֵיכוּת אֶל הַכַּמּוּת,

**while the lower level is regarded as important to the higher level – as the means by which it expresses its qualities and capabilities, from potential to actual, and from quality to quantity.** 

וְנִגְמַר בּוֹ הַמַּעֲשֶׂה וְהַפְּעֻלָּה דְּהַצּוּרָה הָעֶלְיוֹנָה כִּי הוּא בִּבְחִי' חֹמֶר וְגוּף נֶגְדּוֹ.

**And through it** (via the lower level) **the actions and effects of the higher *tzurah* are completed, since it** the lower level **is the aspect of 'material' and 'body' in comparison with it.**  

וַהֲרֵי נִמְצָא כִּי הַכִּנּוּי דְּפַרְצוּף אָדָם וְכָל הַמַּהוּת וְהַתֹּאַר שֶׁבּוֹ הוּא נִדּוֹן בַּעֲרָכִין.

**Thus it turns out that the expression 'the human *partzuf*' with its entire** inner **essence and** outer **description is amenable to comparative analysis.**   

דְּכַל עֶלְיוֹן לְגַבֵּי תַּחְתּוֹן הוּא אֵינוֹ מְכֻנֶּה בְּשֵׁם פַּרְצוּף וּבְמַהוּת אָדָם כְּלָל 

**While the upper** level **compared to the lower level is not dubbed '*partzuf*' or given any human attributions at all,**

אֲבָל כָּל תַּחְתּוֹן לְגַבֵּי עֶלְיוֹן וּמִכָּל שֶׁכֵּן לְהָעֶלְיוֹן דְּעֶלְיוֹן הִנֵּה מְכֻנָּה וּמְתֹאָר הַתַּחְתּוֹן הַשְּׁלִישִׁי.

**such a label and description** is applied to **the lower** level **compared to the upper** level, **and all the more so** when compared **to an even** **higher** level, **as a third-level** entity,   

כְּגוֹן: הַנֶּפֶשׁ לְגַבֵּי נְשָׁמָה וְכֵן הָרוּחַ לְגַבֵּי הַחָיָה 

**for example – *nefesh*** is a third-level (i.e. twice-removed) entity **in comparison with *neshama*,** since the order is *nefesh, ruach, neshama.* **And similarly *ruach*** is a third-level (i.e. twice-removed) entity **in comparison with *chaya*** since the order is *ruach, neshama, chaya*,

בְּכָל תְּכוּנַת וּמַהוּת וְתֹאַר פַּרְצוּף אָדָם מַמָּשׁ 

and they are **indeed** labeled **as** **a human *partzuf*** with **all the qualities and character and description thereof**,

וּכְמוֹ שֶׁהוּא בְּהַגּוּף ע"י הַנֶּפֶשׁ רוּחַ שֶׁבּוֹ.

**just like the body compared with the *nefesh* and *ruach*** vested **within it.**

וְהִנֵּה כְּבָר אָמַרְנוּ לְעֵיל שַׁעַר ג' שכ"א מהנרנח"י הוּא כָּלוּל ג"כ מנרנח"י,

**Now we already explained above in Sha'ar 3 that each of the *nrnch"y*** *nefesh, ruach, nesham, chaya, yechida* **itself comprises *nrnch"y***

  וְיֶשְׁנָם ה' בְּחִי' נרנח"י בְּהַנְפֵּשׁ וַה' בְּחִי' נרנח"י בְּהַרְוּחַ וְכֵן ה' בְּחִי' בְּהַנְשָׁמָה וַה' בְּחִי' בְּהַחָיָה וַה' בְּחִי' בְּהַיְּחִידָה.

**so that there are five levels of *nrnch"y* of the *nefesh*, and five levels of *nrnch"y* of *ruach,*** **and five levels of *nrnch"y* of *neshama,* and five levels of *nrnch"y* of *chaya****,* **and five levels of *nrnch"y* of *yechida.***

וא"כ הֲרֵי כָּל מָה שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ מֵעִנְיָן הִתְפַּשְּׁטוּת הֲגִלּוּיָם דְּכַל תְּכוּנַת וּמַהוּת פַּרְצוּף אָדָם, 

**This being so, then everything we discussed about the concept of radiating forth the revelation of all of the qualities and the character of the human *partzuf,*** 

אֵיךְ שֶׁנִּתְפַּשֵּׁט וְיוֹצֵא מִגִּלּוּי לְגִלּוּי וּמִדַּרְגָּא לְדַרְגָּא,

**and how they radiate forth from illumination to illumination** or revelation to revelation 

מֵהַצּוּרָה הָעֶלְיוֹנָה שֶׁהוּא הַיְּחִידָה עַד הַצּוּרָה הַתַּחְתּוֹנָה שֶׁהוּא הַנֶּפֶשׁ.

**from the loftiest** aspect of the ***tzurah*****, which is *yechida*, until the lowliest** aspect of the ***tzurah*****, which is the *nefesh***.

וְכֵן כָּל מָה שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ מֵעִנְיָן שְׁלִילַת הַתֹּאַר וּמַהוּת דְּפַרְצוּף אָדָם

**Similarly, everything that we stated regarding the negation of descriptors and definitions of human form**   

אֲשֶׁר בְּכָל צוּרָה הַיּוֹתֵר עֶלְיוֹנָה בְּעֵרֶךְ הַתַּחְתּוֹן שֶׁלְּמַטָּה הֵימֵנָה,

**in connection with a higher *tzurah* compared with one below it**,

וְאֵיךְ כִּי עִם כָּל זֶה הִנֵּה בַּעֲרָכִין נִדּוֹן אוֹתוֹ הַמַּהוּת וְהַתֹּאַר בְּכֻלָּם עַל כָּל פָּנִים.

**and how, despite this, their essence and description are nevertheless comparable across the board –**  

הִנֵּה כ"ז הוּא הֵן בהנרנח"י הַכְּלָלִיִּים וְהֵן בהנרנח"י הַפְּרָטִיִּים שבכ"א גּוּפָהּ.

**all of this is true regarding both the macro-level *nrnch"y***, **and the** **micro-level *nrnch"y*** **within each of them** – within each of the macro-levels. 

וְהִנֵּה הַפְּרָטִיִּים הֲרֵי הֵם כ"ה בְּחִי' וּכְנִזְכָּר.

**Thus,** fully iterated, **there are twenty-five distinct aspects, as mentioned** previously.

וא"כ הֲרֵי אָנוּ רוֹאִין מִזֶּה שֶׁכָּל הַתֹּאַר וּמַהוּת דְּפַרְצוּף אָדָם הַנַּעֲשֶׂה וְנִגְמָר בְּהַגּוּף הַחָמְרִי הָעִקָּרִי,

**If so, we see from this that the entire description and essence of the human *partzuf* is fashioned and completed via the physical body as the main thing.** 

כַּמָּה שֶׁרָחוֹק וּמְשּׁוּלָל הוּא מִבְּחִי' הַצּוּרָה הַיּוֹתֵר עֶלְיוֹנָה שֶׁהוּא הַיְּחִידָה הַפְּרָטִית

This is so despite **how** **far apart from and how antithetical the body is to the loftiest aspect of the *tzurah –* the micro-level *yechida* –**  

אֲשֶׁר בְּהַיְּחִידָה הַכְּלָלִית אֲשֶׁר הוּא לְמַעְלָה וְגָבוֹהַּ מֵהַגּוּף הַחָמְרִי כ"ה מַדְרֵגוֹת.

**of the macro-level yechida, which is twenty-five lofty levels above the physical body\!** 

וְגַם כ"ז הוּא רַק בהכ"ה בְּחִי' דנרנח"י הַפְּרָטִיִּים אֲשֶׁר בהנרנח"י הַכְּלָלִיִּים שֶׁבְּעוֹלַם הָעֲשִׂיָּה לְבַד.

**Moreover, all this is only the twenty-five** individual **levels of the micro-level *nrnch"y*** into which the **macro-level *nrnch"y* of the World of Asiyah** alone is subdivided.

וְכֵן הֲרֵי יֶשְׁנָם כ"ז בְּעוֹלַם הַיְּצִירָה, וְכֵן בְּעוֹלַם הַבְּרִיאָה,

**Add to that the fact that all these are also present in the World of Yetzirah, and similarly in the World of Beriah.** 

כִּי בְּכָל עוֹלָם הֲרֵי יֶשְׁנָם ה' בְּחִי' נרנח"י כְּלָלִיִּים וכ"ה בְּחִי' נרנח"י פְּרָטִיִּים

**And so too in every world – there are the five aspects of macro-level *nrnch"y* and twenty-five levels of the micro-level *nrnch"y***

(וְהֵם בְּסַךְ הַכֹּל קכ"ה בְּחִי' וְהוּא בִּפְנִימִיּוֹת וְכֵן הוּא ג"כ בַּחִיצוֹנִיּוּת

**(for a total of 125 inner-dimensional aspects, and similarly in the outer dimension,**  

וכמ"ש הָרַב בְּשַׁעַר פְּנִימִיּוֹת וְחִיצוֹנִיּוּת דָּרוּשׁ י"א ובכ"מ)

**As the rabbi** Rabbi Chaim Vital **writes** in the name of the Arizal **in *Sha'ar Pnimiut v'Chitzoniyut*** \[*Sha'ar* 40\] **chapter 11, and in a number of places).**  

וַהֲרֵי אָנוּ רוֹאִין מכ"ז אֵיךְ שֶׁנֶּעֱלַם וּמְשּׁוּלָל מֵהַשָּׂגַת וּתְפִיסַת בְּנֵי אָדָם 

**So we see from all this the extent of concealment and inaccessibility to man's grasp and understanding**

מִלְּהַבְחִין שׁוּם תְּמוּנַת מַהוּת וְתֹאַר גַּם בְּעוֹלַם הַבְּרִיאָה,

**by which he could discern an image of the nature of** **the world of Beriah, or even a description thereof.**

הַגַּם שֶׁהוּא בֶּאֱמֶת ג"כ עוֹלָם מָלֵא מִכָּל הַמְּצִיאוּת וְהַעֲנִינִים שֶׁבְּהֶעֱלֶם הַזֶּה 

This is so **despite the fact that** Beriah **is indeed also a world, replete with its existence and those matters concealed there** 

וְהוּא כֻּלּוֹ נִבְרָא וּמְחֻדָּשׁ וְאֵינוֹ אֱלָהוּת כְּלָל. 

**and** Beriah **is a creation and is renewed and originated** from nothing, **and it is not Godliness at all.**

אַךְ כְּבָר אָמַרְנוּ לְעֵיל שַׁעַר ב' שֶׁמֵּהַחֹמֶר דְּעוֹלָם הָעֶלְיוֹן נַעֲשָׂה הַצּוּרָה דְּעוֹלָם הַתַּחְתּוֹן 

**Now we already stated above in *Sha'ar* 2 that the *tzurah* of the lower world is fashioned from the 'material' aspect of the higher world,**

וְכָל צוּרָה נֶגֶד הַחֹמֶר הֲרֵי אֵין בָּהּ תֹּאַר מִפַּרְצוּף אָדָם כְּלָל וּכְנִזְכָּר.

**and that *tzurah* compared with materiality has no human countenance at all, as mentioned.** 

וא"כ הֲרֵי כַּמָּה רָחוֹק וּמְשּׁוּלָל הוּא מֵהַשָּׂגָתֵנוּ לְהַשִּׂיג אֶת מַהוּת הַחֹמֶר וְהַגֶּשֶׁם

**This being so, it shows how remote from and inaccessible to our intellectual grasp it is to fathom the essence of 'materiality' and 'physicality'** –

אֲשֶׁר גַּם בְּעוֹלַם הַבְּרִיאָה בְּתַכְלִית הָרִחוּק וְהַשְּׁלִילָה הַיּוֹתֵר אֶפְשָׁרִי:

**even in the World of Beriah, which is as remote and as inaccessible as could possibly be** from our understanding. 

וְזֶהוּ שֶׁאָמְרוּ בַּזֹּהַר חָדָשׁ בְּרֵאשִׁית י"א סע"א: אָמַר ר' יוֹחָנָן בֶּן זַכַּאי: כַּמָּה נִצְטַעֵר הַהוּא גַּבְרָא 

**This is** what is meant **when the *Zohar Chadash, Bereishit* 11a**[^6] **states: Rabbi Yochanan ben Zakkai declared, "How great was the yearning of that great man** to behold the Divine Glory. 

דִּכְתִיב בֵּיהּ 'לֹא כֵן עַבְדִּי מֹשֶׁה בְּכׇל בֵּיתִי נֶאֱמָן הוּא' לְמַחֲזִי חַד שַׁמָּשָׁא דִּמְשַׁמְּשָׁא קָמֵיהּ 

**Regarding him the verse** (Numbers 12:7) **states 'This is not true of My servant Moshe, who is a trusted servant throughout My house'**[^7] 

וְלֹא אֶתֶיְהִבַת לֵהּ רְשׁוּתָא, דִּכְתִיב 'הֲרְאֵנִי נָא אֶת כְּבוֹדֶךָ'.

**and yet** **he was not granted permission, as when the verse** (Exodus 33:18) **states, "Please show me Your Glory"** 

וְהוּא דִּכְתִיב בֵּיהּ 'כָּבוֹד אֵל' וְהוּא מטטרו"ן שָׁר הַפְָּנִים

**referring to the one of whom it is written 'he is the glory of God,' –** the angel **Metatron, the Angel of the Presence.**

וְהָאֵיךְ שֶׁאֵיל מֹשֶׁה לִמֶחְמֵּי דָּא?

**Now how did Moshe think to request this?** After, all was he not a human being?

אֶלָּא מֹשֶׁה סָבַר כְּיוֹן דַּחֲמָא גּוּפֵיה אֶרְבָּעֵן יוֹמִין וְאֶרְבָּעֵן לִילִין דְּלָא אָכִיל וּשְׁתֵּי,

**However, Moshe opined that since he saw that his own body did not eat or drink for forty days and nights,**  

וַהֲוָה נִזּוֹן מֵאֲסְפְּקלַרְיָא דִּלְעִילָא, סָבַר דִּכְדַאי הוּא לְמִשְׁאַל דָּא.

**and he was nourished by a supernal radiance he opined that he was worthy of this request.** 

וּמָה אָתִיב לֵהּ? 'לֹא תּוּכַל לִרְאוֹת אֶת פָּנַי'. הוּא מָרָא דְּאֲפַּיָא.

**But what was the response to him? "You cannot view My countenance"** (Exodus 33:20),[^8] **referring to the Master of the Presence** the Angel Metatron. 

וְלָא יָהֵיב לֵיהּ רְשׁוּתָא כו' 

**And he was not given permission…**[^9]

כִּי מט"ט הִנֵּה הוּא כּוֹלֵל אֶת כָּל הָעוֹלָמוֹת בי"ע,

**for** the Angel **Metatron includes all the worlds of Beriah, Yetzirah and Assiya,**

וכמ"ש בזוה"ק פ' וַיְחִי רכ"ג רע"ב: חַד עוּלִימָא דְּאוֹרְכֵיה מֵרֵישָׁא דְּעַלְמָא לְסַיְּפִי דְּעַלְמָא.

**as the holy Zohar (Vayechi 223b) states** in reference to the Angel Metatron: **One 'youth' who extends from the beginning of the world** of Beriah **to the end of the world** of Asiyah.[^10]   

וְאָמְרוּ עוֹד בְּתִקּוּנִים תִּקּוּן ס"ח שֶׁכָּל דְּיוֹקָנִין דְּמַלְאֲכַיָּא 

**The *Tikunim* (*Tikun* 68\) states further that all the images of the angels** 

וּדְיוֹקָנִין דִּנְשַׁמְתִּין וּדְיוֹקָנִין דְּכָל מָה דְּאִתְבְרִי בִּשְׁמַיָּא וּבְאַרְעָא כֻּלָּם כְּלוּלִים בּוֹ.

**and all the images of the soul, and all the images of whatever was created in the heavens and earth are all included in him** in Metatron,

כִּי הוּא צוּרָתָם וְלָכֵן לֹא הָיָה אֶפְשָׁר גַּם לְמֹשֶׁה רַבֵּנוּ ע"ה בֶּהֱיוֹתוֹ בהעוה"ז לִרְאוֹתוֹ בְּחוּשׁ.

**for he is their form, and therefore even Moshe Rabbeinu could not apprehend him with his sense of sight while he** Moshe **was in this world.**

כִּי אִי אֶפְשָׁר לָאָדָם לִרְאוֹת תֹּאַר הַצּוּרָה בְּשׁוּם אֹפֶן

**Because it is absolutely impossible for a person to see the image of a *tzurah,*** 

מֵאַחַר שֶׁבְּעֵרֶךְ הַגּוּף אֵין לְהַצּוּרָה תְּמוּנָה וְתֹאַר כְּלָל וּכְנִז':

**since, compared with the body, the *tzurah* does not have a picture and an image at all, as mentioned.**  

הִתְבּוֹנֵן בְּכָל הַדְּבָרִים הַלָּלוּ הֲגַם שֶׁפְּשׁוּטִים הֵם,

**So contemplate all of these matters, even though they are straightforward,**

וְקַח אוֹתָם בְּלִבְּךָ כִּי הֵם מֵהַקְדָּמוֹת הַנִּצְרָכוֹת מְאֹד 

**and take them to heart, for they are the exceedingly necessary introduction**

לְכָל מִי שֶׁרוֹצֶה לִכְנֹס בְּעֵדֶן גַּן אֱלֹהִים 

**for anyone who wishes to enter Eden, the Garden of God,**

וְלִטְעֹם טַעַם עֵץ הַחַיִּים כְּדֵי לְהַדְבֵּק בֶּאֱלֹהִים חַיִּים,

**and savor the taste of the Tree of Life in order to cleave to the Living God.**

וְהִקְדַּמְנוּ כ"ז לְהַרְחִיק אֶת הַתְּפִיסָה וְהַדִּמְיוֹן מִכָּל מָה שֶׁנְּדַבֵּר עוֹד בְּזֶה לַמַּטֶּה.

**We have made this introduction in order to dissociate** people from **the** mistaken **idea and** erroneous **impression that will be discussed below.**

וע"ז אָמְרוּ (בְּהַקְדָּמַת הַזֹּהַר ז' רע"א) 'וְאִם רָץ לִבְּךָ שׁוֹב לְאֶחָד'.

**In this regard,** our Sages declared **(in the Introduction to the *Zohar* p. 7a) "If your heart runs, return to the One,"** 

כִּי גַּם וְהַחַיּוֹת שֶׁבְּמֶרְכָּבָה הֵם רָצוֹא וְשׁוֹב:

**because even the** angels **of the Divine Chariot** (the *Merkava*), known as the ***chayot*****, are in a state of 'running and returning'** (See Ezekiel 1:14).

The intention here is that even the holy angels, who are capable of great profundity of thought, grasp these lofty ideas only fleetingly and must return to a lower level in order to process them.[^11]
