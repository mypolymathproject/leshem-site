**Sha'ar 4 Chapter 4**

**Revelation of His Actualization**

הִנֵּה גִּלּוּיוֹ בִּפְעֻלָּתוֹ ית"ש אֲשֶׁר נִתְגַּלָּה בָּזֶה בְּפֹעַל מַמָּשׁ

**Now** we will discuss **His revelation within His actions – where He is revealed in actuality –**  

הִנֵּה הוּא בְּכָל הַמְּצִיאוּת דְּהַד' עוֹלָמוֹת אֲצִילוּת בְּרִיאָה יְצִירָה עֲשִׂיָּה,

**this takes place throughout the existence of the four Worlds, Atzilut, Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah.** 

וְהִנֵּה הָיָה גִּלּוּיוֹ בָּזֶה בִּשְׁנֵי בְּחִינוֹת,

**His revelation in this manner is of two types:** 

אֶחָד בִּבְחִינַת פְּעֻלָּה שֶׁחוּץ מִמֶּנּוּ,

**One is the type of activity outside of Himself;**

וְאֶחָד בִּבְחִינַת פְּעֻלָּה שֶׁהִיא אֶצְלוֹ 

**and the other is the type of activity that is with Him** 

וּמְיֻחָד עִמּוֹ עַד שֶׁנַּעֲשֵׂית חַד עִמּוֹ. 

**and is united with Him to the extent that it becomes one with Him.**

וְהִנֵּה הֵם שְׁתֵּים שֶׁהֵם הַב' בְּחִינוֹת הַנִּזְכָּרִים,

**Now these 'two that are four'[^1] as just mentioned** 

וְהֵן אַרְבַּע שֶׁהֵם הֵד' עוֹלָמוֹת אבי"ע.

 **are the four worlds of ABY"A** – Atzilut, Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah.

וְהָיְינוּ כִּי הִנֵּה מֵהַגִּלּוּי הַשְּׁלִישִׁי הַנָּ"ל שֶׁהוּא אָדָם קַדְמוֹן,

**That is to say, from the third revelation mentioned above – Adam Kadmon –**

אֲשֶׁר בּוֹ נִתְגַּלָּה רְצוֹנוֹ וּמַחְשַׁבְתּוֹ ית"ש לְהַמְצִיא מְצִיאוּת עַל תְּכוּנַת אָדָם

**in which His *ratzon*[^2] and *machashava*[^3] are revealed and produce an existence in the format of Adam,**

שֶׁהוּא בָּנוּי וּמְיֻסָּד עַל עֲשָׂרָה מַדְרֵגוֹת כח"ב חג"ת נהי"ם \[כֶּתֶר-חֲכָמָה-בִּינָה; חֶסֶד-גְּבוּרָה-תִּפְאֶרֶת; נֶצַח-הוֹד-יְסוֹד-מַלְכוּת\]

i.e. **with the ten levels** the ten *sefirot* ***keter-chochmah-binah; chesed-gevurah-tiferet; netzach-hod-yesod-malchut*****[^4]** 

וּמְסֻדָּר בַּג' קַוִּים חח"נ בְּיָמִין 

**arranged in three columns:  *chochmah*-*chesed-netzach* on the right;** 

בג"ה בַּשְּׂמֹאל

***binah-gevurah-hod*** **on the left;** 

כתי"ם בָּאֶמְצַע.

***keter-tiferet-yesod-malchut*** **in the middle** 

הִנֵּה יָצָא בְּמַחֲשַׁבְתּוֹ לַפֹּעַל וְהוֹצִיא מֵאוֹתוֹ הַגִּלּוּי הַשְּׁלִישִׁי שֶׁהוּא הָאָדָם קַדְמוֹן

**Thus it arose in His thought** (i.e. at the level of *machshava*) **to produce and emanate from that third revelation, Adam Kadmon,**

בְּכֹחוֹ וְעַל יָדוֹ,

**with the capability thereof and by means thereof,** 

וְהִמְצִיא אֶת כָּל הַעֲשָׁרָה מַדְרֵגוֹת שֶׁהֵם הָעֶשֶׂר סְפִירוֹת

**to bring out all of the ten levels, which are the ten *sefirot*,** 

כח"ב חג"ת נהי"ם בַּמְּצִיאוּת מַמָּשׁ,

***keter-chochmah-binah; chesed-gevurah-tiferet; netzach-hod-yesod-malchut*****[^5]** **into actual manifest existence.**

וְהוּא יְצִיאָתוֹ ית"ש מָה שֶׁיָּצָא מֵהַכֹּחַ אֶל הַפֹּעֵל, 

**This is His emergence, blessed is His Name, from potential to actual.**

Although the Baal HaLeshem does not mention it explicitly here, it is self-understood that this revelation is of course post-*tzimtzum* and *kav.* 

וְהוּא הַגִּלּוּי הָרְבִיעִי שֶׁנִּמְשַׁךְ וְיָצָא מֵהַגִּלּוּי הַשְּׁלִישִׁי 

**This is the fourth revelation, which is drawn from and emerges out of the third revelation,** Adam Kadmon, 

וּבוֹ יָצָא בִּפְעֻלָּה מַמָּשׁ.

**and by way of which it emerges into actuality.**

וְהִנֵּה אוֹתָהּ הַמְּצִיאוּת הִנֵּה הִיא מְצִיאוּת סְפִירִי וּבְהִירָה כְּזוֹ,

**Now that existence is so luminous and radiant**

עַד שֶׁאֵינוֹ נוֹפֵל עָלֶיהָ לֹא שֵׁם עֲשִׂיָּה וְלֹא שֵׁם יְצִירָה וְלֹא שֵׁם בְּרִיאָה כִּי הִיא זָכָה וְדַקָּה מְאֹד,

**that it** **cannot be labeled 'made' or 'formed' or 'created'** – terms associated with the lower worlds of Asiya, Yetzirah and Beriah,**[^6]** **because it is so pure and refined,** 

לְמַעְלָה הַרְבֵּה מִכָּל הָאֵיכוּת וְהַעֲצְמוּת דְּהֲבְּרִיאָה,

**that it far transcends the entire quality and essence of created being,** 

וְהַשֵּׁם שֶׁלָּהּ הוּא אֲצִילוּת, 

**and is named *'Atzilut*,*'*** 

עַל שֵׁם הַכָּתוּב "וְאָצַלְתִּי מִן הָרוּחַ אֲשֶׁר עָלֶיךָ", 

**as in the verse** (Numbers 11:17) **"I will emanate** (*v'Atzalti*) **from the spirit that is upon you** and place it on them" – on the seventy elders,

כִּי הִיא נֶאֶצְלָה מא"ק \[מְאָדָם קַדְמוֹן\] וְנִשְׁפַּע מִמֶּנּוּ,

**for it was emanated** (*ne'etzlah*) **from Adam Kadmon and is impacted by it,**

וּמִשּׁוּם שֶׁהוּא לְמַעְלָה וּמְרוּמָם מֵאֵיכוּת הָעֲצְמוּת דְּכָל נִבְרָא. 

**and because it is loftier than, and is elevated beyond the essential qualities of every created being.**

לָכֵן אִי אֶפְשָׁר לְשׁוּם נִבְרָא לְהַשִּׂיג אֶת מַהוּת אוֹתָהּ הַמְּצִיאוּת מָה שֶׁהִיא, 

**Therefore it is impossible for any created being to grasp the inner disposition of that existence and what defines it.**

וְהוּא מָה שֶׁקּוֹרֵא אוֹתָן הַסֵּפֶר יְצִירָה בְּשֵׁם עֶשֶׂר סְפִירוֹת בְּלִימָה, 

**This is why that they are called 'the ten *sefirot bli mah*'** – ten undefined *sefirot* – **in *Sefer Yetzirah*,[^7]** 

ור"ל שֶׁהֵם בְּלִי מַהוּת מֻשָּׂג.

**meaning that that they are without discernible character.**

וְכֵן אִי אֶפְשָׁר לְשׁוּם נִבְרָא לְהָבִין בְּדִיּוּק הוֹרָאַת וּבֵאוּר דְשֵּׁם וּמִילַת אֲצִילוּת מָה שֶׁהוּא, 

**It is similarly impossible for any created being to understand exactly the significance and meaning of the word *Atzilut*, and what it is,**

כִּי כָּל שֵּׁם הוּא נִמְשָׁךְ מֵעֶצֶם הַמְּצִיאוּת וּמִשֹּׁרֶשׁ הִתְהַוּוּתוֹ,

**because every name is drawn from the essential existence and the root from which it came into being,**

וְכַנּוֹדָע בַּמֶּה שֶׁכָּתוּב וְיִקְרָא הָאָדָם שֵׁמוֹת, וכמ"ש בְּתִקּוּנִים תִּקּוּן מ"ט**.**

**as is known from the verse** (Genesis 2:20) **"He** (Adam) **gave names** to all the animals…", **as it states in the *Tikunim*** (*Tikunei Zohar*) ***Tikun*** **49.** 

וְלָכֵן כְּשֵׁם שֶׁנֶּעֱלַם מִכָּל נִבְרָא לְהַשִּׂיג אֶת מַהוּת הָאֲצִילוּת 

**Therefore, just as the essence of Atzilut is impenetrable to the grasp of a created being,**

כֵּן נֶעֱלַם מִכָּל נִבְרָא לְהָבִין בְּדִיּוּק הוֹרָאַת דַּשָּׁם אֲצִילוּת ג"כ, 

**so too is it beyond the comprehension of every created being to understand precisely the significance of the name Atzilut,**

כִּי הוּא לְמַעְלָה וּמְרוֹמֵם מֵעֶצֶם אֵיכוּת כָּל נִבְרָא,

**since it is loftier than, and is elevated above the essential quality of every created being,**  

וַהֲרֵי אִי אֶפְשָׁר לְהַשִּׂיג ג"כ אֶת הוֹרָאַת שְׁמוֹ בְּדִיּוּק: 

**making it is impossible to also grasp the significance of its name precisely.**

אָמְנָם הוּא הִנֵּה כָּל הַנֶּאֱצָלִים כֻּלָּם

**Now although it is true that all things emanated** 

הִנֵּה הַגַּם שֶׁהֵם לְמַעְלָה הַרְבֵּה מִכָּל הַנִּבְרָאִים כֻּלָּם, 

**are much loftier than all of the other creations,**

עִם כָּל זֶה הִנֵּה הֵם מִצַּד עַצְמָם אֵינָם אֱלָהוּת כְּלָל,

**nevertheless, in and of themselves they are not Godliness at all,** 

כִּי הֵם נֶאֶצְלוּ וְנִמְצְאוּ מֵחָדָשׁ אַחַר שֶׁלֹּא הָיוּ,

**since they were emanated and emerged anew from non-existence** i.e. *ex nihilo*, 

וְאֵינָם אֱלָהוּת אֶלָּא מִצַּד יִחוּדוֹ יִתְבָּרֵךְ שְׁמוֹ בָּהֶם, 

**and they are not Godliness other than in terms of His unification with them, blessed be His Name.**

כִּי הָאֵין סוֹף הִתְיַחֵד בָּהֶם וְנַעֲשָׂה חַד מַמָּשׁ עִמָּהֶם

**Because the *Ein Sof* united Himself with them and He actually became one with them,** 

וְהֵם מֵעַתָּה אֱלָהוּת גָּמוּר עַל יָדוֹ יָחִיד וּמְיֻחָד,

**so that now they are wholly Godliness as a result of this, and are one and united** with Him. 

כִּי הֵם תְּכוּנָה יְקָרָה נָעֲלָה וּמֵרוֹמֵם כְּזוֹ,

**Thus they are** now **of a very precious and lofty character and are elevated to the extent that** 

שֶׁרְאוּיִם לְקַבֵּל אֶת יְחִדּוֹ בָּהֶם וּלְהַעֲשׂוֹת עִמּוֹ חַד מַמָּשׁ.

**they are worthy of receiving his Oneness in them to become one with Him literally.** 

אֲבָל הֵם מִצַד עַצְמָם זוּלַת יִחוּדוֹ בָּהֶם, אֵינָם אֱלָהוּת כְּלָל,

**But in and of themselves, without his unity in them, they are not Godliness at all,**

וכמ"ש אֵלִיָּהוּ זָכוּר לַטּוֹב בִּתְפִלָּתוֹ

**as Elijah (may he be remembered for good) said in his prayer[^8]:** 

"וְכַד אֻנַּת תִּסְתַּלֵּק מִנַּיְהוּ אִשְׁתַּאֲרֻן כֻּלְהוּ שְׁמָּהָן כְּגוּפָא בְּלֹא נִשְׁמָתָא".

**"if You were to remove Yourself from them, all those Names** i.e. from the *sefirot of Atzilut* **they would be like a body without a soul."**

אָמְנָם מֵאַחַר שֶׁהִתְיַחֵד עִמָּהֶם, וְהוּא מֵעֵת הַתִּקּוּן דְּמַעֲשֶׂה בְּרֵאשִׁית וּמֵאָז וּלְהָלְאָה,

**But since He united Himself with them – from the time of the *Tikun* during the Work of Creation and on –**

The account of Creation in the first chapter of Genesis reveals that first the World of Chaos, called *Tohu*, was created. *Tohu* was a deliberately unstable world that shattered and fell apart, and only then was the rectified world of Atzilut, known also as the World of *Tikun* (rectification), created. The World of *Tikun* rectifies the World of *Tohu*. His Godliness was not manifested in, or united with, the World of *Tohu*.   

הִנֵּה לֹא סָר וְלֹא יָסִיר יִחוּדוֹ מֵהֶם לְעוֹלָמִים,

 **He did not and will not ever remove his Oneness from them.**

וּמְיַחֵד עִמָּהֶם בְּיִחוּד עָצוּם מְאֹד עַד שֶׁנַּעֲשִׂים חַד עִמּוֹ.

**Thus He unites with them in a potent unity to the extent that they become one with Him,** 

ועי"ז נִתְגַּלָּה הוּא לְהִקָּרֵא בַּשֵּׁם עַל יְדֵיהֶם,

**and in this manner He becomes revealed, so that He can be called by Name through them,** 

כִּי הֵם עַצְמָם נַעֲשִׂים לִשְׁמוֹ, וְהוּא כָּל הַשֵּׁמוֹת הַקְּדוֹשִׁים כֻּלָּם, 

**since they themselves become His Name. And this is true of all the Holy Names**

אֲשֶׁר הוּא עַצְמוֹ נִקְרָא בָּהֶם ע"י הִתְלַבְּשׁוֹ בַּאֲצִילוּת

**by which He Himself is called through becoming clothed in Atzilut** 

וְהִתְיַחֲדוֹ עִמָּהֶם לֶהֱיוֹתָם חַד עַמּוֹ,

**and by unifying with them so that they become one with Him.** 

וע"י שֶׁהִתְלַבֵּשׁ בָּהֶם נַעֲשׂוּ הֵם כֻּלָּם ג"כ לַחַד וכמ"ש אֵלִיָּהוּ זל"ט

**Moreover, through His becoming clothed within them they also all become one in themselves, as Elijah declared:** 

"וְאֻנַּת הוּא דְּקָשִׁיר לוֹן וּמְיַחֵד לוֹן וּבְגִין דְּאַנְתְּ מִלְּגָאו

**"You are He who binds them together and unifies them, and because You are within them,** 

כָּל מָאן דְּאַפְרִישׁ חַד מִן חֲבְרֵיה מֵאִלֵן עָשָׂר סְפִירִין אֶתְחַשֵּׁב לֵיהּ כְּאִלּוּ אַפְרֵישׁ בָּך".

**anyone who separates one of them from the other of these ten *sefirot* is regarded as if he made a separation in You,"** 

כִּי הֵם מֵעַתָּה אֱלָהוּת גָּמוּר יָחִיד וּמְיֻחָד, 

**because from now on they are wholly Godliness, one and united,**

וְהוּא ע"י יִחוּדוֹ בָּהֶם, שֶׁנַּעֲשִׂים חַד עַל יָדוֹ וְחַד עַמּוֹ:

**by way of His oneness in them, and thus they are made into a unity by Him, and become one with Him.**

וְהוּא מ"ש הוּא ית"ש לֹא יִהְיֶה לְךָ אֱלֹהִים אֲחֵרִים עַל פָּנָי 

**This is what is stated** in the verse (Exodus 20:3), **"You shall not give credence to the gods of others in My Presence."**[^9]

ור"ל זוּלַת הָאֲצִילוּת שֶׁהוּא פָּנָיו, 

**Meaning to say** that this commandment applies even **outside of the world of Atzilut which is His 'Presence,'** 

כִּי שָׁם הוּא מִתְיַחֵד וּמִתְגַּלֶּה וְהוּא פָּנָיו מַמָּשׁ,

**since that is where** He is revealed **and there He is one and is** in a state of **revelation. This is** therefore referred to as **'His Presence', literally.**

וכמ"ש בזוה"ק פ' אֶמוֹר צ"ג ע"ב, בַּקְּשׁוּ פָנָי, אִלֵין עִטְרֵי מַלְכָּא (ר"ל הָעֶשֶׂר סְפִירוֹת)

**As is written in the Holy Zohar *parshat Emor* 93b** on the verse (Psalms 27:8) **"…seek My Presence[^10]" –** this refers to **those crowns of the King (i.e. the ten *sefirot*)** 

דְּאִהוּ אָחִיד בְּהוּ, וְאִנּוּן בֵּיהּ, אִינּוּן שְׁמֵיה, וְאִיהוּ וּשְׁמֵיה מִלָּה חֲדָא הוּא,

**with which He unites, and they are united with Him; they are His Name, and He and His Name are One.** 

בְּגִין כָּךְ אָמַר דָּוִד אֶת פָּנֶיךָ ה' אֲבַקֵּשׁ (ר"ל שֶׁיָאֵר פָּנָיו אֵלָיו), 

**For this reason King David declared** (Psalms ibid) **"Your Presence[^11], O Lord, I seek" (meaning to say** David prayed that **His Presence shine upon him).**

וְכֵן אָמְרוּ בזוה"ק יִתְרוֹ פ"ו א', עַל פָּנַי, לְאֲפָּקָא אַפֵּי מַלְכָּא.

**And so too, in the Holy Zohar *Yitro* 86a** on the verse (Exodus 20:3) **"in My Presence" – 'this excludes the Presence of the King** from the category of "the gods of others" or "other gods" mentioned in the above prohibition. 

דְּבְהוּ אִתְחַזִּי מַלְכָּא קַדִּישָׁא, וְאִנּוּן שְׁמֵיה וְהוּא אִנּוּן. 

**for in them** in the *sefirot* **the Holy King is revealed; they** the *sefirot* **are His Name** and through them He guides all the worlds**[^12]** **and He is them'** He is essentially the life-force of the *sefirot***[^13]**

הוּא שְׁמֵיה דִּכְתִיב אֲנִי ה' הוּא שְׁמִי, הוּא וּשְׁמֵיה חַד הוּא.

**'He is His Name', as is written** (Isaiah 42:8) **"I am God, this is My Name" –** that is to say, **'He and His Name are one!'[^14]**

וְכֵן כָּתַב ג"כ הרח"ו ז"ל בפ' שְׁמוֹת ג' ע"ב בְּהַגָּהוֹת מהרח"ו שָׁם אוֹת ד' ע"ש, 

**Rabbi Chaim Vital writes in similar vein** in a comment on the *Zohar, **Exodus*** **p. 3b,** and **in his *Hagahot Maharchu* para. 4.**    

וּלְזֶה רַמַז גַּם הָרַמְבָּ"ן בְּפָרָשַׁת יִתְרוֹ בְּפָסוּק ל"א לֹא יִהְיֶה לָךְ,

**The Ramban** (Nachmanides) **also alludes to this** interpretation in his commentary to **the section** **in** **Yitro on the** **verse** (20:3) **"You shall not give credence to the gods of others…"**

וְיֹאמַר כִּי בָּזֶה יָבֹא כָּל הַכָּתוּב כִּפְשׁוּטוֹ וּמַשְׁמָעוֹ, 

**And he declares that with this interpretation the entire verse** can be understood **simply and at its face value.**

וְכֵן כָּתְבוּ הָרְקַנַטִי וְהַצִּיּוֹנִי וְרַבֵּנוּ בְּחַיֵּי בְּפָרָשַׁת יִתְרוֹ שָׁם,

**And so too write the Recanati,[^15] the Tzioni[^16] and Rabbeinu Bachye[^17] on that** verse **in *Yitro*.**    

וכ"ז הוּא כִּי ע"י יִחוּדוֹ בַּאֲצִילוּת נַעֲשָׂה כָּל הָאֲצִילוּת כֻּלּוֹ אֱלָהוּת גָּמוּר, 

**This is all because of His unifying with** the World of **Atzilut, so that the entire Atzilut became wholly Godliness.** 

כִּי מֵעֵת הַתִּקּוּן אֲשֶׁר לְצֹרֶךְ מַעֲשֵׂה בְּרֵאשִׁית הִנֵּה הִתְיַחֵד א"ס ית"ש בַּאֲצִילוּת 

**Because from the time of the *Tikun* that was necessary** to rectify **the Work of Creation,** from that time on, **the Infinite One, blessed is He, united himself with the world of Atzilut.**

During the early process of creation the World of *Tohu* was formed. *Tohu* was a deliberately unstable world that shattered and had to be reconstructed as the World of *Tikun*, which is essentially the World of Atzilut,**[^18]** as explained in a previous note.

וְנַעֲשָׂה עִם הָאֲצִילוּת חַד גָּמוּר ועי"ז הֲוָה כָּל הָאֲצִילוּת כֻּלּוֹ אֱלָהוּת גָּמוּר יָחִיד וּמְיֻחָד.

**He made Himself absolutely one with the world of Atzilut, and thus the entire Atzilut is wholly Godliness, one and united.** 

אֲבָל זוּלַת יִחוּדוֹ בָּהֶם וְקֹדֶם שֶׁהִתְיַחֵד עִמָּהֶם,

**But without His oneness in them** in the *sefirot,* **prior to His uniting Himself with them** the *sefirot* of Atzilut 

הִנֵּה הֵם מִצַּד עַצְמָם אֵינָם אֱלֹהוּת כְּלָל וּכְלָל,

**in and of themselves, they are not Godliness at all.** 

וְלָכֵן אֵרַע בָּהֶם בְּעוֹלָם הַתֹּהוּ כָּל מָה שֶׁאֵרַע, וְיָצָא מֵהֶם אָז כָּל מָה שֶׁיָּצָא: 

**And therefore what occurred to them in the world of *Tohu* occurred,** the vessels shattered **and what transpired, transpired.**

וְהָאֱמֶת וְהָעִקָּר הוּא כִּי אָז לֹא הָיוּ נִקְרָאִים בְּשֵׁם אֲצִילוּת כְּלָל 

**The truth and the main point is that then** prior to this first *tikun* **they were not called 'Atzilut' at all** 

וְלֹא בְּשֵׁמוֹת הַסְּפִירוֹת כְּלָל,

**and not by the names of the *sefirot* at all.** Prior to this point all the revelations were transcendent and therefore undefinable, 

וכמ"ש באד"ר פ' נָשָׂא דַּף קל"ה ע"ב, וְכֻלְּהוּ אִתְקְרוּן בְּשְׁמָּהָן אַחֲרָנִין מִן קַדְמָאִין כו' ע"ש. 

**As is written in the *Idra Rabba*,[^19] page 135b: "…and all of them are now** in the World of Atzilut **called by names other than the initial ones."**

In this section of the *Zohar* Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai explains the fate of the seven *sefirot* of the World of *Tohu* which shattered and fell. The names given initially were Bela, which comes to be called *da'at* in Atzilut; Yovav becomes *chesed*; Chusham, *gevurah* etc.  

וּשְׁמוֹת הַסְּפִירוֹת וְשֵׁם אֲצִילוּת הוּא נִתְחַדֵּשׁ וְנַעֲשָׂה בָּהֶם רַק מֵעֵת הַתִּקּוּן 

Thus **the names of the *sefirot***, **and** indeed **the name Atzilut** itself**, were all innovated and became relevant to them only from the time of Tikun,** 

אֲשֶׁר לְצֹרֶךְ מַעֲשֵׂה בְּרֵאשִׁית וּמֵאָז וּלְהָלְאָה.

**for the needs of the Work of Creation and on.** 

וְהוּא מֵעֵת אֲשֶׁר הִתְיַחֵד הַמַּאֲצִיל ית"ש בָּהֶם לְהִקָּרֵא שָׁם בִּשְׁמוֹ.

**That is – from the time that the blessed Emanator unified Himself in them so that they could be called by His Name.** 

וְנַעֲשׂוּ מֵאָז וּלְהָלְאָה אֱלָהוּת גָּמוּר עַל יָדוֹ וְכַנִּזְכָּר.

**As a result, from then on, they** the *sefirot* of Atzilut **became wholly Godliness, as mentioned.** 

וְכֵן נִקְרְאוּ מֵאָז וּלְהָלְאָה בִּשְׁמוֹת הַסְּפִירוֹת 

**And they are also called from then on by the names of the *sefirot*** as we now know them:

כח"ב חג"ת נהי"מ, וּבְשֵׁם אֲצִילוּת,

***keter-chochmah-binah; chesed-gevurah-tiferet; netzach-hod-yesod-malchut,*** **and** that world itself is known **by the name Atzilut.** 

וְזֶהוּ מָה שֶׁאָמַר הָאֲרִיזָ"ל, בְּשַׁעַר הַצַּלָּם פ"א וּבְשַׁעַר הַשֵּׁמוֹת פ"א ובכ"מ,

**The following are the words of the Arizal in** *Etz Chaim **Sha'ar HaTzelem*** **chapter 1 and *Shaar HaShemot* chapter 1, and in a number of other places:** 

כִּי כָּל הָאֲצִילוּת כֻּלּוֹ הוּא אֱלָהוּת גָּמוּר אֶחָד יָחִיד וּמְיֻחָד, 

**All of Atzilut is wholly Godliness, one, unique and united.**

כִּי הֲרֵי מֵעֵת אֲשֶׁר זָכוּ לְשֵׁם הָאֲצִילוּת, 

**Because from the time that they were worthy of being called Atzilut,**

וְהוּא מֵאַחַר אֲשֶׁר הִתְיַחֵד עִמָּהֶם וְכַנִּזְכָּר, 

**i.e. from when He united Himself with them, as mentioned,**

הִנֵּה מֵאָז וּלְהָלְאָה הֲוָה הָאֲצִילוּת בֶּאֱמֶת אֱלָהוּת גָּמוּר עַל יָדוֹ וּכְכָל מָה שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ.

**from then on Atzilut was truly wholly Godliness, because of this and everything else we explained** above. 

וַע' בָּזֶה בִּדְבָרֵינוּ בַּסֵּפֶר הדע"ה ח"א דָּרוּשׁ ה' סִי' ז'.

**See further in *Sefer HaDe'ah* section 1 *drush* 5:7** 

שָׁם הֶאֱרַכְנוּ בָּזֶה וּבֵאֵרֵנוּ כָּל זֶה בֵּאֵר הֵיטֵיב, ע"ש וְתִמְצָא כָּל מְבֻקָּשֵׁךְ בַּעִנְיָנִים הַלָּלוּ:

**where we explain at length and clarify this subject well. Search there and you will find what you seek regarding these matters.** 

וְהִנֵּה כָּל הָעֶשֶׂר סְפִירָן כֻּלָּם הִנֵּה הַגַּם שֶׁהֵם נַעֲלָה וּמְרוּמָם בְּמַדְרֵגָה וּמַעְלָה 

**Now all the ten *sefirot*, even though they are of a very lofty and exalted status,** 

וּגְבוֹהִים מְאֹד מִתְכֻנַת כָּל נִבְרָא, 

**far above the qualities of all created beings,**

אָמְנָם בְּעַצְמוּתָם וּתְכוּנָתָם הַמִּתְיַחֵס לָהֶם

**nevertheless, the essence and the character associated with them** with the *sefirot* of Atzilut  

הִנֵּה יֵשׁ בָּהֶם מִצַּד עַצְמָם (ר"ל זוּלַת יִחוּדוֹ בָּהֶם)

**in and of themselves (aside from His unification in them) they have**  

כָּל הָעִנְיָנִים כֻּלָּם וְכָל הַסֵּדֶר כֻּלּוֹ כְּמוֹ בְּהַעוֹלֵמוֹת בי"ע.

**all of the** same **conceptual content, and the same entire order of that of the** lower **Worlds of Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah.**   

וְאַדְּרַבָּה הוּא, כִּי בָּהֶם הוּא הַשֹּׁרֶשׁ לְכָל זֶה.

**On the contrary, it is in them** the *sefirot* of Atzilut **that the root of all these** aspects of BiY"A is to be found, and therefore these similarities exist.  

וְהוּא כִּי גַּם עִקַּר כָּל מְצִיאוּתָם הֵם ג"כ רַק הַשָּׁרָשִׁים לְכָל הַמְצִיאוּת דבי"ע

**For their existence** the existence of the *sefirot* of Atzilut **is primarily to serve as the roots of the existence of BiY"A[^20]**

וְלָכֵן מְסֻדָּרִים הֵם ג"כ בְּכָל הָעִנְיָנִים אֶשְׁר בהבי"ע, בִּבְחִינַת שֹׁרֶשׁ לָהֶם.

**and therefore they** the *sefirot* of Atzilut **are arranged in exactly the same manner as in BiY"A, as constituting their root.** 

אֶלָּא שֶׁבְּכָל עוֹלָם וְעוֹלָם הוּא לְפִי עֶרְכּוֹ וּלְפִי מַהוּתוֹ וְעַצְמוּתוֹ הַמִּתְיַחֵס אֵלָיו. 

**The only difference being that** the manifestation of the *sefirot* **in each of the worlds is according to the character and essence associated with it** with each world. 

וּכְשֵׁם שֶׁמַּהוּת כָּל הָאֲצִילוּת הֲרֵי הוּא נֶעֱלַם מִכָּל נִבְרָא 

**Just as the inner nature of all of Atzilut is concealed from all created beings,**

מִשּׁוּם שֶׁהוּא נַעֲלָה וּמְרוֹמֵם מֵהֶם, 

**since it is so exalted and elevated above them,** 

כֵּן הוּא נֶעֱלָם מִלְּהַשִּׂיג אֵיךְ שֶׁעוֹמְדִים שָׁם כָּל אוֹתָן הָעִנְיָנִים כֻּלָּם ג"כ, גַּם בְּרוֹמְמוּת מְעַלְתֶּם, 

**so too is it masked from our comprehension how all the features** of the lower worlds **are also present there, even in the very loftiest echelons.**

אֲבָל עכ"פ הוּא כִּי לְפִי עֶרְכָם הַמִּתְיַחֵס לָהֶם

**But in any event this is so, and however worthy the stature attributed to them is,** 

הִנֵּה יֶשְׁנוֹ בָּהֶם כָּל הָעִנְיָנִים אֲשֶׁר בְּהַעוֹלָמוֹת בי"ע.

**all the same concepts manifested in the worlds of BiY"A are found in them too.** 

כִּי אַדְּרַבָּה הוּא שֶׁבַּאֲצִילוּת הוּא הַיְּסוֹד וְהַשֹּׁרֶשׁ לְכָל זֶה.

**And on the contrary, that which is in Atzilut is the foundation and root of all these** qualities in the lower worlds.

וָשָׁם הוּא הַכֹּל בְּעֶצֶם, וכמ"ש הַגְּרָ"א בַּסֵּפֶר יְצִירָה פ"א מְשַׁנָּה ב' אֹפֶן ג',

**But there everything is an integral part of the essence** of the World of Atzilut, **as the Gaon of Vilna writes** in his commentary to ***Sefer Yetzirah*** **1:2, third approach:**    

כִּי כָּל דָּבָר שֶׁהוּא בְּמִקְרֶה הֵן בְּשָׁרְשַָׁם בְּעֶצֶם

**Anything** that appears here **as happenstance has its roots in the essence,**

וְעֹמֵד שָׁם בֶּהֱוֵה תָּמִיד, כִּי כָּל הָאֲצִילוּת הֲרֵי הוּא אֱלָהוּת כַּנִּזְכָּר:

**where it is exists in the continuous present, since Atzilut is indeed Godliness, as mentioned.** 

וְהָעִנְיָן הוּא כִּי הִנֵּה הַמַּאֲצִיל ית"ש אֲשֶׁר נִתְפַּשֵּׁט וּמִתְלַבֵּשׁ בְּאוֹרוֹ 

**The concept is as follows: the blessed Emanator spread forth and clothed His light**

בְּכָל הַסְּפִירוֹת דַּאֲצִילוֹת וְנַעֲשָׂה חַד עִמָּהֶם 

**in all the *sefirot* of Atzilut, and became one with them.** 

הִנֵּה הוּא פָּעַל וּפוֹעֵל עַל יְדֵיהֶם אֶת כָּל הַמְּצִיאוֹת דבי"ע בְּכַמוּתָם וְאֵיכוּתָם וְכָל עִנְיְנֵיהֶם וּפְעֻלּוֹתֵיהֶם.

**Now by means thereof** i.e. via the *sefirot* of Atzilut **He brought into being, and He continues to sustain the existence of** the Worlds of **BiY"A in quantity and quality in all matters pertaining to them and in all their effects.**   

הַכֹּל ע"פ סֵדֶר אוֹתָן הָעִנְיָנִים הַמְּסֻדָּר בַּאֲצִילוּת לְפִי עֵרֶךְ הַמִּתְיַחֵס שָׁם,

**Everything is according to the structure of those matters as arranged in Atzilut, and according to the principles that pertain there.**   

וְזֶהוּ גִּלּוּיוֹ ית"ש אֲשֶׁר נִתְגַּלָּה בַּאֲצִילוּת.

**And this is His revelation as it is revealed in Atzilut,**

הַיְינוּ בִּמְצִיאוּת הִתְהַוּוּת הַנֶּאֱצָלִים עַצְמָן 

**that is to say, in bringing into being the emanations of Atzilut itself**

וְהִתְלַבְּשׁוֹ בָּהֶם וְיִחוּדוּ עִמָּהֶם בְּכָל סִדְרָם וְעִנְיָנָם,

**and clothing Himself within them and uniting with them throughout their structure and the principles** that pertain there, 

וְלִפְעֹל עַל יְדֵיהֶם אֶת כָּל הַמְּצִיאוּת דהבי"ע בְּכָל אוֹתוֹ הֶסְדֵּר וְהַעֲנִינִים ג"כ,

**and via them, the entire existence of BiY"A is imbued with that same entire structure and the principles** that pertain there **as well.**   

וע"י כ"ז הִנֵּה נִתְגַּלָּה לְהִקָּרֵא בַּשֵּׁם, 

**By means of all this He becomes revealed to be called by Name** 

כִּי ע"י הִתְלַבְּשׁוֹ וְגִלּוּיוֹ בַּאֲצִילוּת וּבְכָל עֲנִינִי הַנִּזְכָּר,

**– by clothing Himself** in the *sefirot* **and His revelations in Atzilut in all the matters mentioned,**  

הִנֵּה נִתְגַּלָּה לְהִקָּרֵא בִּשְׁמוֹ הַגָּדוֹל שֶׁהוּא הַשֵּׁם הֲוָיָה ב"ה

**He becomes revealed so that He can be called by His great Name, the blessed Name Havaye** the ineffable Name, 

וּבְכָל הַשֵּׁמוֹת הַמִּסְתַּעֲפִים מִמֶּנּוּ, וּכְמוֹ שֶׁאָמְרוּ לְפִי מַעֲשֵׂי אֲנִי נִקְרָא,

**and all the** other holy **Names that derive from that Name, as we explained** previously, **"according to My deeds I am called."**

כִּי הַשֵּׁמוֹת הֵם עַצְמָם עֶצֶם כָּל הַכֹּחוֹת וְהַפְּעֻלּוֹת, 

**This is because the Names themselves are those very powers and actions,**  

וַהֲרֵי יָצָא מֵעַתָּה לְהִקָּרֵא בְּשֵׁם.

**and thus He emerged from now on to be called by Name.**

וְהוּא גִּלּוּיוֹ הָרְבִיעִי מִמַּדְרֵגַת אֲמִתָּתוֹ הַנַּעֲלָמָה, 

**This is the fourth revelation from His true concealed being** 

וְהוּא בְּחִי' גִּלּוּיוֹ אֲשֶׁר בִּפְעֻלָּה מַמָּשׁ.

**– His revelation** of Himself **within His deeds, literally.** 

וּבָזֶה הִנֵּה הֲוָה הוּא ית"ש שֹׁרֶשׁ כָּל נִמְצָא וִיסוּד כָּל נִבְרָא.

**In this manner He becomes the root of everything in existence, and the foundation of all created things.** 

וְהוּא ע"פ מָה שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ שֶׁהָאֲצִילוֹת הוּא הַיְּסוֹד וְהַשֹּׁרֶשׁ דְּכָל הָעוֹלָמוֹת בי"ע עִם כָּל עִנְיְנֵיהֶם,

**This is concurs with our statement that Atzilut is the foundation and the root of all the Worlds of BiY"A and all of their aspects,**   

וְהוּא עִם הָאֲצִילוּת הֲרֵי הוּא חַד עִמָּהֶם כַּנָּ"ל

**and He,** as He clothes Himself in Atzilut and becomes one **with Atzilut, is one with them** (the lower worlds) as well, **as mentioned** above. 

וא"כ הֲרֵי נִתְגַּלָּה ע"י הִתְלַבְּשׁוֹ בַּאֲצִילוּת לִהְיוֹת שֹׁרֶשׁ הַכֹּל. וְהַבֵּן כ"ז הֵיטֵיב:

**This being so, He becomes revealed by clothing Himself in Atzilut where He becomes the root of everything. Understand all this well!**

וְהִנֵּה זֶהוּ שֶׁאָמְרוּ בַּזֹּהַר הַקֹּדֶשׁ וְיִקְרָא י"א ב' בְּעִנְיַן הָעֲשָׂרָה שֵׁמוֹת 

**This is in fact what the holy *Zohar* states (*Vayikra* 11b) regarding the Ten Names,** 

דְּעֶשֶׂר סִפַּי' דַּאֲצִילוּת שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ לְעֵיל פ"ב, 

**which correspond to the ten *sefirot* mentioned above in chapter 2.** 

אָמְרוּ שָׁם וְאִילֵין אִנּוּן כִּתְרִין קַדִּישִׁין דְּמַלְכָּא, דְּהוּא אִשְׁתָּמוּדַע בָּהוּ

**This is what they said there: "These are the holy crowns of the King; through them He is known**  

(שֶׁמִּתְלַבֵּשׁ בָּהֶם וּמִתְגַּלֶּה בָּהֶם בְּכָל הֲגִלּוּיָם הַנֵּז') וְאִינוּן שְׁמֵיה וְהוּא אִנּוּן עכ"ל.

**(because He is clothed within them and reveals Himself in them in all the manners of revelation mentioned). They are His Name, and He is them."**  

וְהוּא ע"פ מָה שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ כִּי כָּל הָאֲצִילוּת הוּא מֵעַתָּה הוּא עֲצְמוֹ ית"ש.

**This concurs with what we stated** earlier **that the entire Atzilut from now on is He himself, blessed is He,**

אֲשֶׁר נִתְגַּלָּה בָּזֶה הוּא עַצְמוֹ אַחֵר שֶׁיָּרַד מִמַּדְרֵגָה לְמַדְרֵגָה.

**for in this way He Himself is revealed, having descended level after level,** 

שֶׁהוּא מַדְרֵגָּה הָרְבִיעִית מֵאֲמִתָּתוֹ הַנֶּעֱלָמָה,

**this being the fourth level from His true hidden being.** 

וּבַמַּדְרֵגָה הָרְבִיעִי הַזֶּה הִנֵּה יָצָא בִּבְחִינַת פְּעֻלָּה מַמָּשׁ,

**It is at this fourth level that His actual effects emerge**  

וְנִתְגַּלָּה בּוֹ כִּבְיָכוֹל בִּבְחִינַת יְצִיאָה מִן הַכֹּחַ אֶל הַפֹּעַל,

**and where He becomes revealed, so to speak, as emerging from potential to actual.**

וְהִנֵּה הוּא גִּלּוּי גָּמוּר וְהוּא גִּלּוּי שְׁמוֹ, 

**This is full revelation – the revelation of His Name –**

וּכְמוֹ שֶׁיִּתְבָּאֵר עִקַּר הַדְּבָרִים בָּזֶה לְקַמָּן פ"ו בעה"י:

**the main points of which will be explained below in chapter 6, God willing.**
