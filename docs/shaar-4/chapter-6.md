**Sha'ar 4 Chapter 6**

**More on Revelations from Will to Actualization**

וְהִנֵּה אָמַרְנוּ כִּי אֵלּוּ ב' הַגִּלּוּיִים הַד' וְהָה' 

**We stated** in the previous chapter **that these two revelations, the fourth** i.e. the revelation in Atzilut **and the fifth** i.e. the revelation in BiY"A ( out of five in total)

הִנֵּה הֵם גִּלּוּיָיו ית"ש אֲשֶׁר נִתְגַּלָּה בִּבְחִי' פְּעֻלָּה. 

**are His revelations that are manifested in action.**

כִּי הַג' גִּלּוּיָם הָרִאשׁוֹנִים הֵם בִּבְחִי' רָצוֹן וּמַחֲשָׁבָה וְהִרְהוּר כַּנָּ"ל פ"ג. 

**The first three revelations are the aspect of *ratzon, machshava* and *hirhur* as discussed in chapter 3** of this section.

אַךְ הַב' גִּלּוּיִים הָאַחֲרוֹנִים שֶׁהֵם הֵד' וְהָהּ' 

**However, the latter two revelations, the fourth and the fifth,**

הִנֵּה הֵם גִּלּוּיָיו ית"ש בִּבְחִי' יְצִיאָה מֵהַכֹּחַ אֶל הַפֹּעַל. 

**are His revelations in the sense of emerging from potential to actual –**  

וְהֵם הָאֲצִילוּת והבי"ע, כִּי בָּהֶם שְׁנֵיהֶם נִתְגַּלָּה בִּפְעֻלָּתוֹ. 

**i.e. Atzilut and BiY"A, because it is in these two that His actions are revealed.**

וְלָכֵן כְּתִיב עַל שְׁנֵיהֶם לָשׁוֹן עֲשִׂיָּה 

**And this is why the term *asiyah* – action – applies to both of them,** 

וכמ"ש כִּי שֵׁשֶׁת יָמִים עָשָׂה ה' אֶת הַשָּׁמַיִם וְאֶת הָאָרֶץ 

**as the verse** (Exodus 20:11) **states, "For six days God made** *– asah* – **the heavens and the earth."** 

וְאָמַר שֵׁשֶׁת וְלֹא בְּשֵׁשֶׁת כִּי קַאי עַל הו"ק דַּאֲצִילוּת וְהֵם הַשֵּׁשֶׁת יְמֵי בְּרֵאשִׁית. 

**Now the verse states "six days"** שֵׁשֶׁת יָמִים **and not "*in* six days"** בְּשֵׁשֶׁת יָמִים **because it refers to the six extremities of Atzilut** the six *sefirot* comprising *ze'ir anpin*: *chesed, gevurah, tiferet, netzach, hod* and *yesod*. These are called 'extremities' because they will eventually come to define the parameters of space – up, down and four directions, right and left, front and back. **These are the six 'days' of Creation,**

וּכְמוֹ שֶׁרָמַז הָרַמְבָּ"ן זַ"ל שָׁם וְכֵן הוּא בזוה"ק בְּרֵאשִׁית ל"א סע"א וְע' בְּתִקּוּנִים רֵישׁ תִּקּוּן י"א. 

**as Ramban hints to** in his commentary *ad loc*, **and so too in the Holy *Zohar,* *Bereishit* 31a. See also *Tikunim,* beginning of *Tikun* 11**

וַהֲרֵי לָנוּ לְשׁוֹן מַעֲשֶׂה וּפְעֻלָּה גַּם עַל הָאֲצִילוּת. 

**So here we have an** example **of action and activity even in Atzilut.** 

כִּי בַּאֲצִילוּת וּמִכָּל שֶׁכֵּן בבי"ע הִנֵּה בָּהֶם נִתְגַּלָּה הַמַּאֲצִיל בִּבְחִי' יְצִיאָה מִן הַכֹּחַ אֶל הַפֹּעַל וְכַנָּז': 

**For in Atzilut, and all the more so in BiY"A, the Emanator is revealed in the aspect of emerging from potential to actual, as mentioned previously.** 

אָמְנָם הִנֵּה יֵשׁ חִלּוּק גָּדוֹל בֵּין בְּחִי' פְּעֻלָּתוֹ שֶׁבָּאֲצִילִית שֶׁהוּא גִּלּוּיוֹ הָרְבִיעִי 

**However, there is a tremendous difference between the aspects of His action in Atzilut, which is the fourth** stage of **His revelation**

לִפְעֻלָּתוֹ שבבי"ע שֶׁהוּא גִּלּוּיוֹ הַחֲמִישִׁי. 

**and His action in BiY"A, which is the fifth** stage of **His revelation.**

וְהוּא כִּי הֲרֵי כְּבָר אָמַרְנוּ שֶׁכָּל הָאֲצִילוּת הִנֵּה הוּא מְצִיאוּת נָעֲלָה וּמְרוֹמָם כְּזוֹ 

**And this is** the difference: **we already noted that Atzilut is a type of lofty and elevated existence**

אֲשֶׁר רְאוּיָה לְהִתְקַדֵּשׁ בְּאוֹר קְדֻשָּׁתוֹ ית"ש 

**that is worthy of being sanctified with the light of His holiness, may His Name be blessed,** 

וְהִתְיַחֵד אוֹרוֹ בָּהּ עַד שֶׁנַּעֲשֵׂית חַד עִמּוֹ וְהִיא מֵעַתָּה אֱלָהוּת גָּמוּר. 

**and He united His light with it** with Atzilut **to the extent that it became one with Him, and from that point on it became complete Godliness.** 

כִּי מֵעֵת שֶׁהִתְיַחֵד אוֹרוֹ בַּאֲצִילוּת שֶׁהוּא מִמַּעֲשֵׂה בְּרֵאשִׁית וָהָלְאָה 

**For the moment that He merged His light with Atzilut, which was from the act of Creation and on,**  

הֲוָה כָּל הָאֲצִילוּת הִיא וְהוּא כֻּלּוֹ חַד וְנִתְגַּלָּה ע"י הָאֲצִילוּת 

**all of Atzilut and He, became one. And this was revealed via Atzilut**

לְהִקָּרֵא בַּשֵּׁם שֶׁהוּא הַשֵּׁם הוי"ה ב"ה. 

**in that it came to be called by the Name, the Name *Havaye*, blessed is He.**

וא"כ נִמְצָא שֶׁכָּל פְּעֻלַּת הָאֲצִילוּת הוּא כִּבְיָכוֹל בְּחִי' פְּעֻלָּה בְּעַצְמוּתוֹ ית"ש, 

**Accordingly, it turns out that the entire procedure of activating Atzilut is, so to speak, an activity within His essence, blessed be His Name,**  

כִּי עַל יָדָהּ נַעֲשָׂה וְנִפְעַל הוּא עַצְמוֹ בְּהוֹרָאַת וְתֹאַר קְדֻשַּׁת הַשֵּׁם יהו"ה ב"ה 

**since this action effectuated that He Himself came to be expressed and described by the holiness of the Name *Havaye*,** 

וּבְכָל תֹּאַר הוֹרָאַת קְדֻשַּׁת הַשֵּׁמוֹת כֻּלָּם הַמִּסְתַּעֲפִים מִמֶּנּוּ. 

**and by all of the descriptions and expressions of all the holy names that derive from it.**

משא"כ פְּעֻלָּתוֹ שבבי"ע שֶׁהוּא גִּלּוּיוֹ הַחֲמִישִׁי 

**This is not so with respect to His activities in BiY"A, the fifth revelation** 

הִנֵּה פָּעַל אוֹתָם בַּבְחִי' פְּעֻלָּה כְּזוֹ שֶׁיִּהְיוּ חוּץ מִמֶּנּוּ. 

**which was effectuated in a manner that categorizes the activity involved as 'outside of Him'[^1]**

כִּי הִמְצִיא אוֹתָם כֻּלָּם בִּמְצִיאוּת נִבְדָּל וְנִפְרַד מֵאֱלָהוּת 

**since all** such activities **are produced as an existence separate and distinct from Godliness,**  

וְאֵין מְצִיאוּתָם גּוּפָהּ שׁוּם אֱלָהוּת כְּלָל וּכְלָל ח"ו. 

**and their existence itself is not Godly in any way,[^2] heaven forfend.** 

וְלָכֵן נִקְרָאִים כֻּלָּם בְּשֵׁם עוֹלָמוֹת הַנִּפְרָדִים, 

**For this reason all of them** the Worlds of BiY"A **are referred to as separated worlds**  

כִּי הֵם נִפְרָד וְנִבְדָּל מֵאֱלָהוּת, וּבָהֶם הוּא כָּל הַפֵּרוּד וְהָרִבּוּיִם. 

**since they are separated and distinct from Godliness, and in them in separation and multiplicity.**

וַעֲלֵיהֶם נֹאמַר לֹא יִהְיֶה לְךָ אֱלֹהִים אֲחֵרִים עַל פָּנָי ור"ל זוּלַת הָאֲצִילוּת כַּנָּ"ל פ"ד 

**Regarding them the verse** (Exodus 20:3) **states "You shall not recognize the gods of others in My presence" – meaning to exclude Atzilut, as noted above in chapter 4.**

The Worlds of BiY"A are separated from His presence, whereas "My presence" is manifested in Atzilut, as just explained.

כִּי הֵם כֻּלָּם רַק נִבְרָאִים נוֹצְרִים נַעֲשִׂים. 

**Because all of them** the Worlds of BiY"A **are only created, formed and made**, and are not unified with His presence.

וְאָסוּר בְּכָל חֹמֶר אִסּוּר לְכַנּוֹת וּלְתָאֵר עַל שׁוּם דָּבָר מֵהַמְּצִיאוּת שֶׁבְּהָעוֹלֵמוּת בי"ע 

**And** therefore **it is strictly forbidden to ascribe or assign to any aspect of the existence of the Worlds of BiY"A**

שׁוּם תֹּאַר אֱלֹהוּתוֹ יִתְבָּרֵךְ. 

**any description pertaining to His blessed Godliness.** 

וְלָכֵן נִקְרָא הוּא ית"ש עֲלֵיהֶם בְּשֵׁם אֲדֹנָ"י.

**And therefore in relationship to them He is called by the name Ad'nai,** 

שֶׁהוּא כְּמוֹ אָדוֹן עַל עֲבָדָיו כִּי הֵם אֵינָם מְיֻחָדִים בְּעַצְמוּתוֹ 

**like a master** (*adon*) **to his servants, who are not one with Him**, 

לְהִקָּרֵא בֶּאֱלֹהוּתוֹ יִתְבָּרֵךְ כְּלָל וּכְלָל ח"ו. 

**that His Godliness could be attributed to them at all, heaven forfend.**

משא"כ כָּל הָאֲצִילוּת כֻּלּוֹ הִנֵּה הוּא ית"ש מִתְיַחֵד שָׁם בְּיִחוּד עָצוּם מְאֹד 

**But this is not so as regards all of Atzilut, with which He is united with an excessively powerful unity**

עַד שֶׁנַּעֲשֵׂית חַד עַמּוֹ וְהִיא כֻּלָּהּ אֱלָהוּת גָּמוּר עַל יָדוֹ. 

**to the extent that it** Atzilut **becomes one with Him, and in its entirety it is completely Godly as a result.**   

וַהֲרֵי יֵשׁ חִלּוּק גָּדוֹל בֵּין אֵלּוּ ב' הֲגִלּוּיָם ג"כ 

**Thus there is a huge difference between these two revelations as well,**

הַגַּם שֶׁשְּׁנֵיהֶם הֵם בִּבְחִי' פְּעֻלָּה וְכַנִּז':

**even though both of them are** in the category of **activity** as opposed to *ratzon, machshava* and *hirhur* which are all 'inside' of Him, so to speak.

אָמְנָם מָה שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ שֶׁכָּל הָעוֹלָמוֹת בי"ע אֵינָם אֱלָהוּת כְּלָל 

**However, our statement that the Worlds of BiY"A are not Godliness at all,** 

וְהֵם עוֹלָמוֹת נִפְרָדִים הִנֵּה הוּא רַק מְצִיאוּתָם גּוּפָהּ. 

**that they are separated worlds, this only applies to their existence.**

אֲבָל בַּפְּנִימִית פְּנִימִיּוּתָם הִנֵּה הוּא ית"ש אֵינוֹ נִבְדָּל גַּם מֵהֶם כְּלָל וּכְלָל ח"ו. 

**However, in their innermost being, He is not separated from them at all, heaven forfend,**

כִּי מִתְפַּשֵּׁט תָּמִיד בְהֶאָרָתוֹ בָּהֶם, וּכְמוֹ שֶׁמִּתְבָּאֵר לְעֵיל שַׁעַר ג' 

**since His radiance continuously spreads forth in them, as explained in Shaar 3,** 

כָּל הַבְּחִי' דּאֹור אֱלָהוּת הַמִּתְפַּשֵּׁט וְעוֹמֵד בְּהַעוֹלֵמוּת בי"ע ע"ש. 

**that all aspects of the Godly light spread forth and are manifested in the Worlds of BiY"A. See there** in *Sha'ar* 3**.**

וְהִנֵּה עוֹמֵד בָּהֶם אוֹרוֹ ית"ש תָּמִיד בִּבְחִי' נָשְׁמָה לַנְּשָׁמָה 

**Now manifested in them** in the Worlds of BiY"A **continuously is His light**, **as the aspect of *neshama* of *neshama*** – the soul of their soul.  

וְעַל יָדוֹ הוּא כָּל קִיּוּמָם וְכֵן כָּל עִקַּר מְצִיאוּתָם וְהִתְהַוּוּתָם. 

**It is through this that they endure, and** in fact **their primary existence and coming into being** is also by virtue of this *neshama* of *neshama*. 

וְכֵן עַל יָדוֹ הוּא כָּל תַּהֲלוּכוֹתֵיהֶם בְּכָל מִקְרֵיהֶם שֶׁהוּא כָּל הַמַּעֲשִׂים כֻּלָּם. 

**It is also through this that their entire development and all the events that occur with them unfold; including all of their actions.**

אַךְ אוֹרוֹ וּקְדֻשָּׁתוֹ שֶׁבָּהֶם הוּא בְּהֶעְלֵם גָּדוֹל מְאֹד 

**However, His light and holiness in them is greatly concealed**

וְאֵינוֹ נִכָּר כְּלָל אֶלָּא מִצַּד תַּהֲלוּכוֹתֵיהֶם וְכָל מִקְרֵיהֶם וּמְאֹרְעוֹתֵיהֶם 

**and is not recognized at all, other than in their development and all the events and occurrences that take place with them,**

וכמ"ש בזוה"ק פ' וְיָרֵא ק"ג ב' קב"ה אִתְיְדַע וְלֹא אִתְיְדַע 

**as the holy *Zohar*** (vol. 1) **states (*Vayera* 103b): The Holy One, blessed is He, is known** as He manifests Himself in the lower Worlds **but is not known** in His essence,**[^3]**

בְּגִין דְּאִיהוּ נִשְׁמְתָא לְנִשְׁמְתָא רוּחָא לְרוּחָא גָּנִּיז וְטָמִיר מִכֹּלָא כו' ע"ש. 

**since He is the *neshama* of the *neshama*, the *ruach* of *ruach*, hidden and concealed from all. See there,**  

וְכֵן בפ' בְּהַר ק"ט ב' דְּאַנְתְּ הוּא נְשָׁמָה לִנְשָׁמוֹת כו' 

**and also in** (*Zohar* III) ***parshat Behar*** **109b: "You are the *neshama* of *neshamas…***

אֲנְּת לְבַר מִכֹּלָא וּלְגָו מִכֹּלָא וּלְכָל סִטְרָא וּלְעֵלָּא מִכֹּלָא וּלְתַתָּא מִכֹּלָא כו'. 

**You are beyond all things and within all things, in all directions and above and below…"**

וְכֵן בְּפ' פִּנְחָס רכ"ה א' אִיהוּ סוֹבֵב עַל כָּל עָלְמִין כו' אִיּהוּ מְמַלֵּא כָּל עָלְמִין כו'. 

**Also in** (*Zohar* III) ***parshat Pinchas*** **125a: "He transcends all worlds… and He fills all worlds…"** the transcendent light is called *sovev kol almin,* and the immanent light filling the worlds is called *memalei kol almin*.

וְכֵן הוּא בְּהַקְדָּמַת תִּקּוּנִים ז' א' 

**The Introduction to the Tikunim** (*Tikunei Zohar*) **p. 7a**, also makes the same point.

\[וְנוֹדַע הוּא מָה שֶׁהֶעֱמִיקוּ בָּזֶה אֵיזֶה חֲכָמִים זַ"ל 

**\[It is known that there are some Sages who delved into this deeply,**

אָמְנָם הָאֲרִיזָ"ל אֲשֶׁר גִּלָּה לָנוּ כָּל סוֹד וְכָל סֵתֶר נֶעֱלַם לֹא פֵּרֵשׁ כֵּן. 

**but the Arizal, who revealed to all of us all the secrets and all hidden matters, did not explain it that way.**

וְדַעְתּוֹ הוּא בְּעִנְיַן מְמַלֵּא וְסוֹבֵב רַק ע"פ מ"ש בע"ח שַׁעַר עִיגֻלִּים וְיֹשֶׁר עָנָף ב' וְעָנָף ד'.

**His view regarding *sovev*** *kol almin* **and *memalei*** *kol almin* **is only according to what it states in *Eitz Chaim, sha'ar Igulim v'Yosher, anaf* 2 and 4** 

שֶׁאוֹרוֹ ית"ש מִתְלַבֵּשׁ בְּכֻלָּם בִּפְנִימִיּוּתָם וְכֵן הוּא מַקִּיף אוֹתָם מִבַּחוּץ. 

**that His light is clothed within all of them in their innermost being** (*memalei kol almin*), **and He also envelops them from without** (*sovev kol almin*),

וּכְמוֹ שֶׁיִּתְבָּאֵר קְצָת סֵדֶר הַהִתְלַבְּשׁוּת לְקַמָּן שַׁעַר ה'. 

**as will be explained somewhat** – regarding the **order of 'enclothing'** – **below, *Sha'ar* 5**.

'Enclothing' is the process whereby higher spiritual forces and profiles (called *kochot* and *partzufim*) affect and influence lower ones from within, rather than at a distance.   

וְכֵן אָמַר הָאֲרִיזָ"ל מְפֹרָשׁ בַּסֵּפֶר שַׁעַר הַקְדָּמוֹת דַּף ו' ע"א 

**Indeed the Arizal stated this explicitly in the work *Sha'ar Hakdamot* p. 6a,**

וְכֵן בִּמְבוֹא שְׁעָרִים שַׁעַר א' פ"ב וְכֵן בְּסֵפֶר מַאַמְרֵי רַשְׁבִּ"י פ' בְּהַר ע"ש וְתִמְצָא מְפֹרָשׁ\].

**and in *Mevo Sha'arim, Sha'ar* 1 chapter 2, and in the work *Mamarei Rashbi, Behar* where you will find it stated explicitly.\]**  

וְאוֹתוֹ הַהִתְלַבְּשׁוּת שֶׁהוּא מִתְלַבֵּשׁ גַּם בְּכָל הבי"ע כֻּלָּם 

**This 'enclothing' – in which He is clothed throughout BiY"A as well –**   

הוּא אוֹר שְׁכִינָתוֹ ית"ש שֶׁהוּא הַמַּלְכוּת דַּאֲצִילוּת 

**is the light of His *Shechinah*, which is *malchut* of Atzilut** 

הַשָּׁרוּי וְשׁוֹכֵן בִּפְנִימִית דִּפְנִימִית דְּכָל הָעוֹלָמוֹת בי"ע כֻּלָּם. 

**that reposes and dwells in the innermost being of all the Worlds of BiY"A,**

וּמִמֶּנּוּ הוּא עֶצֶם כֹּחַ דְּכָל הִתְהַוּוּתָם תָּמִיד וְכֵן כָּל מָה שֶׁנַּעֲשָׂה בָּהֶם תָּמִיד. 

**and from it** from the *Shechinah* **is itself the very force that maintains their continued existence, and whatever transpires with them at all times.**

כִּי הוּא הַנְּשָׁמָה לַנְּשָׁמָה רוּחָא לְרוּחָא כְּנִז'. 

**For He is the *neshama* of the *neshama*,** and **the *ruach* of the *ruach*, as mentioned.** 

In other words, the *sine qua non* of their existence is only Godliness, so that without Godliness at their innermost core, they would simply not exist. 

אָמְנָם הוּא בָּהֶם בְּהֶעְלֵם גָּדוֹל, וְכָל גִּלּוּיוֹ הוּא רַק בְּהַתּוֹרָה ע"י יִשְׂרָאֵל. 

**He is in them, however, in a deeply concealed manner, and all His revelations are only in the Torah and through Israel,**  

כִּי בְּקִיּוּמָהּ וּשְׁמִירָתָהּ מִתְפַּשֵּׁט וּמִתְגַּלֶּה אוֹר קְדֻשָּׁתוֹ 

**because through observing it** i.e. fulfilling the positive commandments, **and guarding it** guarding against transgressing the prohibitions, **the light of His holiness expands and becomes revealed.**  

וְיַכִּירוּ וְיָדְעוּ כֹּל יֹשְׁבֵי תֵבֵל כִּי הוּא לְבַדּוֹ עָשָׂה וְעוֹשֶׂה לְכָל הַמַּעֲשִׂים. 

**So that all the inhabitants of the earth will come to recognize and know that He alone did and does all actions,** 

וכמ"ש (יְשַׁעְיָה כט, כג-24) כִּי בִרְאֹתוֹ יְלָדָיו מַעֲשֵׂה יָדַי בְּקִרְבּוֹ יַקְדִּישׁוּ שְׁמִי כו' וְיָדְעוּ תֹעֵי רוּחַ בִּינָה כו'. 

**as the verse** (Isaiah 29:23-24) **states, "when he** (Jacob) **sees his children, My handiwork in their midst, who will sanctify My Name… Those of misguided spirit will attain understanding…**

שֶׁיַּכִּירוּ הַכֹּל כִּי ה' הוּא הָאֱלֹהִים אֵין עוֹד מִלְבַדּוֹ. 

**All will come to recognize that *Havaye*** God in His transcendence **is** ***Elokim*** God enclothing Himself in the world in an immanent manifestation **– there is none other than Him** as the verse (Deuteronomy 4:35) states.

וְהוּא אוֹר אֱלֹהוּתוֹ ית"ש הָעוֹמֵד בבי"ע שֶׁהוּא גִּלּוּיוֹ הַחֲמִישִׁי וְכַנָּ"ל: 

**And this is the light of His blessed Godliness which inheres in BiY"A, which is His fifth revelation, as mentioned above.**

וַהֲרֵי נִתְבָּאֵר לָנוּ עִנְיַן גִּלּוּיָיו ית"ש בְּדֶרֶךְ כְּלָל 

**Thus we have clarified for ourselves, in general, the concept of His revelations, may His name be blessed,** 

כִּי הִנֵּה הֵם בַּה' מַדְרֵגוֹת כְּלָלִיִּים:

**in which there are five general levels:**

הַג' הָרִאשׁוֹנִים הֵם בִּבְחִי' רָצוֹן וּמַחֲשָׁבָה וְהִרְהוּר. 

**The first three are** called ***ratzon, machshava*** **and *hirhur,*** as explained previously.

וְהֵם גִּלּוּיוֹ אֲשֶׁר בִּבְחִי' א"ס שֶׁקֹּדֶם הַצִּמְצוּם וּלְמַעְלָה מֵהַצִּמְצוּם. 

**These are:** 1\) **His revelation as the aspect of *Ein Sof***,**[^4]** **prior to, and above the *tzimtzum*;**

וְהָעוֹלָם דְּקַּו וְצִמְצוּם שֶׁנִּקְרָא אֲוִיר הַקַּדְמוֹן וְטַהִירוּ עִלָּאָה 

2\) **The realm of the *kav* and the *tzimtzum*[^5] which is called *avir kadmon* and *t'hiru ila'a*;**

וְהָעוֹלָם דְּאָדָם קַדְמוֹן הֵם הָעוֹלָמוֹת דא"ס. 

3\) **The World of Adam Kadmon. These are the three** infinite revelations, called **'the infinite worlds.'** 

וְשָׁנִים הָאַחֲרוֹנִים הֵם בִּבְחִי' פְּעֻלָּה 

**The other two** of the five revelations mentioned above **are the aspect of 'action':**

וְהוּא אֶחָד הָעוֹלָם דַּאֲצִילוּת שֶׁהוּא כֻּלּוֹ אֱלָהוּת גָּמוּר 

**One of them is the World of Atzilut, which is all complete Godliness,**

וְהַשֵּׁנִי הָעוֹלָמוֹת בי"ע שֶׁהֵם עוֹלָמוֹת הַנִּפְרָדִים: 

**and the second is the Worlds of BiY"A, which are the separated worlds.**

וְהִנֵּה בְּמָה שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ שֶׁגִּלָּה ית"ש בִּבְחִי' פְּעֻלָּה הוּא רַק בַּאֲצִילוּת ובבי"ע 

**Now when we said that He revealed Himself in the category of 'activity' only in Atzilut and BiY"A,**

וְאָמַרְנוּ שֶׁהֵם ב' פְּעֻלּוֹת מְחֻלָּקוֹת וְכַנִּז'. 

**and we also noted that these are two separate activities** i.e. emanating Atzilut and producing BiY"A constitute two different actions.

הִנֵּה יוּבַן לָנוּ בָּזֶה שֹׁרֶשׁ הַדְּבָרִים אֲשֶׁר אָמַרְנוּ לְעֵיל שַׁעַר ג' פ' ה' ו' 

**We can now understand this matter in its root discussed above *Sha'ar* 3 chapters 5 and 6:**

שֶׁהַנִּבְרָאִים הֵם רַק שְׁנַיִם שֶׁהֵם רוּחַ וְנֶפֶשׁ, 

We stated over there **that there are only two entities** in the category of **'created'** – ***ruach*** **and *nefesh***, whereas *neshama, chaya* and *yechida* are not in the category of *created*, as the author is about to state, since they are 'within' the Creator.

אֲבָל הַנְּשָׁמָה, וּמִכָּל שֶׁכֵּן הֶחָיָה וְהַיְּחִידָה, הֵם לְמַעְלָה מֵהַבְּרִיאָה. 

**But *neshama,* and all the more so, *chaya* and *yechida*, are above creation.**

וְהוּא כִּי הֲרֵי אָמַרְנוּ לְעֵיל פ"ג שָׁהַה' גִּלּוּיִם הַנִּז' הֵם הַשָּׁרָשִׁים להנרנח"י. 

**And as we stated above** *Sha'ar* 4 **chapter 3 the five revelations mentioned there are the roots of *nrnch"y*,[^6]** 

וּכְשֵׁם שֶׁהַג' גִּלּוּיִם הָרִאשׁוֹנִים הֵם בִּבְחִי' אוֹר א"ס וְהֵם לְמַעְלָה מֵהַפְּעֻלָּה 

**and just as the first three revelations there are in the category of *Ohr Ein Sof*, transcending** the category of **action,**

כֵּן הוּא הַיְּחִידָה חָיָה נְשָׁמָה לְמַעְלָה מֵהַבְּרִיאָה שֶׁהוּא לְמַעְלָה מֵהַגּוּף. 

**so too, *yechida, chaya* and *neshama* transcend creation, corresponding to the body, which they transcend.**

וְכֵן הוּא עוֹד כִּי כְּשֵׁם שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ שֶׁפְּעֻלָּתוֹ אֲשֶׁר בהבי"ע שֶׁהוּא גִּלּוּיוֹ הַחֲמִישִׁי 

**Furthermore, just as we explained that His activity in BiY"A is the fifth revelation,**

הִנֵּה פָּעַל אוֹתָם שֶׁיִּהְיוּ חוּץ מִמֶּנּוֹ שֶׁהֵם אֵינָם אֱלָהוּת כְּלָל וְכַנָּ"ל, 

**which He produced to exist 'outside' of Himself,** in the sense that **they are not Godliness at all,**  

כֵּן הוּא ג"כ בְּחִי' הַנֶּפֶשׁ שֶׁהִיא כְּנֶגְדּוֹ כִּי הִיא הֶעָנָף דְּהַגִּלּוּי הַחֲמִישִׁי, 

**so too, as regarding the corresponding aspect of *nefesh*** (which corresponds to BiY"A) – **it is** merely **a branch of the fifth revelation,**

שֶׁהַגִּלּוּי הַחֲמִישִׁי הוּא שָׁרְשָׁהּ וְכַנִּז'.

**for the fifth revelation is its root, as mentioned.**

הִנֵּה הֲוָה הַנֶּפֶשׁ לְעוֹלָם בִּבְחִי' לְבַר מִגּוּפָא שֶׁהִיא אֵינָהּ מֵעִקָּר הָאָדָם. 

**Accordingly, the aspect of *nefesh* is always in the category of extrinsic since it is not part of the intrinsic aspects of man**

כִּי עִקַּר הָאָדָם הוּא רַק הָרוּחַ חג"ת \[חֶסֶד, גְּבוּרָה, תִּפְאֶרֶת\]

**since the primary aspect of man is only *ruach,*** corresponding to ***chesed, gevurah, tiferet*****,**  

אֲבָל הַנֶּפֶשׁ נהי"ם \[נֶצַח, הוֹד, יְסוֹד, מַלְכוּת\] 

**whereas *nefesh*** corresponding to ***netzach, hod, yesod, malchut*** 

הִיא רַק הַשֹּׁרֶשׁ לְמָה שֶׁלְּמַטָּה מִמַּדְרֵגַת הָאָדָם 

**is merely the root of what is below the level of man,**

וְהִיא עוֹמֶדֶת בְּהָאָדָם רַק לְקַשֵּׁר אוֹתוֹ 

**and only exists in man in order to connect him**

לְכָל מָה שֶׁלְּמַטָּה מִמֶּנּוּ שֶׁהוּא לְקַשָּׁרוֹ עִם הַגּוּף וְהָעוֹלָם. 

**to whatever is below him – i.e. to connect him to the body and to the world.**

וְלָכֵן נִקְרָא הַנֶּפֶשׁ לַבַּר מִגּוּפֵיה

**This is why the *nefesh* is referred to as extrinsic,** 

(וכמ"ש בזוה"ק בְּרֵאשִׁית כא ע"ב. ופ' תּוֹלְדוֹת קמו א'. ופ' וְיִשְׁלַח קסו א'. ופ' פְּקוּדֵי רנז ב'. ופ' נָשָׂא קמ"ג א') 

**(as stated in *Zohar*** vol. I ***Bereishit* 21b, *Toldot* 146a;** vol. II ***Vayishlach*** **166a, *Pekudei* 257b;** vol. III ***Nasso*** **143a)**

כִּי הִיא כְּנֶגֶד גִּלּוּיוֹ הַחֲמִישִׁי וְכַנָּז' וְהַבֵּן: 

**since it corresponds to His fifth revelation, as explained. Understand this.**

וְדַע כִּי כָּל הָהּ' גִּלּוּיִם שֶׁאָמַרְנוּ הִנֵּה הָיָה שִׁעוּר מְצִיאוּת הַגִּלּוּי דְּכָל עֶלְיוֹן 

**Know also that regarding all five revelations we mentioned – the degree of disclosure of all higher revelations** 

רַק לְפִי צֹרֶךְ הַגִּלּוּי דְּהַתַּחְתּוֹן שֶׁיּוֹצֵא מִמֶּנּוּ 

**is commensurate with the need for revelation of the levels below, that emerge from it** from the higher level

כִּי הֵם כֻּלָּם רַק לִהְיוֹת סִבָהּ זֶה לְזֶה. 

**since they all constitute the cause one for the other.** 

וְלָכֵן כָּל הַבְּחִינוֹת שֶׁיֶּשְׁנָם בָּזֶה יֶשְׁנָם בָּזֶה, וְהֵם כֻּלָּם בְּסֵדֶר אֶחָד, 

**And therefore all the aspects that exist in one** level, **exist in the other one** as well, **and all of them constitute a single arrangement,**

אֶלָּא שבכ"א הוּא לְפִי עֶרְכּוֹ 

**except that each of them is according to its** spiritual **status,** 

וְכָל עֶלְיוֹן בַּכֹּל בְּחִינוֹתָיו הוּא הֲסִבָּה לְהֲתַחְתּוֹן וּלְכָל בְּחִינוֹתָיו. 

**and every higher level in all of its aspects is the cause for the lower level in all of its aspects.**

וְכֵן הוּא תַּכְלִיתוֹ ג"כ כִּי הֲסִבָּה עַצְמוֹ הוּא הַתַּכְלִית 

**And so too is it regarding its peak aspiration, because its cause *is* its peak aspiration,**

שֶׁתַּכְלִית כָּל דָּבָר שֶׁחוֹזֵר לְסִבָתוֹ וּלְסִבַת סִבָתוֹ עֲד הֲסִיבָּה הָרִאשׁוֹנָה: 

**since the peak aspiration of every entity is to return to its cause, and to the cause of its cause,** all the way **up** **until the First Cause.**

וְהִנֵּה סִדֵּר הַמַּאֲצִיל ית"ש אֶת כָּל אוֹתָן הֲגִלּוּיִם כֻּלָּם 

**Now the blessed Emanator arranged all of those revelations** in a manner that 

שֶׁיְּהֵא כָּל עֶלְיוֹן נִצְרָךְ לְהַתְחְתּוֹן (ר"ל שֶׁהַתַּחְתּוֹן צָרִיךְ לְהַעֶלְיוֹן) בְּהֶכְרֵחַ וְחִיּוּב, 

**the higher** level **is needed by the lower** level **(i.e. the lower needs the higher[^7]) of necessity and as a requisite**

כִּי הֲרֵי רַק הוּא סִבָתוֹ וְתַכְלִיתוֹ. 

**since** the higher level **is its only cause and its peak aspiration.**

אֲבָל כָּל תַּחְתּוֹן הוּא נִצְרָךְ לְהָעֶלְיוֹן רַק בְּכַוָּנָה וְרָצוֹן, 

**Conversely, every lower** level **is needed by the higher** level **only in terms of intention and desire** ('desire' in the sense of will)

כִּי עָלָיו הָיָה כַּוָּנָתוֹ וּרְצוֹנוֹ, וְרַק לְזֶה נִתְפַּשֵּׁט וְנִתְגַּלָּה, 

**since this** the lower level **was the intention and desire of** the higher level, **and it was only for this** purpose **that it spread forth and became revealed,**

וְהָיָה כָּל גִּלּוּיוֹ רַק מָה שֶׁנּוֹגֵעַ לְהֲתַחְתּוֹן לִהְיוֹת סִבָתוֹ. 

**so that its entire revelation is only that which is relevant becoming the cause of the lower** level. 

וא"כ נִמְצָא שֶׁהֵם כֻּלָּם בֶּאֱמֶת סִבּוֹת זֶה לְזֶה 

**Thus it turns out that they are all actually causes for one another,**

וְכֵן הֵם כֻּלָּם תַּכְלִיּוֹת זֶה לְזֶה, 

**and also the peak aspiration for each other.**

כִּי הֲרֵי כְּשֵׁם שֶׁכָּל עֶלְיוֹן הוּא הֲסִבָה וְהַתַּכְלִית לְהֲתַחְתּוֹן כַּנִּז', 

**Because just as every higher** level **is the cause and the peak aspiration of the lower level, as mentioned,**

כֵּן הוּא ג"כ כָּל תַּחְתּוֹן סִבָּה וְתַכְלִית לְהַעֲלְיּוֹן, 

**so too is every lower** level **the cause and the peak aspiration of the higher** level,

שֶׁהֲרֵי סִבַּת וְתַכְלִית הַגִּלּוּי דְּכָל עֶלְיוֹן 

**since the cause and purpose of the revelation of the higher** level

הוּא כְּדֵי לְהָבִיא עַל יָדוֹ אֶת הַשֵּׁנִי אֵלָיו עַל מְצִיאוּתוֹ וְתַכְלִיתוֹ. 

**is to thereby bring the second one** the lower level **up to it** to the level of the cause **existentially and in terms of what it ultimately aspires to** 

וּבָזֶה יֻשְׁלַם תַּכְלִיתוֹ דְּהָעֶלְיוֹן ג"כ וְהַבֵּן כָּל הַדְּבָרִים: 

**And in this way the ultimate purpose of the higher** level **is also fulfilled. Understand these matters well.**

וְהִנֵּה בְּכָל אֵלּוּ הָהּ' גִּלּוּיִם כֻּלָּם יֵשׁ רְשׁוּת לְהַלֵּב לְחַשֵּׁב וּלְהַשְׂכֵּל לְהִתְבּוֹנֵן 

**Now regarding all these five revelations, the heart has permission to think, theorize and meditate**

בְּכָל סִדְרֵי מְצִיאוּתָם וּבְהַרְצוֹן שֶׁבָּהֶם. 

**on the entire order of their existence and the Divine will invested in them.**

אֲבָל לְמַעְלָה מֵאֵלּוּ הָהּ' גִּלּוּיָם בְּלֹם פִּיךָ מִלְּדַבֵּר וְלִבְּךָ מִלְּהַרְהֵר, 

**But beyond these five revelations, "prevent your mouth from speaking, and your heart from ruminating"** as the *Sefer Yetzirah* (1:8) states in connection with the *sefirot* of Atzilut.**[^8]**

וְגַם הַהִתְבּוֹנְנוּת בָּאֵלּוּ הָהּ' אֵינוֹ בְּכֻלָּם שָׁוֶה. 

**And even as regards contemplating these five – not all are equal,**

וְלֹא הֻתַּר אֶלָּא בְּכָל אֶחָד לְפִי עֶרְכּוֹ בְּכָל יוֹתֵר עֶלְיוֹן רַק בִּכְלָלוּת וְרַק בְּמַחְשָׁבָה, 

**so that it is only permitted to contemplate each one according to its stature, and the more elevated it is the more general** the idea **must be, and only in thought,**

וְגַם זֶה רַק בְּרָצוֹא וָשׁוֹב, 

**and even then, only in a manner of *ratzo v'shov*,[^9]** literally, 'to and fro' i.e. not for extended periods of time. As soon as one's thoughts soar up to the highest levels one must immediately return to where one is grounded. 

וּבְיוֹתֵר תַּחְתּוֹן גַּם בִּפְרָטוֹת וּבְדִבוּר,

**Whereas regarding lower levels** it is permitted **even in detail and in speech.**

אָמְנָם הֱתֶר הַדִּבּוּר שֶׁבּוֹ הוּא רַק לְפִי עֵרֶךְ הָעֶלְיוֹן שֶׁעָלָיו, 

**Nevertheless, permission to speak** about these levels **is only in comparison with what is above it,**

אֲבָל לֹא בְּעֵרֶךְ הֲתַּחְתוֹן שֶׁלְּמַטָּה מִמֶּנּוּ. 

**but not in comparison with what is below it.** In other words, speaking about higher levels is comparatively restricted, and the lower the level, the more it is permitted to speak about it. Thus the degree to which one can discuss a lower level clearly does not transfer to the next level up.

כִּי בָּזֶה נִבְחַן הוּא לְעֶלְיוֹן וּבְכָל מִשְׁפָּטָיו וְחֻקּוֹתָיו. 

**Indeed, this is what distinguishes it as a higher level as regards all of its rational and super-rational laws.**

וְגַם הַהִתְבּוֹנְנוּת הַמֻּתָּר בְּכָל אֶחָד 

**Also, regarding the contemplation which is allowed at each level**

צְרִיכִים לִזַהֵר מְאֹד לָדַעַת שֶׁהוּא רַק לְפִי עֶרְכּוֹ הַמִּתְיַחֵס אֵלָיו, 

**one must take exceptional care that it is only according to the** spiritual **stature thereof** of that level. 

וְהַבֵּן גַּם כָּל זֶה (וְהַמְּעֲיִן בִּדְבָרֵינוּ בְּסֵפֶר הַכְּלָלִים וּבְסֵפֶר הדע"ה וּבְחֵלֶק הַבֵּאוּרִים 

**Understand this too (and one who examines our words in *Sefer HaKlalim* and in *Sefer HaDe'ah*, and in the elucidations** at the end of this work

יִמְצָא מְבֹאָר כָּל אוֹתָן הַדְּבָרִים כָּפוּל וּמְשֻׁלָּשׁ כַּמָּה פְּעָמִים):

**will find these matters explained two and three times over.**

אָמְנָם כָּל אֵלּוּ הָהּ' גִּלּוּיִם הִנֵּה הֵם נִמְשָׁכִים זֶה מִזֶּה 

**Nevertheless, all of these five revelations are drawn one from the other,**

וּמֻשְׁפָּעִים זֶה מִזֶּה וּקְשׁוּרִים וּמְיֻחָדִים זב"ז, 

**and are affected by one another and are bound to each other and are united one with another –** 

כָּל תַּחְתּוֹן בֶּהָעֶלְיוֹן שֶׁעָלָיו עַד אֲמִתָּתוֹ הַפְּשׁוּטָה הַנַּעֲלָמָה

**each lower level with the one above it, all the way up to His true, simple** in the sense of uncompounded **hidden Being**

אֲשֶׁר לְמַעְלָה מִכָּל הֲגִלּוּיָם כֻּלָּם. 

**that transcends all revelation.**

כִּי הוּא אֲשֶׁר נִתְגַּלָּה בְּהַגִילוּי הָרִאשׁוֹן, וּמִמֶּנּוּ לְהַשֵּׁנִי וּמִמֶּנּוּ לְהַשְׁלִישִׁי וְכֵן כו' כו' כַּנָּ"ל ספ"ד. 

**For He is revealed in the very first revelation, and from there to the second, and then to the third etc. as explained above end of chapter 4,**

אֲבָל בַּאֲמִתָּתוֹ הַנַּעֲלָמָה עַצְמוֹ אֲשֶׁר נִתְגַּלָּה בָּהֶם 

**But** as regards **His true hidden Being itself which is revealed within them** within these levels

הִנֵּה אָסוּר וְנִמְנַע וּמְשּׁוּלָל מִכָּל לֵב לְחַשֵּׁב וּמִכָּל שֶׁכָּל לְהִתְבּוֹנֵן, 

**– it is forbidden and** permission is **withheld and denied to any heart to think about, and all the more so to contemplate,**

כִּי רַק הוּא לְבַדּוֹ ית"ש יוֹדֵעַ אֶת עַצְמוֹ וּמַכִּיר אֶת אֲמִתָּתוֹ יִתְבָּרֵךְ וְיִתְעַלֶּה שְׁמוֹ לָעַד:

**for He alone knows Himself and is aware of His true being, may His Name be blessed and exalted forever.**
